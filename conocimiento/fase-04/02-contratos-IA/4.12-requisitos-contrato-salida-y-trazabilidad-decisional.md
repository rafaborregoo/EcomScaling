# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P12 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para salida y trazabilidad decisional

> Define requisitos de información. No define schemas técnicos implementados.

---

## 1. Decision Identity

Debe poder identificarse:

- decision;
- candidate selected;
- decision type;
- priority rank;
- portfolio state;
- decision timestamp / as-of context;
- validity window.

---

## 2. Decision Context

Debe poder viajar con la decisión:

- objective;
- stage;
- constraints;
- resources;
- horizon;
- business preferences;
- relevant environment.

---

## 3. Rationale

Debe poder comunicar:

- why this;
- why now;
- why before alternatives;
- value at stake;
- mechanism;
- opportunity cost.

---

## 4. Relative Rationale

Debe poder identificar:

- main runner-up;
- why not B;
- trade-off;
- what is sacrificed;
- whether both can coexist.

---

## 5. Decision Type

Debe poder distinguir conceptualmente:

- ACT;
- VALIDATE;
- PREPARE;
- SEQUENCE;
- MONITOR;
- DEFER;
- STOP.

---

## 6. Evidence

Debe poder asociarse cada claim material con:

- evidence source;
- evidence type;
- provenance;
- freshness;
- relevance;
- strength;
- conflict status.

---

## 7. Evidence Conflict

Debe poder representarse:

- conflicting sources;
- unresolved discrepancy;
- reconciliation needed;
- decision impact.

---

## 8. Epistemic Classification

Cada item relevante debe poder distinguirse como:

- fact;
- inference;
- assumption;
- preference;
- prediction;
- recommendation;
- unknown.

---

## 9. Critical Assumptions

Debe poder comunicar:

- assumption;
- why critical;
- what depends on it;
- observable evidence;
- invalidation condition.

---

## 10. Uncertainty

Debe poder representar:

- known unknown;
- missing data;
- unresolved causal uncertainty;
- materiality to decision.

---

## 11. Decision Confidence

Debe poder expresar:

- confidence level;
- reasons;
- evidence strengths;
- remaining uncertainty;
- ranking sensitivity.

No se prescribe porcentaje.

---

## 12. Diagnostic vs Decision Confidence

Deben ser representables por separado cuando aplique.

---

## 13. Risk

Debe poder comunicar:

- main downside;
- reversibility;
- risk capacity implications;
- failure mode.

---

## 14. Constraint / Dependency

Debe poder expresar:

- current constraint;
- dependency role;
- sequence implications;
- ultimate vs immediate priority.

---

## 15. Resource / Portfolio Effect

Debe poder comunicar:

- scarce resource consumed;
- resource conflict;
- parallel work;
- opportunity cost;
- displaced work.

---

## 16. Counterfactual

Debe poder representar:

- best alternative;
- do nothing;
- wait;
- validate;
- expected outcome under alternative.

---

## 17. Rank-Switch Condition

Debe poder comunicar:

- what new evidence changes rank;
- trigger;
- affected candidate;
- expected change.

---

## 18. Validity Window

Debe poder representar:

- decision half-life concept;
- expected duration;
- expiration condition.

---

## 19. Review Trigger

Debe poder representar:

- event-driven trigger;
- time-driven trigger;
- material threshold concept;
- next review reason.

---

## 20. Success Criteria

Debe poder comunicar:

- expected business outcome;
- leading indicators;
- lagging indicators;
- guardrails;
- failure criteria.

---

## 21. Decision Thesis

Debe poder representar:

> A should be priority because X will produce Y under conditions Z.

---

## 22. Original Expected Mechanism

Debe preservarse para ex-post validation.

---

## 23. Actual Intervention

Debe poder distinguirse de original decision:

- actual action;
- scope change;
- partial execution;
- intervention drift.

---

## 24. Actual Outcome

Debe poder registrar:

- observed outcome;
- benefit timing;
- primary result;
- guardrail effects.

---

## 25. Expected vs Actual

Debe poder representar:

- expected value;
- realized value;
- variance;
- realization gap.

Sin exigir monetización si no es fiable.

---

## 26. Error Classification

Debe poder distinguir:

- data error;
- diagnosis error;
- priority error;
- portfolio error;
- intervention/execution error;
- model error;
- assumption error;
- timing error;
- capacity error;
- external change;
- measurement error;
- randomness.

---

## 27. Causal Confidence

Debe poder representarse aparte de observational confidence cuando sea relevante.

---

## 28. Validation Method

Debe poder comunicar método usado:

- controlled experiment;
- cohort comparison;
- time series;
- operational evidence;
- financial reconciliation;
- scenario comparison;
- other suitable method.

---

## 29. Incrementality

Debe poder representar si el benefit observado es:

- incremental;
- partially attributable;
- uncertain;
- non-causal.

---

## 30. Learning Statement

Debe poder expresar:

- what was right;
- what was wrong;
- what changed externally;
- what should be believed differently now;
- what is reusable;
- what is context-specific.

---

## 31. Decision Memory

Debe poder conectar:

- what was decided;
- why;
- what happened;
- what was learned.

---

## 32. Audience View

La misma decisión puede ofrecer:

- executive summary;
- operator view;
- analyst view.

La lógica base debe permanecer consistente.

---

## 33. Challengeability

Debe poder responder:

- why A?;
- why not B?;
- what assumption matters?;
- what if evidence changes?;
- what risk is accepted?

---

## 34. Contestability

Debe poder incorporar:

- user-provided new evidence;
- explicit preference change;
- explicit objective change.

Y distinguirlos.

---

## 35. Auditability

Debe poder reconstruirse:

`business context → evidence → interpretation → trade-off → priority → review → learning`

---

## 36. Minimum Decision Output

Una salida P12 debería poder contener:

1. decision;
2. decision type;
3. why this;
4. why now;
5. why before alternatives;
6. evidence;
7. facts vs assumptions;
8. uncertainty;
9. confidence;
10. main risk;
11. opportunity cost;
12. rank-switch condition;
13. validity/review trigger;
14. validation thesis.
