# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P10 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para ranking de prioridad

> Este documento define requisitos conceptuales de información. No define un schema implementado.

---

## 1. Decision Context

Debe poder intercambiarse:

- current business objective;
- decision scope;
- resource scope;
- priority horizon;
- business preferences;
- constraints relevant to ranking.

---

## 2. Candidate Identity

Cada candidato debe poder identificarse con:

- unique conceptual identity;
- candidate type;
- business outcome connection;
- source issue/opportunity/risk;
- root-cause relationship cuando aplique.

---

## 3. Eligibility Status

Debe poder representarse si el candidato está:

- eligible now;
- blocked;
- conditional;
- validation required;
- not currently feasible.

Sin imponer enum técnico definitivo.

---

## 4. Hard Gates

El contrato debe poder comunicar:

- legality constraint;
- technical impossibility;
- resource impossibility;
- hard dependency;
- hard deadline;
- survival constraint;
- minimum evidence requirement.

---

## 5. Special Conditions

Debe poder indicarse si existe:

- existential risk;
- active critical incident;
- irreversible closing window;
- critical unlock;
- current-constraint protection;
- critical validation need.

---

## 6. Dimensional Profile

Debe poder representar por separado:

- objective alignment;
- urgency;
- time to damage;
- value at stake;
- constraint relevance;
- dependency position;
- expected value;
- resource requirements;
- feasibility;
- risk;
- reversibility;
- opportunity cost;
- stage fit;
- strategic fit;
- confidence.

No debe obligarse a colapsar estas dimensiones en un score.

---

## 7. Importance vs Executability

Debe poder comunicar independientemente:

- business importance;
- execution status.

---

## 8. Dependency Information

Debe poder representar:

- prerequisite relationships;
- hard vs soft dependency;
- minimum prerequisite;
- unlock relationship;
- execution precedence.

---

## 9. Resource Conflict

Debe poder comunicar:

- resources required;
- shared scarce resource;
- resource independence;
- parallelism;
- bottleneck resource.

---

## 10. Constraint Role

Debe poder indicar si el candidato:

- releases;
- protects;
- worsens;
- is neutral to;

the current constraint.

---

## 11. Relative Comparison

El contrato debe permitir explicar comparaciones A vs B:

- why A before B;
- where B is stronger;
- trade-off;
- preference basis.

---

## 12. Dominance

Debe poder representarse:

- clearly dominated;
- non-dominated;
- robust dominance;
- scenario-dependent dominance.

---

## 13. Priority Tier

Puede comunicarse una categoría como:

- critical;
- high;
- medium;
- low;

si existe una definición consistente.

No se prescribe escala definitiva.

---

## 14. Priority Class

Debe poder distinguirse conceptualmente:

- incident;
- structural;
- unlock;
- opportunity;
- validation;
- monitor.

---

## 15. Priority State

Debe poder comunicarse un tratamiento como:

- act now;
- validate now;
- prepare;
- sequence after X;
- run in parallel;
- monitor;
- defer;
- drop.

No implica el playbook de solución.

---

## 16. Rank

Debe poder representarse:

- ordinal rank;
- tie / near-tie;
- priority distance cuando pueda justificarse;
- rank cluster.

---

## 17. Ranking Confidence

Debe distinguirse de candidate confidence.

Debe poder expresar:

- high;
- medium;
- low;
- insufficient evidence.

---

## 18. Sensitivity

Debe poder comunicar:

- key assumption;
- rank-switch variable;
- scenario dependence;
- robustness;
- weight sensitivity cuando exista weighting.

---

## 19. Rank-Switch Conditions

Toda prioridad frágil debería poder comunicar:

- what changes rank;
- trigger;
- threshold or condition;
- expected successor candidate.

---

## 20. Re-Ranking Trigger

Debe poder indicarse qué input material justificaría reevaluación:

- evidence;
- constraint;
- capacity;
- deadline;
- risk;
- objective;
- opportunity.

---

## 21. Facts, Inferences, Preferences

Debe poder mantenerse distinción conceptual entre:

- facts;
- inferences;
- business preferences.

---

## 22. Evidence

Debe poder asociarse:

- source;
- quality;
- uncertainty;
- absolute data where available;
- category summary where useful.

---

## 23. Ranking Rationale

Una prioridad defendible debe poder explicar:

- why this;
- why now;
- why before the others;
- value at stake;
- main uncertainty;
- resource implication;
- risk;
- key dependency.

---

## 24. Counter-Ranking

Debe poder responder:

> qué tendría que ser cierto para que B pasara por delante de A.

---

## 25. Objective Dependency

Debe poder registrarse que el ranking está condicionado por el objective actual.

Si cambia el objective, debe poder reevaluarse.

---

## 26. Stakeholder Conflict

Debe poder representar conflicto entre preferences sin esconderlo en weighting.

---

## 27. Abstention

Debe poder devolver:

- cannot reliably order;
- practical tie;
- more evidence required;
- validation priority.

---

## 28. Auditability

Debe existir capacidad conceptual para reconstruir:

- context;
- candidates;
- evidence;
- assumptions;
- gates;
- comparisons;
- decisions;
- explanation.

---

## 29. Resultado mínimo

Una salida P10 completa debe poder comunicar:

1. rank / tier;
2. candidate;
3. priority type;
4. state;
5. rationale;
6. value at stake;
7. why now;
8. constraint/dependency role;
9. key resource;
10. main risk;
11. confidence;
12. rank-switch condition;
13. what can run in parallel;
14. what is blocked;
15. what should be monitored.
