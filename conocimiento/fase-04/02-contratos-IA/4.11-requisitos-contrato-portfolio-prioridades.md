# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P11 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para portfolio de prioridades

> Define requisitos de información. No define schemas implementados.

---

## 1. Portfolio Context

Debe poder comunicarse:

- active business objective;
- active business priorities;
- current portfolio;
- current stage;
- current resource capacity;
- run-the-business load;
- change-the-business capacity.

---

## 2. Initiative Identity

Cada iniciativa debe poder representarse con:

- identity;
- parent business priority;
- portfolio role;
- priority rank;
- portfolio state;
- owner/resource pool;
- expected outcome.

---

## 3. Portfolio Role

Debe poder distinguirse conceptualmente:

- protective;
- recovery;
- growth;
- structural;
- experimental;
- resilience;
- maintenance;
- mandatory.

---

## 4. Portfolio State

Debe poder representar:

- active;
- queued;
- blocked;
- paused;
- monitoring;
- stopped;
- completed.

---

## 5. Resource Requirements

Debe poder comunicar por iniciativa:

- resource types;
- scarce resources;
- timing;
- minimum effective allocation;
- shared resource dependencies;
- reserved resources.

---

## 6. Capacity

Debe poder representar:

- nominal capacity;
- effective capacity;
- run load;
- change capacity;
- buffer;
- seasonality;
- resource arrival/departure.

---

## 7. Resource Conflict

Debe poder comunicar:

- shared resource;
- resource pool;
- overlap intensity;
- whether simultaneous execution is feasible;
- bottleneck impact.

---

## 8. Dependency Graph Requirements

Debe poder representar:

- hard dependency;
- soft dependency;
- prerequisite;
- unlock;
- critical path;
- slack.

---

## 9. Interaction Graph Requirements

Debe poder representar cuando sea material:

- synergy;
- complementarity;
- cannibalization;
- duplication;
- mutual exclusivity;
- risk correlation;
- measurement interference.

---

## 10. Parallelism

Debe poder comunicarse:

- true parallel;
- conditional parallel;
- not parallel;
- hidden shared resource;
- coordination burden.

---

## 11. Sequencing

Debe poder expresar:

- required sequence;
- preferred sequence;
- reason for sequence;
- cost of delay;
- risk reduction;
- learning value;
- resource availability.

---

## 12. WIP

Debe poder representar:

- active WIP;
- resource-specific WIP;
- queue;
- cycle time;
- lead time;
- congestion;
- carrying cost.

No prescribe límites numéricos universales.

---

## 13. Admission

Debe poder comunicar:

- candidate ready?;
- resource available?;
- displacement required?;
- displaced initiative;
- marginal alternative;
- active vs backlog decision.

---

## 14. Exit

Debe poder representar:

- continue;
- reduce;
- pause;
- stop;
- complete;
- exit reason;
- capacity released.

---

## 15. Kill / Continue / Scale / Pause Criteria

Debe poder asociarse a una iniciativa:

- continuation evidence;
- stop condition;
- pause condition;
- scale condition;
- reactivation condition.

---

## 16. Preemption

Debe poder comunicar:

- preempting event;
- affected work;
- resource switched;
- switching cost;
- restart expectation;
- resumption condition.

---

## 17. Portfolio Risk

Debe poder representar:

- individual risks;
- shared assumptions;
- correlated risks;
- shared downside;
- aggregate exposure;
- total cash exposure;
- critical-system overlap.

---

## 18. Transformation Density

Debe poder comunicar:

- number of simultaneous major changes;
- affected critical systems;
- change-surface overlap;
- organizational load.

---

## 19. Portfolio Balance

Debe poder describir mix por:

- horizon;
- role;
- risk;
- protect/grow;
- core/adjacency;
- run/change;
- exploit/explore.

Sin imponer porcentajes universales.

---

## 20. Portfolio Liquidity

Debe poder representar:

- reversible commitments;
- locked resources;
- reassignment friction;
- contractual lock-in;
- inventory lock-up;
- long transformations.

---

## 21. Portfolio Robustness

Debe poder comunicar comportamiento bajo:

- base;
- downside;
- upside;
- cash shock;
- demand shock;
- resource loss;
- incident.

---

## 22. Shared Assumptions

Debe poder representar assumptions que afectan varias iniciativas.

---

## 23. Rebalancing Trigger

Debe poder comunicar triggers como:

- objective change;
- incident;
- constraint shift;
- capital shock;
- market change;
- resource change;
- new evidence.

---

## 24. Portfolio Review Decision

Debe poder producir:

- continue;
- accelerate;
- reduce;
- pause;
- stop;
- resequence;
- replace;
- validate.

---

## 25. Value Realization

Debe poder asociar:

- original business case;
- expected outcome;
- completion;
- benefit lag;
- realized value;
- remaining value.

---

## 26. Priority Cascade

Debe poder conservar relación:

- objective;
- business priority;
- portfolio initiative;
- downstream task.

No confundir project count con priority count.

---

## 27. Facts / Preferences / Constraints

Debe poder distinguir:

- business facts;
- strategic preferences;
- hard constraints;
- governance requirements.

---

## 28. Portfolio Auditability

Debe poder reconstruirse:

- why initiative entered;
- what it displaced;
- why it remained;
- why it paused/stopped;
- what resource it consumed;
- what risk it added;
- what assumption supported it.

---

## 29. Resultado mínimo

Una salida P11 útil debe poder comunicar:

1. active portfolio;
2. queued portfolio;
3. blocked/paused/monitoring work;
4. resource map;
5. dependency graph;
6. conflicts;
7. true parallelism;
8. sequence;
9. WIP load;
10. aggregate risk;
11. admission/displacement decisions;
12. stop decisions;
13. rebalance triggers;
14. capacity buffer;
15. next-ready work.
