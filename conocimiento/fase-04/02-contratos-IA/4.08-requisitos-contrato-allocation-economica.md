# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P8 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para allocation económica

> Este documento define requisitos conceptuales de información. No define un schema implementado.

---

## 1. Objeto del intercambio

P8 necesita representar información suficiente para comparar alternativas de uso de recursos escasos.

No debe reducirse a un único ROI o score.

---

## 2. Candidate identity

Debe poder identificarse:

- candidato;
- alcance;
- resource envelope;
- horizon;
- expected effect;
- alternativas comparables.

---

## 3. Value profile

Debe poder comunicarse:

- value to create;
- value to protect;
- value to recover;
- value to unlock;
- direct value;
- enabling value;
- recurring / one-off value;
- persistence.

---

## 4. Incrementality

Debe poder diferenciarse:

- gross value;
- incremental value;
- attributed value;
- baseline;
- counterfactual;
- cannibalization;
- value displaced.

---

## 5. Expected Value

Debe poder representar:

- outcomes relevantes;
- valor de cada outcome;
- probability o plausibility;
- uncertainty;
- evidence supporting probabilities.

Si no existe evidencia suficiente, no debe obligarse a probabilidades numéricas.

---

## 6. Capturable Value

Debe poder comunicarse:

- theoretical value;
- capturable value;
- constraints que limitan captura;
- capacity ceiling;
- dependency requirements.

---

## 7. Risk visibility

Debe poder acompañarse EV de:

- downside profile;
- cash exposure;
- reversibility;
- tail risk;
- risk constraints.

P8 no debe ocultar P7 mediante una cifra ajustada arbitrariamente.

---

## 8. Resource profile

Debe poder indicar qué recursos consume el candidato:

- cash;
- engineering;
- creative;
- management attention;
- inventory;
- operations;
- technology;
- otros recursos escasos.

La lista no constituye un enum obligatorio.

---

## 9. Opportunity cost

Para cada recurso relevante debe poder comunicarse:

- best alternative use;
- value sacrificed;
- whether A actually displaces B;
- whether options can coexist;
- resource-specific opportunity cost.

---

## 10. Marginal economics

Debe poder representarse:

- current allocation;
- next unit of resource;
- marginal value;
- marginal cost;
- saturation;
- point where returns deteriorate.

---

## 11. Capacity to deploy

Debe poder indicar cuánto recurso puede absorber una oportunidad antes de que su economics cambie materialmente.

---

## 12. Resource conflict

Debe poder comunicarse si elegir A:

- prevents B;
- delays B;
- reduces B;
- has no material effect on B.

No debe inventarse opportunity cost sin conflicto real.

---

## 13. Cost of delay

Debe poder expresarse:

- value lost while waiting;
- delay sensitivity;
- timing window;
- sequence effect.

---

## 14. Value of Information

Debe poder representarse:

- uncertainty being reduced;
- whether information can change decision;
- information cost;
- delay cost;
- expected decision improvement;
- information durability.

---

## 15. Optionality

Debe poder comunicarse:

- option to delay;
- expand;
- contract;
- abandon;
- switch;
- options closed by commitment;
- cost of preserving flexibility.

---

## 16. Learning Value

Debe poder distinguirse:

- direct test value;
- information gain;
- reusable learning;
- learning externality;
- learning decay.

---

## 17. Exploration vs exploitation

Debe poder representarse si una asignación persigue principalmente:

- explotación;
- exploración;
- aprendizaje reusable;
- compounding.

No se define cuota o budget obligatorio.

---

## 18. Cash and timing

Debe poder comunicarse:

- upfront cash;
- cash timing;
- payback;
- capital lock-up;
- capital recycling;
- liquidity constraints.

---

## 19. Working capital

Para inventario puede ser relevante:

- margin;
- contribution;
- turns;
- cycle;
- working-capital consumption;
- overstock / stockout implications.

---

## 20. Enabling Value

Debe poder indicarse:

- qué downstream values dependen de un enabler;
- strength of dependency;
- unlock probability;
- whether enabler is necessary or sufficient.

Debe evitarse double counting.

---

## 21. Initiative interactions

Debe poder expresarse si las alternativas son:

- complementary;
- cannibalizing;
- mutually exclusive;
- compatible;
- conditional;
- sequential.

---

## 22. Dynamic allocation

Debe poder comunicar:

- reallocation trigger;
- switching point;
- threshold condition;
- current winner;
- conditions under which another option dominates.

---

## 23. Reallocation costs

Debe poder representarse:

- transition cost;
- context switching;
- contractual cost;
- lost momentum.

---

## 24. Sunk vs remaining

Debe distinguirse:

- sunk investment;
- remaining investment;
- remaining expected value;
- released resources if stopped.

---

## 25. Stop / continue

Debe poder comunicarse conceptualmente:

- continue;
- stop;
- reduce;
- reallocate;
- validate;
- stage.

No se prescribe enum técnico.

---

## 26. Strategic constraints

Debe poder incorporarse contexto sobre:

- objectives;
- brand;
- business model;
- strategic fit.

P8 no debe convertir valor económico aislado en prioridad automática.

---

## 27. Decision confidence

Debe poder comunicar:

- evidence quality;
- confidence;
- decision stability;
- sensitivity.

---

## 28. Resultado mínimo

Una evaluación P8 útil debe poder transmitir:

1. valor incremental;
2. counterfactual;
3. outcomes plausibles;
4. uncertainty;
5. capturable value;
6. recursos requeridos;
7. recurso escaso;
8. best alternative forgone;
9. marginal return;
10. cost of delay;
11. VOI;
12. optionality;
13. payback / cash timing;
14. interactions;
15. switching conditions;
16. confidence.
