# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P9 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para Strategic Fit

> Este documento extrae requisitos de información. No define schemas implementados.

---

## 1. Objeto del intercambio

P9 necesita representar si una iniciativa encaja con:

- objective;
- core;
- capabilities;
- customer;
- market;
- business model;
- maturity;
- complexity;
- future state.

No debe reducirse a un único `fit_score`.

---

## 2. Candidate context

Debe poder identificarse:

- iniciativa;
- alcance;
- hipótesis nuevas;
- dimensiones que modifica;
- target state esperado.

---

## 3. Objective Fit

Debe poder comunicarse:

- current business objective;
- relation between candidate and objective;
- direct / indirect alignment;
- potential distraction;
- competing strategic priorities.

---

## 4. Core Fit

Debe poder representarse:

- current economic core;
- core customer;
- core products;
- core channels;
- core capabilities;
- health of the core;
- remaining upside;
- signs of saturation.

---

## 5. Adjacency

Debe poder comunicarse:

- adjacency type;
- customer overlap;
- product overlap;
- channel overlap;
- geographic relation;
- capability reuse;
- value-chain relation.

---

## 6. Right to Win

Debe poder describirse:

- transferable advantages;
- unique access;
- distribution;
- brand;
- data;
- supply;
- technology;
- operational know-how;
- other sources of advantage.

No se prescribe un enum.

---

## 7. Capability Fit

Debe poder distinguir:

- existing capability;
- adjacent capability;
- missing critical capability;
- capability stretch;
- capability leap;
- capability building required.

---

## 8. Strategic Distance

Debe poder expresar cuántas dimensiones nuevas introduce una iniciativa:

- customer;
- product;
- channel;
- market;
- operations;
- supply chain;
- business model.

---

## 9. Stage Fit

Debe poder comunicarse:

- current maturity;
- current scale;
- current complexity;
- next target state;
- capability gap;
- stage transition requirements.

---

## 10. Readiness to Scale

Cuando aplique, debe poder reflejar:

- economic readiness;
- supply readiness;
- operational readiness;
- financial readiness;
- organizational readiness;
- data readiness;
- technical readiness.

---

## 11. Strategic Coherence

Debe poder expresar:

- whether initiatives reinforce common value engine;
- whether they fragment resources;
- whether they compound learning;
- whether they create competing fronts.

---

## 12. Portfolio Role

Debe poder indicar, conceptualmente, si una iniciativa busca:

- defend core;
- expand core;
- build capability;
- test adjacency;
- create new engine.

No se define un enum obligatorio.

---

## 13. Capability Architecture

Debe poder diferenciar:

- must-have capability;
- differentiating capability;
- commodity capability;
- capability deserving excellence;
- capability where adequacy is enough.

---

## 14. Defensibility

Debe poder representarse:

- durable advantage;
- owned vs rented capability;
- proprietary learning;
- customer relationship;
- supply advantage;
- dependency reduction.

---

## 15. Business-Model Fit

Debe poder comunicar si la iniciativa altera:

- revenue model;
- customer relationship;
- operations;
- capital structure;
- channel economics.

---

## 16. Complexity Fit

Debe poder reflejar:

- permanent complexity introduced;
- systems touched;
- teams affected;
- maintenance burden;
- interaction complexity.

---

## 17. Focus

Debe poder comunicarse:

- active major initiatives;
- strategic theme;
- what would receive less attention;
- what is deliberately not pursued.

---

## 18. Maturity Gap

Debe poder expresar qué capability faltante limita el next target state.

No debe asumirse que lowest maturity = highest priority.

---

## 19. Future-State Fit

Debe poder describir el negocio que existiría si la iniciativa tuviera éxito:

- economics;
- dependencies;
- capabilities;
- complexity;
- customer;
- positioning;
- control.

---

## 20. Brand / Customer Fit

Debe poder representar:

- brand transferability;
- customer permission;
- customer overlap;
- pricing coherence;
- channel conflict;
- positioning effects.

---

## 21. Strategic Optionality

Debe poder comunicar:

- plausible future paths opened;
- paths closed;
- capabilities built;
- dependencies created.

---

## 22. Tactical Exception

Debe poder diferenciar:

- temporary tactical use;
- permanent strategic identity.

---

## 23. Fit over time

Debe poder representar que:

- fit can improve;
- fit can deteriorate;
- not-now opportunities remain valid.

---

## 24. Evidence and confidence

Debe poder acompañarse cada conclusión de:

- evidence;
- assumptions;
- uncertainty;
- confidence.

---

## 25. Resultado mínimo

Una evaluación P9 útil debe poder transmitir:

1. objective fit;
2. core fit;
3. adjacency logic;
4. right to win;
5. capability fit;
6. strategic distance;
7. stage fit;
8. readiness;
9. strategic coherence;
10. complexity fit;
11. defensibility;
12. future-state fit;
13. major misfit dimensions;
14. whether the issue is “not now” or fundamentally weak;
15. confidence.
