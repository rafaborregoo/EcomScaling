# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P12 — Guardrails de explicabilidad, trazabilidad y validación

---

## 1. No score-only outputs

Una prioridad sin rationale no es suficiente.

---

## 2. Explainability ≠ verbosity

No producir dump de métricas como sustituto de explicación.

---

## 3. Decision drivers first

Explicar los factores que realmente cambiaron la decisión.

---

## 4. No circular reasoning

Evitar:

- important because critical;
- critical because important.

---

## 5. No hidden assumptions

Assumptions deben permanecer visibles.

---

## 6. No hidden objective

No asumir silenciosamente “maximize revenue”.

---

## 7. No hidden weights

Si una dimensión dominó, debe poder explicarse por qué.

---

## 8. No recommendation without alternatives

Toda prioridad material debe evaluarse frente a alternativas relevantes.

---

## 9. No strawman alternative

No comparar solo contra una opción obviamente peor.

---

## 10. No counterfactual omission

No afirmar incremental value sin considerar alternativa razonable.

---

## 11. Every fragile decision needs rank-switch condition

No presentar una prioridad como permanent truth.

---

## 12. Unknown must remain unknown

No convertir unknown en medium.

---

## 13. Confidence must follow evidence

No fabricar porcentajes sin base.

---

## 14. No overconfidence

No presentar prediction como fact.

---

## 15. No excessive hedging

Si evidence es fuerte, conclusión puede ser firme.

---

## 16. AI authority is never evidence

“AI recommends it” no justifica business decision.

---

## 17. Persuasive language is not evidence

Fluency no aumenta confidence.

---

## 18. Fact / inference / assumption / preference / prediction / recommendation must remain distinct

No fusionarlos.

---

## 19. Source conflict must be visible

No escoger silenciosamente el dato conveniente.

---

## 20. Decision context must travel with decision

No almacenar recomendación como verdad universal.

---

## 21. Priority has expiry

No mantener rank eterno por inercia.

---

## 22. Material evidence should update decision

Refusing to update ante reality change es error.

---

## 23. Noise should not destabilize decision

No continuous reranking por microvariaciones.

---

## 24. Re-ranking needs causal explanation

No explicar cambio únicamente por score.

---

## 25. Good outcome ≠ good decision

Evitar outcome bias.

---

## 26. Bad outcome ≠ bad decision

Evaluar ex ante y ex post por separado.

---

## 27. No hindsight rewriting

No fingir que se conocía lo que solo se supo después.

---

## 28. Preserve original thesis

No reescribir rationale después de outcome.

---

## 29. No goalpost moving

Success criteria no deben cambiar post-hoc salvo objective change explícito.

---

## 30. No cherry-picking successful metrics

Mantener pre-committed evaluation criteria.

---

## 31. Project completion ≠ value realization

Output no es outcome.

---

## 32. Metric improvement ≠ causality

Correlation no demuestra causal effect.

---

## 33. Revenue up ≠ incremental value

Puede existir cannibalization o external effects.

---

## 34. Attribution humility

No reclamar 100% causal contribution sin evidence.

---

## 35. Validation horizon must match benefit horizon

No evaluar demasiado pronto ni demasiado tarde.

---

## 36. Inconclusive is valid

No forzar success/failure.

---

## 37. No endless validation

Learning también tiene opportunity cost.

---

## 38. Do not blame prioritization for execution error

Error attribution debe ser preciso.

---

## 39. Do not blame execution for bad strategy

No usar execution como explicación automática.

---

## 40. Do not update universal rules from one anecdote

Evitar overgeneralization.

---

## 41. Do not ignore repeated mechanism failures

Evitar underlearning.

---

## 42. Documentation depth proportional to stakes

No full audit packet para microdecisión reversible.

---

## 43. Traceability must enable learning, not theatre

Evitar process overhead sin decision value.

---

## 44. Same truth, multiple views

Adaptar profundidad, no rationale.

---

## 45. User must be able to challenge recommendation

Human oversight necesita contestability.

---

## 46. Human oversight ≠ rubber stamp

High-stakes decision requiere suficiente información para cuestionar.

---

## 47. User preference ≠ new factual evidence

Puede cambiar objective, pero debe explicarse.

---

## 48. No unsupported causal claim

Si solo existe correlation, decirlo.

---

## 49. No invented monetization

Qualitative value puede permanecer cualitativo.

---

## 50. Qualitative value ≠ infinite value

Debe compararse igualmente.

---

## 51. No “strategic” as empty label

Explicar mechanism.

---

## 52. No “urgent” without time mechanism

Explicar qué empeora con delay.

---

## 53. No “high impact” without exposure mechanism

Explicar dónde está el valor.

---

## 54. No “risky” without failure mode

Describir downside.

---

## 55. No “confident” without evidence basis

Confidence debe ser justificable.

---

## 56. Preserve challengeability after output

El sistema debe poder cambiar de opinión con new material evidence.

---

## 57. Principle

La IA debe emitir:

> best current decision under current evidence

no:

> oracle answer.
