# Fase 04 · P01 — Tenant context de decisión y objetivos

## Propósito

P01 es uno de los bloques más fuertemente orientados a `06-tenant-context`.

Aquí deben vivir los valores concretos de cada ecommerce que hacen significativa la priorización.

Este documento define categorías; no inventa valores.

---

## 1. Business Stage

Contexto específico:
- current stage/maturity;
- maturity confidence;
- capability readiness.

Proviene principalmente de F3/D3.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

Debe existir, cuando sea conocido:

- objective;
- rationale;
- target/outcome;
- horizon;
- urgency;
- confidence.

Ejemplos conceptuales:
- survival;
- cash preservation;
- profitability;
- profitable growth;
- retention;
- expansion;
- stability;
- scalability;
- learning.

---

## 3. Secondary Objectives

Lista de objetivos secundarios, con:
- relationship to primary;
- horizon;
- conflicts;
- precedence.

---

## 4. Declared Objective

Lo que el business declara querer.

---

## 5. Revealed Objective

Lo que budget, commitments y behavior sugieren que el negocio está protegiendo realmente.

Debe conservarse como contextual inference, no como truth automática.

---

## 6. Objective Conflicts

Puede incluir:
- growth vs cash;
- profitability vs market share;
- customer experience vs cost;
- short vs long term;
- explore vs exploit.

---

## 7. Time Horizon

Debe incluir ventanas relevantes:
- 24h;
- 7d;
- 30d;
- quarter;
- year;
- multi-year;
- event deadline.

---

## 8. Current Constraint

Referencia al constraint activo de F3/D9.

Puede modificar la interpretación del objetivo.

---

## 9. Cash Context

Puede incluir, cuando exista:
- runway;
- liquidity;
- working capital;
- obligations;
- inventory commitments;
- financing.

---

## 10. Unit Economics

Puede incluir:
- contribution;
- margin;
- CAC tolerance;
- payback;
- returns;
- shipping economics;
- other relevant economics.

---

## 11. Available Resources

Puede incluir:
- cash;
- team;
- engineering;
- creative;
- inventory;
- supplier capacity;
- time;
- management attention.

---

## 12. Risk Appetite

Cuánto risk quiere aceptar el tenant.

---

## 13. Risk Capacity

Cuánto downside puede realmente absorber.

Debe permanecer separado de appetite.

---

## 14. Critical Guardrails

Puede incluir:

### Economic
- minimum contribution;
- CAC limits;
- runway;
- inventory exposure;
- payback.

### Operational
- fulfillment capacity;
- support;
- inventory;
- supplier;
- technology.

### Customer
- delivery;
- refunds;
- satisfaction;
- trust.

### Strategic
- brand;
- concentration;
- product quality.

Solo usar thresholds si el tenant realmente los define.

---

## 15. Key Commitments

Puede incluir:
- inventory orders;
- contracts;
- launches;
- retailers;
- market entries;
- migrations;
- supplier obligations.

---

## 16. Commitment Nature

Cuando sea relevante:
- reversible;
- irreversible;
- sunk cost;
- future obligation.

---

## 17. Must Win

Resultados necesarios para alcanzar el objective.

---

## 18. Must Not Fail

Condiciones cuya ruptura invalida el plan.

Ejemplos conceptuales:
- checkout;
- cash runway;
- supplier continuity;
- core inventory;
- compliance.

---

## 19. Major Dependencies

Puede incluir:
- prerequisite;
- blocked capability;
- upstream requirement;
- critical path.

---

## 20. Seasonal / Event Window

Puede incluir:
- Black Friday;
- launch;
- peak season;
- fundraising;
- renegotiation;
- expansion date.

---

## 21. Core Economic Engine

Debe poder reflejar qué elementos generan la mayor parte del valor:
- hero products;
- acquisition channels;
- repeat engine;
- key markets;
- key customer segments.

---

## 22. Revenue Concentration

Cuando exista:
- by SKU/product;
- market;
- channel;
- customer.

---

## 23. Contribution Concentration

Especialmente relevante para exposición económica.

---

## 24. Channel Concentration

Puede identificar dependency risk incluso si el channel está sano.

---

## 25. Supplier Concentration

Puede identificar future risk antes de un incidente.

---

## 26. Customer Concentration

Importante en modelos con wholesale o pocos clientes grandes.

---

## 27. Current State

Debe reflejar reality, no solo aspiration.

Puede incluir:
- revenue;
- economics;
- operational health;
- inventory;
- data;
- maturity.

---

## 28. Target State

El outcome que el business quiere alcanzar.

---

## 29. Gap-to-Objective

Puede incluir:
- gaps;
- binding gaps;
- non-binding gaps.

---

## 30. Objective Confidence

Qué tan claro es el objective actual.

Si falta:
- OBJECTIVE UNCERTAINTY.

---

## 31. Context Freshness

El contexto debe actualizarse cuando cambian:
- financing;
- cash;
- supplier;
- strategy;
- event window;
- maturity;
- constraints;
- resources.

---

## 32. Context Snapshot

Un snapshot útil debería permitir responder:

- BUSINESS STAGE
- PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
- SECONDARY OBJECTIVES
- TIME HORIZON
- CURRENT CONSTRAINT
- CASH CONTEXT
- UNIT ECONOMICS
- RISK CAPACITY
- CRITICAL GUARDRAILS
- AVAILABLE RESOURCES
- KEY COMMITMENTS
- MUST WIN
- MUST NOT FAIL
- MAJOR DEPENDENCIES
- SEASONAL / EVENT WINDOW

---

## 33. Exclusiones

No pertenecen a tenant-context:
- universal objective theory;
- anti-patterns;
- universal guardrail definitions;
- ranking logic;
- priority scores;
- complete history of objective changes.
