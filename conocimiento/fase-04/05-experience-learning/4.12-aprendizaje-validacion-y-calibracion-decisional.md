# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P12 — Experience & Learning sobre validación y calibración decisional

---

## 1. Decision Memory

Conservar conceptualmente:

- decision;
- context;
- rationale;
- evidence;
- assumptions;
- confidence;
- expected outcome;
- actual outcome;
- learning.

---

## 2. Ex-Ante Quality Learning

Evaluar si la decisión era razonable usando solo información disponible en t0.

---

## 3. Ex-Post Outcome Learning

Evaluar:

- what happened;
- realized value;
- unexpected changes;
- interactions.

---

## 4. Decision vs Outcome Calibration

Registrar casos:

- good decision / good outcome;
- good decision / bad outcome;
- bad decision / good outcome;
- bad decision / bad outcome.

---

## 5. Forecast Calibration

Comparar predicted vs actual.

Detectar systematic over/underestimation.

---

## 6. Confidence Calibration

Si high-confidence decisions fall repeatedly, confidence model necesita revisión.

---

## 7. Impact Calibration

Detectar impact inflation o underestimation.

---

## 8. Effort Calibration

Comparar estimated vs actual resource requirement.

---

## 9. Urgency Calibration

Detectar urgency inflation y delay underestimation.

---

## 10. Risk Calibration

Requiere repeated observations.

No inferir model failure por un único event.

---

## 11. Strategic-Fit Calibration

Observar si la iniciativa fortaleció realmente el intended value engine.

---

## 12. Stage-Fit Calibration

Observar si organización absorbió complexity.

---

## 13. Portfolio-Fit Calibration

Observar si concurrent initiatives interfirieron.

---

## 14. Constraint Validation

Después de aliviar un supuesto constraint:

- did throughput improve?;
- what became next constraint?

---

## 15. Opportunity-Cost Learning

Observar qué ocurrió con la alternativa sacrificada.

Quizá podría haber corrido en paralelo.

---

## 16. Reversibility Learning

Comparar assumed vs actual reversal cost.

---

## 17. Realization Gap Learning

Analizar:

`EXPECTED VALUE vs REALIZED VALUE`

y por qué difirieron.

---

## 18. Error Attribution Learning

Clasificar dónde entró error:

- data;
- diagnosis;
- priority;
- portfolio;
- execution;
- model;
- assumption;
- timing;
- measurement.

---

## 19. Decision Drift Learning

Registrar cuando actual intervention cambió respecto de original thesis.

---

## 20. Benefit-Lag Learning

Aprender cuánto tardan distintos benefit types en madurar.

---

## 21. Leading/Lagging Indicator Learning

Observar qué leading indicators realmente anticipan outcomes.

---

## 22. Causal Learning

Registrar cuándo evidence permite:

- causal claim;
- contribution claim;
- correlation only.

---

## 23. Counterfactual Learning

Mejorar estimaciones de what would have happened without intervention.

---

## 24. Rank-Switch Calibration

Comparar trigger predicted vs actual event que cambió prioridad.

---

## 25. Decision Expiry Learning

Observar cuánto tiempo distintas priority classes permanecen válidas.

---

## 26. Review-Cadence Learning

Detectar:

- under-reviewing;
- over-reviewing;
- appropriate cadence by context.

---

## 27. Stability Learning

Distinguir:

- healthy belief updating;
- model flapping;
- stale persistence.

---

## 28. Explanation Learning

Observar qué rationales fueron:

- understandable;
- decision-useful;
- too verbose;
- too vague.

---

## 29. Audience-Layer Learning

Aprender qué depth funciona para:

- executives;
- operators;
- analysts.

Sin alterar underlying truth.

---

## 30. Mechanism-Level Learning

Convertir review en statement del tipo:

> X mattered because mechanism Y operated under context Z.

No almacenar solo “worked/failed”.

---

## 31. Generalizability Classification

Distinguir:

- tenant-specific learning;
- context-specific pattern;
- potentially generalizable mechanism.

---

## 32. Pattern Promotion

Promover a RAG estable solo cuando:

- pattern repeats;
- mechanism is strong;
- context boundaries are known.

---

## 33. Learning Decay

Revisar validez de patrones cuando cambian:

- market;
- technology;
- channel;
- stage;
- business model.

---

## 34. AI Recommendation Review

Para cada material AI recommendation, conservar:

- recommendation;
- evidence;
- assumptions;
- confidence;
- human challenge/override;
- eventual outcome;
- review conclusion.

---

## 35. Wrong-Belief Update

El sistema debe poder registrar:

- we were wrong about X;
- why;
- what belief changes.

---

## 36. Reasonable-Decision/Bad-Outcome Learning

También:

- decision was reasonable;
- external outcome adverse;
- no unnecessary rule update.

---

## 37. F9 Bridge

P12 debe producir experiencia suficientemente estructurada para que F9 pueda:

- aggregate;
- compare;
- calibrate;
- generalize;
- decay;
- promote.

Sin mezclar experiencia con conocimiento estable demasiado pronto.
