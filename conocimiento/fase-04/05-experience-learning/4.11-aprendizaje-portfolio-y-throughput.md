# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P11 — Experience & Learning sobre portfolio y throughput

---

## 1. Concurrency Learning

Registrar cómo cambia throughput cuando aumenta o disminuye:

- active initiatives;
- WIP;
- shared resource load.

---

## 2. WIP Calibration

Observar:

- number of starts;
- number of finishes;
- cycle time;
- lead time;
- queue time.

Esto permite detectar exceso de WIP.

---

## 3. Context-Switching Learning

Registrar dónde el switching realmente consume:

- team time;
- management attention;
- restart effort.

---

## 4. Carrying-Cost Learning

Comparar estimated vs actual carrying cost de iniciativas activas o pausadas.

---

## 5. Minimum Effective Allocation Learning

Aprender qué iniciativas necesitan critical mass para progresar.

---

## 6. Hidden Resource Learning

Detectar shared bottlenecks no visibles inicialmente:

- founder;
- analyst;
- QA;
- legal;
- approval;
- critical data.

---

## 7. True Parallelism Learning

Registrar qué workstreams realmente coexistieron sin deteriorar throughput.

---

## 8. Fake Parallelism Learning

Registrar iniciativas que parecían independientes pero compartían bottlenecks.

---

## 9. Sequence Learning

Comparar sequence expected vs actual:

- dependency;
- delay;
- risk;
- learning;
- constraint.

---

## 10. Critical-Path Learning

Observar qué resources retrasaron outcome principal y cuáles tenían slack.

---

## 11. Portfolio Admission Learning

Para cada entrada:

- what it displaced;
- expected value;
- actual resource use;
- outcome;
- whether admission was justified.

---

## 12. Exit Learning

Para cada stop/pause:

- capacity released;
- value preserved/lost;
- later outcome;
- whether stopping was timely.

---

## 13. Zombie Detection Learning

Registrar signals que anticipan zombie initiatives:

- no owner;
- no progress;
- repeated pauses;
- no clear business case;
- token allocation.

---

## 14. Preemption Learning

Comparar:

- reason;
- interruption cost;
- restart cost;
- benefit achieved.

---

## 15. Firefighting Learning

Detectar loops donde incident work starving structural work causes new incidents.

---

## 16. Structural-Capacity Learning

Observar cuánto capacity necesita reservarse para eliminar causas recurrentes.

---

## 17. Portfolio Risk Learning

Comparar expected vs actual:

- correlated failures;
- transformation density;
- shared assumptions;
- aggregate downside.

---

## 18. Assumption Concentration Learning

Registrar assumptions que dañaron varias iniciativas simultáneamente.

---

## 19. Experiment Collision Learning

Observar cuando concurrent experiments produjeron:

- contamination;
- ambiguous learning;
- analysis backlog.

---

## 20. Exploration/Exploitation Learning

Comparar balance real entre:

- exploit;
- explore;

y su impacto en growth, learning y cash.

---

## 21. Revealed-Strategy Learning

Comparar declared strategy con actual resource allocation.

---

## 22. Resource-Yield Learning

Observar value realized por bottleneck resource.

---

## 23. Queue Learning

Registrar qué shared resource queues generan mayor cost of delay.

---

## 24. Resource Calendar Learning

Comparar capacity planned vs actual por:

- seasonality;
- incidents;
- hires;
- departures;
- project completion.

---

## 25. Capacity-Creation Learning

Para hiring, automation o agencies:

- expected capacity gain;
- actual gain;
- lead time;
- maintenance/coordination cost.

---

## 26. Portfolio Robustness Learning

Observar comportamiento real en:

- cash shocks;
- demand shocks;
- resource losses;
- incidents.

---

## 27. Rebalancing Learning

Registrar:

- trigger;
- old mix;
- new mix;
- switching cost;
- outcome.

---

## 28. Stability Calibration

Aprender si portfolio changes fueron:

- too frequent;
- too slow;
- appropriately responsive.

---

## 29. Value-Realization Learning

Comparar:

- project completion;
- benefit arrival;
- realized outcome;
- lag.

---

## 30. AI Portfolio Recommendation Learning

Conservar:

- active set recommended;
- conflicts;
- sequence;
- parallelism;
- stop decisions;
- buffer rationale;
- later outcomes.

---

## 31. Knowledge Promotion

No convertir un WIP limit, mix o concurrency pattern de un tenant en regla universal.

---

## 32. Decay

Portfolio learnings pueden cambiar cuando cambian:

- team;
- resource pools;
- scale;
- tooling;
- seasonality;
- strategy;
- business model.
