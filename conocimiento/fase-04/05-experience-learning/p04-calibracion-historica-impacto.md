# Fase 04 · P04 — Experience & Learning de impacto

## Propósito

P04 genera un histórico muy valioso para calibrar estimaciones de impacto, recaptura, substitution, incrementality y constraint release.

No define un learning engine automático.

---

## 1. Estimated vs Realized Impact

Puede conservarse:

- estimated range;
- realized impact;
- error;
- bias;
- assumptions.

Esto permite calibrar futuras estimaciones.

---

## 2. Baseline Accuracy

Puede registrarse:
- baseline used;
- actual counterfactual proxy;
- whether baseline over/underestimated impact.

---

## 3. Recapture History

Puede observarse por tenant:
- deferred demand;
- recaptured demand;
- permanent loss;
- recovery horizon.

---

## 4. Substitution Rates

Histórico de:
- SKU substitution;
- channel substitution;
- customer switching;
- contribution dilution.

---

## 5. Cannibalization History

Puede conservar:
- promotion cannibalization;
- channel cannibalization;
- product cannibalization;
- realized incrementality.

---

## 6. Cohort Economics

Histórico tenant-specific de:
- repeat;
- CLV;
- contribution CLV;
- returns;
- payback;
- cohort differences.

---

## 7. Returns / Refund Economics

Puede observar:
- direct refund;
- reverse logistics;
- stock damage;
- support load;
- future value.

---

## 8. Impact Persistence

Registrar:
- temporary;
- persistent;
- decaying;
- compounding impact.

---

## 9. Recurring Impact

Puede conservar:
- per-unit impact;
- frequency;
- duration;
- accumulated value.

---

## 10. Constraint Release Outcomes

Después de liberar un constraint:
- expected unlocked throughput;
- actual throughput;
- next constraint;
- marginal decay.

---

## 11. Marginal Return History

Puede comparar:
- historical average;
- next-unit marginal return;
- saturation point.

---

## 12. Opportunity Capture History

Puede conservar:
- theoretical upside;
- capturable estimate;
- actual captured value;
- capacity limits.

---

## 13. Risk Realization

Puede registrar:
- downside scenario;
- actual outcome;
- tail event;
- resilience performance.

---

## 14. Resilience Value History

Cuando redundancy se activa:
- value protected;
- downtime avoided;
- transactions preserved.

---

## 15. Enabling Value Outcomes

Puede observar si una capability como:
- data;
- automation;
- measurement;

realmente desbloqueó trabajo high-value.

---

## 16. Learning Value Outcomes

Puede medir si un experimento/information gain:
- changed allocation;
- changed strategy;
- prevented bad decision.

---

## 17. Impact Attribution Calibration

Puede comparar:
- attributed impact;
- later evidence;
- actual incremental effect.

---

## 18. Overlap / Double-count Audit History

Puede registrar casos donde:
- same economic loss;
- appeared under multiple diagnoses.

Ayuda a mejorar portfolio-level estimation.

---

## 19. Concentration / Fragility History

Puede observar:
- concentration before failure;
- realized blast radius;
- effectiveness of diversification.

---

## 20. Assumption Sensitivity History

Puede identificar qué assumptions suelen dominar error:
- substitution;
- demand;
- margin;
- duration;
- recapture;
- CLV.

---

## 21. Boundaries

P04 no define:
- automatic impact-model training;
- cross-tenant parameter sharing;
- reinforcement learning;
- autonomous accounting model;
- auto-adjusted scoring weights.

Es memoria de calibración para fases posteriores.
