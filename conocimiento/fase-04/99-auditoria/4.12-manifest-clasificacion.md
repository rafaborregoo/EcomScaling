## 2. Conceptos representados en varias capas

### Explainability

- RAG: principle of understandable rationale.
- Contracts: output fields/concepts required.
- Runtime: explanation-generation sequence.
- Guardrails: no score-only or generic rationale.

### Traceability

- RAG: decision lineage.
- Contracts: provenance and auditability requirements.
- Runtime: evidence trace and preserved original decision.
- Experience: decision memory.

### Confidence

- RAG: decision confidence concept.
- Contracts: reasons and uncertainty.
- Runtime: computed from evidence/sensitivity conceptually.
- Guardrails: no invented precision.
- Experience: calibration over repeated cases.

### Rank-Switch / Review

- RAG: falsifiability.
- Contracts: condition + trigger.
- Runtime: review workflow.
- Guardrails: no permanent truth.
- Experience: calibration of triggers.
- Tenant: actual contextual variable.

### Validation

- RAG: ex ante vs ex post, expected vs actual.
- Contracts: expected/actual/error class.
- Runtime: review and error diagnosis.
- Guardrails: no outcome bias.
- Experience: accumulated learning.

### Human Challengeability

- RAG: contestability.
- Contracts: must support question/update.
- Runtime: new evidence can trigger reevaluation.
- Guardrails: AI authority not sufficient.

## 3. Redundancia consolidada

El fuente desarrolla muchos ejemplos y anti-patterns alrededor de principios repetidos.

Se han consolidado:

- explain drivers, not dumps;
- why this / why now / why before others;
- decision = evidence + assumptions + uncertainty;
- unknown remains unknown;
- good outcome ≠ good decision;
- bad outcome ≠ bad decision;
- project completion ≠ value realization;
- metric improvement ≠ causality;
- material new evidence should update belief;
- noise should not destabilize;
- traceability enables debugging and learning.
