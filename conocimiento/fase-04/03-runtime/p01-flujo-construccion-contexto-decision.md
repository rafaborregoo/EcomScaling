# Fase 04 · P01 — Runtime conceptual de construcción del contexto de decisión

## Estado

P01 no define un runtime técnico implementado.

Sí describe suficientemente el proceso de razonamiento necesario para construir y mantener el contexto decisional.

Este documento traduce ese proceso a una secuencia conceptual sin inventar infraestructura.

---

## 1. Inputs

El proceso puede consumir:

- business reality;
- maturity;
- explicit objectives;
- management behavior;
- capital constraints;
- budgets;
- commitments;
- forecasts;
- active constraints;
- resources;
- risk context;
- guardrails;
- seasonality/events;
- diagnostic context.

---

## 2. Paso 1 — Identificar objetivo explícito

Recoger:

- stated goal;
- target;
- why;
- by when;
- acceptable cost;
- stated constraints.

---

## 3. Paso 2 — Separar conceptos

Clasificar la expresión como:

- goal;
- metric/target;
- strategy;
- initiative;
- preference.

No aceptar automáticamente una iniciativa como objetivo.

---

## 4. Paso 3 — Detectar revealed objective

Observar:

- budgets;
- hiring;
- inventory;
- spend;
- management decisions;
- commitments;
- cash protection behavior.

Comparar:

`DECLARED OBJECTIVE` vs `REVEALED OBJECTIVE`.

---

## 5. Paso 4 — Detectar conflictos

Buscar:

- incompatible goals;
- incompatible resource assumptions;
- risk contradictions;
- margin contradictions;
- timeline conflicts.

No prometer objetivos incompatibles simultáneamente.

---

## 6. Paso 5 — Establecer jerarquía

Distinguir:

- primary;
- secondary;
- must win;
- must not fail;
- guardrails.

---

## 7. Paso 6 — Evaluar survival/stability layer

Antes de objetivos ordinarios comprobar:

- continuity;
- cash;
- transaction integrity;
- critical supplier/inventory;
- legal/compliance;
- platform availability.

---

## 8. Paso 7 — Evaluar maturity fit

Preguntar:

- Is the objective appropriate for current maturity?
- Is the organization ready?
- Is it premature?
- Is current operating model holding back a mature business?

---

## 9. Paso 8 — Evaluar feasibility

Contrastar objective con:

- cash;
- inventory;
- team;
- supplier capacity;
- engineering;
- creative;
- time;
- management attention.

---

## 10. Paso 9 — Identificar current constraint

Consumir F3/D9.

Reformular el objetivo alrededor del binding constraint.

Ejemplo:

Growth + Inventory Constraint
→
profitable throughput under supply constraint.

---

## 11. Paso 10 — Identificar guardrails

Separar:

- economic;
- operational;
- customer;
- strategic;
- continuity.

No confundir guardrail con constraint.

---

## 12. Paso 11 — Evaluar risk appetite/capacity

No usar appetite como sustituto de capacity.

Si la empresa quiere asumir más riesgo del que puede absorber:
- registrar conflicto.

---

## 13. Paso 12 — Incorporar commitments

Clasificar:

- reversible commitment;
- irreversible commitment;
- sunk cost;
- future obligation.

No dar prioridad automática por sunk cost.

---

## 14. Paso 13 — Incorporar time horizon

Separar:

- NOW;
- NEXT;
- LATER;

y event windows como:
- Black Friday;
- launch;
- fundraising;
- expansion;
- renegotiation.

---

## 15. Paso 14 — Evaluar core economic engine y concentration

Identificar:
- key products;
- key channels;
- contribution concentration;
- supplier/customer dependencies.

---

## 16. Paso 15 — Construir gap-to-objective

Mapear:

`CURRENT STATE → GAPS → TARGET STATE`

Después distinguir:
- binding gaps;
- non-binding gaps.

---

## 17. Paso 16 — Reframe si el objetivo está mal formulado

Ejemplos:

“We need more Meta spend”
→
“We need more profitable new customer acquisition.”

“We need better ROAS”
→
“Need better contribution allocation.”

---

## 18. Paso 17 — Evaluar context confidence

Preguntar:

- Are objectives clear?
- Is cash context known?
- Is maturity known?
- Are constraints known?
- Are resources visible?
- Are guardrails explicit?
- Is the horizon known?

Si falta objetivo:
- registrar `OBJECTIVE UNCERTAINTY`;
- no asumir growth.

---

## 19. Paso 18 — Producir Context Snapshot

Debe poder responder conceptualmente:

- business stage;
- primary objective;
- secondary objectives;
- time horizon;
- current constraint;
- cash context;
- unit economics;
- risk capacity;
- critical guardrails;
- resources;
- commitments;
- must win;
- must not fail;
- dependencies;
- seasonal/event window;
- confidence/gaps.

---

## 20. Paso 19 — Mantener contexto dinámico

Si ocurre un trigger material:

- financing;
- supplier failure;
- runway collapse;
- new major commitment;
- market change;

reevaluar objective/context.

---

## 21. Paso 20 — Evitar objective drift

No cambiar el objetivo por cada nueva métrica.

Exigir evidencia material.

Balance:

- enough stability to compound;
- enough adaptability to respond.

---

## 22. Output conceptual

El resultado no es todavía un ranking.

Es un contexto suficientemente claro para que bloques posteriores puedan priorizar:

`DECISION CONTEXT + DIAGNOSTIC CANDIDATES → PRIORITIZATION`

---

## 23. Boundary

P01 no:
- rankea candidatos;
- calcula priority score;
- selecciona playbooks;
- ejecuta iniciativas;
- define schemas técnicos.
