# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P10 — Runtime de ranking y síntesis de prioridad

> Este documento describe la lógica operacional. No prescribe implementación técnica.

---

## 1. Ingestar Decision Context

Antes de ranking:

- resolve current objective;
- resolve resource scope;
- resolve horizon;
- resolve decision scope;
- resolve business preferences.

Si el objetivo es ambiguo o contradictorio, marcarlo antes de generar ranking fuerte.

---

## 2. Normalizar Candidate Set

Para cada candidato:

- comprobar conexión con business outcome;
- detectar duplicados;
- agrupar symptoms con root causes;
- retirar non-material observations;
- retirar already-resolved items.

---

## 3. Hard Screening

Aplicar gates antes de scoring o ranking:

- legality;
- technical possibility;
- minimum evidence;
- survival;
- hard dependency;
- hard deadline;
- resource impossibility.

Resultado posible:

- eligible;
- blocked;
- conditional;
- validation required;
- not currently feasible.

---

## 4. Preserve Importance

Si un candidato crítico está bloqueado:

- mantener importance;
- separar executability.

Nunca convertir blocked → low priority automáticamente.

---

## 5. Detect Special Conditions

Buscar:

- existential risk;
- active severe incident;
- irreversible window;
- hard unlock;
- constraint protection;
- critical validation.

Si aplica, activar treatment especial.

---

## 6. Build Dimensional Profile

Construir perfil de:

- objective;
- urgency;
- time;
- value;
- constraint;
- dependency;
- expected value;
- resource need;
- feasibility;
- risk;
- reversibility;
- opportunity cost;
- stage fit;
- strategic fit;
- confidence.

No colapsar inmediatamente.

---

## 7. Remove Clearly Dominated Options

Cuando A domina B de forma robusta y comparable:

- reducir B en deliberación activa;
- conservar trazabilidad.

---

## 8. Identify Pareto Set

Seleccionar candidatos non-dominated para deep comparison.

---

## 9. Pairwise Comparison

Cuando exista ambigüedad:

- A vs B;
- A vs C;
- B vs C;

solo donde sea material.

No es necesario tournament exhaustivo.

---

## 10. Surface Trade-Offs

Explicar qué se intercambia:

- impact / effort;
- speed / certainty;
- upside / downside;
- now / long-term;
- efficiency / resilience.

No esconder trade-offs dentro de un número.

---

## 11. Constraint Adjustment

Para cada candidato:

- release?;
- protect?;
- worsen?;
- neutral?

Aplicar capturable-value logic si otro constraint limita beneficio.

---

## 12. Dependency Adjustment

Resolver:

- hard dependency;
- soft dependency;
- minimum prerequisite;
- unlock sequence.

Generar precedence cuando corresponda.

---

## 13. Detect Parallelism

Si candidatos usan recursos distintos y no tienen dependency:

- marcar run in parallel;
- no crear conflicto artificial.

---

## 14. Build Resource Conflict Map

Identificar:

- cash conflict;
- engineering conflict;
- creative conflict;
- management conflict;
- operations conflict.

Puede haber varios rankings por resource pool.

---

## 15. Urgency Adjustment

Evaluar:

- cost of delay;
- time to damage;
- intervention lead time;
- closing windows.

Filtrar artificial deadlines.

---

## 16. Protect Structural Work

Evitar que todos los slots se consuman por urgencies.

Mantener visibilidad de important-but-not-urgent structural work.

---

## 17. Risk Adjustment

Comprobar:

- downside;
- risk capacity;
- reversibility;
- false-positive / false-negative asymmetry.

Si downside excede capacity, cambiar decision structure.

---

## 18. Marginal Allocation Adjustment

Preguntar:

- best use of next scarce unit;
- current marginal return;
- alternative forgone;
- opportunity cost.

---

## 19. Strategic / Stage Adjustment

Comprobar:

- current objective;
- strategic coherence;
- stage fit;
- transformation nature;
- premature scaling risk.

---

## 20. Confidence Handling

Interpretar baja confidence según stakes.

### High impact + low confidence

Considerar `VALIDATE NOW`.

### Low impact + low confidence

Considerar `MONITOR` o defer.

### High confidence + low impact

No elevar por certainty únicamente.

---

## 21. Build Preliminary Order

Generar orden provisional manteniendo:

- tiers;
- states;
- blocks;
- parallelism.

---

## 22. Sanity Checks

Ejecutar checks de:

- objective fit;
- materiality;
- ease bias;
- recency bias;
- executability;
- dependencies;
- downside;
- constraint interaction;
- structural neglect;
- double counting;
- symptom/root-cause confusion;
- historical vs marginal;
- success capacity.

---

## 23. Sensitivity Analysis

Para top candidates:

- vary material assumptions;
- test downside/base/upside;
- inspect possible weight sensitivity;
- identify rank-switch drivers.

---

## 24. Detect Fragile Rank

Si pequeñas variaciones cambian winner:

- reduce ranking confidence;
- represent tie / near-tie;
- identify decisive unknown.

---

## 25. Abstain When Needed

Si evidencia no separa candidatos:

- no inventar winner;
- devolver top cluster;
- identify minimum evidence needed.

---

## 26. Determine Priority State

Asignar treatment conceptual:

- ACT NOW;
- VALIDATE NOW;
- PREPARE;
- SEQUENCE AFTER X;
- RUN IN PARALLEL;
- MONITOR;
- DEFER;
- DROP.

---

## 27. Determine Priority Class

Clasificar funcionalmente cuando sea útil:

- incident;
- structural;
- unlock;
- opportunity;
- validation;
- monitor.

---

## 28. Determine Tier and Rank

Separar:

- rank;
- tier;
- distance;
- confidence.

---

## 29. Generate Rank-Switch Condition

Para cada top priority:

- what evidence changes it?;
- what threshold?;
- what candidate overtakes?;
- what trigger forces reranking?

---

## 30. Stabilize Against Noise

Aplicar hysteresis conceptual:

- no rerank por cambios triviales;
- sí rerank ante material decision-input changes.

---

## 31. Consider Switching Cost

Antes de cambiar prioridad ya en ejecución:

- compare benefit of switching;
- transition cost;
- lost momentum;
- sunk cost must not dominate.

---

## 32. Decision Effort Scaling

Usar:

- light triage para minor reversible decisions;
- deep prioritization para high-stakes or irreversible decisions.

---

## 33. Triage Stage

Eliminar:

- noise;
- duplicates;
- dominated options;
- low-materiality work.

---

## 34. Deep Comparison Stage

Aplicar análisis completo solo al conjunto que podría recibir top attention.

---

## 35. Generate Explanation

Para cada top priority responder:

- why this?;
- why now?;
- why before others?;
- what value?;
- main uncertainty?;
- main dependency?;
- key resource?;
- main risk?;
- confidence?;
- what changes rank?

---

## 36. Progressive Disclosure

User-facing summary:

- concise top priorities;
- short rationale.

Detailed mode:

- evidence;
- trade-offs;
- assumptions;
- sensitivity;
- counter-ranking.

---

## 37. Audit Trace

Conservar conceptualmente:

`FACT → INTERPRETATION → TRADE-OFF → RANK`

más:

- evidence;
- objective;
- gates;
- overrides;
- states;
- sensitivity.

---

## 38. Re-Ranking

Reevaluar cuando cambie materialmente:

- evidence;
- constraint;
- opportunity;
- deadline;
- capacity;
- risk;
- objective.

---

## 39. Final Output

La salida final debe producir:

- defensible order of attention;
- priority state;
- sequence;
- parallelism;
- blocked items;
- monitoring items;
- rank confidence;
- rank-switch triggers.

El output no debe ser únicamente una lista de scores.
