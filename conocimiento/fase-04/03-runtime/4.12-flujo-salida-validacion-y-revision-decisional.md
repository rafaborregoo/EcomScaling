# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P12 — Runtime de salida, validación y revisión decisional

> Describe comportamiento operacional. No define APIs ni persistencia.

---

## 1. Receive Final Priority State

Recibir desde P10/P11:

- ranked priority;
- priority type;
- portfolio state;
- alternatives;
- resource allocation;
- dependencies;
- constraints;
- confidence.

---

## 2. Build Executive Decision

Generar una salida inicial con:

- what;
- why;
- why now;
- why before alternatives;
- main value;
- main risk;
- confidence;
- rank-switch condition.

---

## 3. Build Evidence Trace

Para cada claim decisivo:

- source;
- freshness;
- type;
- provenance;
- strength;
- conflict.

---

## 4. Classify Epistemic Status

Separar:

- FACT;
- INFERENCE;
- ASSUMPTION;
- PREFERENCE;
- PREDICTION;
- RECOMMENDATION;
- UNKNOWN.

---

## 5. Detect Source Conflicts

Si existen datos contradictorios:

- expose conflict;
- estimate decision materiality;
- require reconciliation when stakes justify it.

---

## 6. Identify Decision Drivers

No exponer todas las dimensiones.

Identificar las pocas que realmente movieron el ranking.

---

## 7. Explain Mechanism

Conectar:

`EVIDENCE → BUSINESS MECHANISM → IMPACT → TRADE-OFF → PRIORITY`

---

## 8. Explain Why Now

Usar:

- delay;
- window;
- damage velocity;
- dependency;
- state.

---

## 9. Explain Why Before Others

Comparar explícitamente con principal alternative.

---

## 10. Explain Opportunity Cost

Mostrar:

- what waits;
- what resource is occupied;
- what alternative is forgone.

---

## 11. Explain Portfolio Effects

Mostrar cuando aplique:

- parallelism;
- resource conflict;
- sequencing;
- displaced active work.

---

## 12. Identify Critical Assumptions

Seleccionar solo assumptions que podrían cambiar la decisión.

---

## 13. Preserve Unknowns

No completar campos ausentes.

Mark as unknown cuando corresponda.

---

## 14. Estimate Decision Confidence

Basar confidence en:

- evidence quality;
- evidence completeness;
- rank separation;
- sensitivity;
- uncertainty.

No usar fórmula obligatoria.

---

## 15. Produce Rank-Switch Condition

Definir qué evidence observable cambiaría la decisión.

---

## 16. Set Validity Window

Determinar conceptualmente cuánto tiempo puede permanecer vigente antes de reevaluación.

---

## 17. Set Review Trigger

Puede ser:

- event-driven;
- time-driven;
- threshold-driven;
- evidence-driven.

No establecer cadence universal.

---

## 18. Define Decision Thesis

Formular:

> A should be priority because X is expected to produce Y under Z.

---

## 19. Define Success / Failure Evidence

Antes de outcome:

- primary benefit;
- leading indicators;
- lagging indicators;
- guardrails;
- failure criteria.

---

## 20. Apply Progressive Disclosure

### Executive layer
- concise decision.

### Analyst layer
- evidence;
- assumptions;
- sensitivity;
- counterfactual;
- alternative comparison.

---

## 21. Support Follow-Up Queries

Debe poder responder consistentemente:

- why A?;
- why not B?;
- what if cash changes?;
- what assumption matters?;
- what if this source is wrong?

---

## 22. Monitor Review Conditions

Observe critical triggers conceptually.

Cuando cambia materialmente input:

- mark for reevaluation.

---

## 23. Reevaluate Decision

Si material new evidence:

1. identify changed assumption;
2. recompute relevance;
3. reassess rank;
4. explain change or persistence.

---

## 24. Distinguish Reality Change from Model Instability

Si rank cambia:

- was reality materially different?;
- or did tiny noise flip it?

---

## 25. Preserve Original Decision Record

Do not rewrite historical rationale.

Keep original:

- evidence;
- assumptions;
- thesis;
- context.

---

## 26. Validation Start Condition

No validar before sufficient signal maturity.

No wait past useful learning window.

---

## 27. Observe Actual Intervention

Before judging outcome:

- did intended intervention happen?;
- was it partial?;
- did scope drift?;
- did execution differ?

---

## 28. Observe Actual Outcome

Capture:

- output;
- outcome;
- leading signals;
- lagging result;
- guardrail changes.

---

## 29. Compare Expected vs Actual

Calculate conceptually:

- expected;
- actual;
- variance;
- realization gap.

---

## 30. Diagnose Variance

Ask whether difference came from:

- data;
- diagnosis;
- priority;
- portfolio;
- execution;
- model;
- assumption;
- timing;
- capacity;
- external change;
- randomness;
- measurement.

---

## 31. Separate Decision Quality from Outcome Quality

Evaluate ex ante:

- reasonable given information?;

Then ex post:

- what happened?;
- what was learned?

---

## 32. Causal Validation

When appropriate:

- counterfactual;
- experiment;
- cohort;
- time series;
- operational evidence;
- financial reconciliation.

---

## 33. Avoid Attribution Overclaim

If causality is not demonstrated:

- state contribution or uncertainty;
- do not claim full attribution.

---

## 34. Validate Individual Dimensions

Review:

- urgency;
- impact;
- constraint;
- opportunity cost;
- risk;
- reversibility;
- feasibility;
- strategic fit;
- stage fit;
- portfolio fit.

---

## 35. Validate Portfolio Interactions

Check:

- synergy;
- cannibalization;
- resource conflict;
- WIP;
- aggregate effects.

---

## 36. Produce Decision Review

Review should say:

- what was right;
- what was wrong;
- what changed;
- where error entered;
- whether original prioritization remained reasonable.

---

## 37. Produce Learning Statement

Output:

- mechanism learned;
- generalizable part;
- context-specific part;
- updated belief.

---

## 38. Feed Experience Layer

Only learning, not raw outcome label, should be promoted to future decision memory.

---

## 39. Support “Inconclusive”

If evidence insufficient:

- return inconclusive;
- decide whether more learning has sufficient VOI.

---

## 40. Close Decision

When evidence sufficient for current stakes:

- continue;
- change;
- stop;
- archive review;
- update belief.

---

## 41. Final Operational Chain

`FINAL PRIORITY`
↓
`EXPLANATION`
↓
`TRACE`
↓
`THESIS`
↓
`VALIDITY WINDOW`
↓
`REVIEW TRIGGER`
↓
`ACTUAL INTERVENTION`
↓
`ACTUAL OUTCOME`
↓
`VARIANCE`
↓
`ERROR ATTRIBUTION`
↓
`LEARNING`
↓
`UPDATED DECISION MODEL`
