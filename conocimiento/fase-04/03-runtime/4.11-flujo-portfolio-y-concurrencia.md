# Fase 4 — Priorización y decisión
## P11 — Runtime de portfolio y concurrencia

> Describe lógica operacional. No define implementación técnica.

---

## 1. Ingestar Ranking y Contexto

Recibir:

- ranked candidates de P10;
- objective;
- resource pools;
- stage;
- run load;
- active work;
- risks;
- dependencies.

---

## 2. Normalizar Business Priorities

Agrupar proyectos subordinados bajo outcomes superiores.

Evitar tratar cien tasks como cien prioridades.

---

## 3. Construir Active/Backlog Baseline

Separar:

- active;
- queued;
- blocked;
- paused;
- monitoring;
- stopped;
- completed.

---

## 4. Calcular Effective Capacity

Para cada resource pool:

1. nominal capacity;
2. subtract run-the-business load;
3. subtract fixed commitments;
4. account for seasonality/incidents;
5. preserve reasonable buffer;
6. obtain change capacity.

---

## 5. Mapear Recursos

Para cada iniciativa:

- resource types;
- shared resource;
- timing;
- minimum effective allocation;
- critical approver;
- hidden bottleneck.

---

## 6. Construir Dependency Graph

Mapear:

- hard dependencies;
- soft dependencies;
- unlocks;
- critical path;
- slack.

---

## 7. Construir Interaction Graph

Cuando sea material:

- synergy;
- complementarity;
- cannibalization;
- duplication;
- mutual exclusivity;
- risk interaction;
- measurement collision.

---

## 8. Detectar Conflictos

Para cada par material:

- resource conflict?;
- temporal conflict?;
- technical conflict?;
- strategic conflict?;
- customer conflict?;
- inventory conflict?;
- change-capacity conflict?;
- risk conflict?

---

## 9. Detectar True Parallelism

A y B pueden correr en paralelo si:

- resources independientes;
- dependencies compatibles;
- coordination manageable;
- risk not materially increased;
- measurement not contaminated.

---

## 10. Detectar Fake Parallelism

Buscar shared hidden resource:

- founder;
- data analyst;
- legal;
- QA;
- executive approval;
- critical system.

---

## 11. Evaluar WIP

Medir conceptualmente:

- active initiatives;
- queues;
- context switching;
- cycle time;
- lead time;
- carrying cost;
- throughput.

---

## 12. Detectar Portfolio Overload

Señales:

- too many starts;
- few finishes;
- thin allocation;
- long queue time;
- recurring reprioritization;
- context loss.

---

## 13. Aplicar WIP Discipline

No introducir nuevos active items si:

- no existe effective capacity;
- no pueden recibir minimum effective allocation;
- displacement no está justificado.

---

## 14. Admission Decision

Para cada new candidate:

1. high enough priority?;
2. ready?;
3. resource available?;
4. if not, what must move out?;
5. is displacement worth it?;
6. admit active or queue/defer.

---

## 15. Displacement Logic

Cuando capacity está llena:

- identify worst funded use of scarce resource;
- compare marginal value;
- account for switching cost;
- decide replace / defer.

---

## 16. Sequencing

Ordenar conflictos por:

- dependency;
- cost of delay;
- risk;
- learning;
- constraint;
- peak calendar;
- strategic foundation;
- resource availability.

---

## 17. Preserve Parallel Work

No bloquear iniciativas inferiores si usan recursos independientes.

---

## 18. Resource Queue Ordering

Para shared bottleneck:

- cost of delay;
- business value;
- constraint role;
- dependency role;
- risk.

No FIFO por defecto.

---

## 19. Protect Critical Path

Evitar consumir critical-path resources con trabajo secundario que retrase outcome principal.

---

## 20. Reserve Buffer

No planificar 100% change capacity.

Mantener margen razonable para:

- incidents;
- opportunities;
- dependencies;
- reviews.

---

## 21. Detect Portfolio Pollution

Buscar:

- zombie projects;
- pet projects;
- low-value reporting;
- duplicated work;
- unowned initiatives;
- legacy commitments.

---

## 22. Revalidate Active Work

Para cada iniciativa activa:

- still valuable?;
- still aligned?;
- still feasible?;
- still worth resource?;
- has risk changed?;
- better alternative appeared?

---

## 23. Apply Continue/Pause/Stop Logic

Si business case ya no justifica active slot:

- reduce;
- pause;
- stop;
- release capacity.

No mantener por sunk cost.

---

## 24. Reallocate Freed Capacity

Cuando capacity se libera:

- identify best current use;
- avoid automatic low-value fill;
- assign intentionally.

---

## 25. Preemption Evaluation

Ante nuevo evento:

1. value of switching now;
2. delay imposed on current work;
3. switching cost;
4. disruption;
5. alternate resource?;
6. if net advantage material → preempt.

---

## 26. Protect Structural Work

Evitar que incidentes menores consuman permanentemente structural capacity.

---

## 27. Aggregate Resource Load

Evaluar simultáneamente:

- total cash;
- engineering;
- creative;
- management;
- operational load.

---

## 28. Aggregate Risk

Evaluar:

- shared assumptions;
- shared failure modes;
- critical-system overlap;
- risk stacking;
- total cash downside;
- transformation density.

---

## 29. Learning Collision

Antes de concurrent experiments, comprobar:

- traffic overlap;
- customer exposure;
- measurement interference;
- analysis bandwidth.

---

## 30. Portfolio Balance

Comprobar si mix actual refleja objective:

- protect;
- recover;
- grow;
- structural;
- experiment;
- resilience;
- maintenance;
- mandatory.

No usar quotas fijas.

---

## 31. Scenario Stress Test

Ejecutar conceptualmente:

### Demand Down
- what stops?;
- what is protected?

### Cash Down
- what becomes unfundable?

### Demand Up
- can success be absorbed?

### Resource Loss
- what breaks?

### Major Incident
- what gets preempted?

---

## 32. Portfolio Robustness

Clasificar conceptualmente:

- robust;
- scenario-dependent;
- fragile.

---

## 33. Rebalance Trigger Check

Rebalance cuando cambia materialmente:

- objective;
- constraint;
- capital;
- market;
- resource;
- risk;
- evidence.

---

## 34. Avoid Over-Rebalancing

No cambiar composition por daily noise.

Preservar execution continuity.

---

## 35. Build Temporal Queue

Representar:

- NOW;
- NEXT;
- LATER;
- MONITOR.

---

## 36. Build Portfolio States

Para cada item:

- ACTIVE;
- QUEUED;
- BLOCKED;
- PAUSED;
- MONITORING;
- STOPPED;
- COMPLETED.

---

## 37. Maintain Ready-Next Queue

Preparar high-value work próximo a entrar cuando se libera capacidad.

No crear half-finished inventory.

---

## 38. Capacity Release Calendar

Anticipar:

- projects ending;
- hires arriving;
- capacity leaving;
- seasonality;
- vendor changes.

---

## 39. Review Decision

Cada portfolio review debe producir acción:

- continue;
- accelerate;
- reduce;
- pause;
- stop;
- resequence;
- replace;
- validate.

---

## 40. Explain Portfolio

La salida debe explicar:

- why active;
- why queued;
- what conflicts;
- what can run in parallel;
- what must stop;
- what is the bottleneck;
- what risk is aggregate;
- what would trigger rebalance.

---

## 41. Output

P11 debe producir:

> executable portfolio

no solo:

> ranked list.
