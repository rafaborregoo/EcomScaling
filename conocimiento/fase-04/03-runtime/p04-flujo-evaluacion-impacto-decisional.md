# Fase 04 · P04 — Runtime conceptual de evaluación de impacto

## Propósito

P04 evalúa cuánto valor empresarial real está en juego por cada decision candidate.

No produce todavía el ranking final ni ordena por effort.

---

## 1. Inputs

Puede consumir:

- decision candidate;
- diagnosis/root cause;
- exposure;
- business context;
- unit economics;
- customer economics;
- cash context;
- inventory;
- concentration;
- counterfactual/baseline;
- constraints;
- time horizon;
- risk capacity;
- evidence confidence.

---

## 2. Paso 1 — Identificar value type

Preguntar:

- revenue?
- contribution?
- cash?
- customer value?
- capacity?
- strategic?
- resilience?
- optionality?
- learning?
- risk exposure?

---

## 3. Paso 2 — Separar current vs potential

Clasificar:

- realized;
- potential;
- recoverable;
- irrecoverable.

---

## 4. Paso 3 — Elegir economic layer correcta

No usar revenue por defecto.

Según la decisión:
- gross profit;
- contribution;
- cash;
- CLV;
- strategic value.

---

## 5. Paso 4 — Definir baseline

Elegir:
- historical normal;
- forecast;
- matched cohort;
- control;
- expected trajectory;
- seasonally adjusted baseline.

---

## 6. Paso 5 — Construir counterfactual

Preguntar:

> What would have happened otherwise?

Sin counterfactual no afirmar incrementality.

---

## 7. Paso 6 — Medir absolute + relative impact

Cuando sea posible:
- value;
- %;
- share of business.

---

## 8. Paso 7 — Evaluar exposure

Por:
- revenue;
- contribution;
- orders;
- customers;
- traffic;
- market;
- device;
- product;
- channel.

---

## 9. Paso 8 — Aplicar value density

No tratar unidades expuestas como iguales.

Ponderar según:
- margin;
- CLV;
- contribution;
- strategic relevance.

---

## 10. Paso 9 — Evaluar recoverability

Preguntar:
- can demand be deferred?
- can it be recaptured?
- can customers substitute internally?
- does lost value return later?

---

## 11. Paso 10 — Evaluar substitution

Distinguir:
- internal transfer;
- lost demand;
- external switching;
- contribution dilution.

---

## 12. Paso 11 — Evaluar cannibalization

Para opportunities:
- what is gross new value?
- what existing value is displaced?
- what is truly incremental?

---

## 13. Paso 12 — Evaluar cash

Analizar:
- liquidity consumed;
- cash blocked;
- timing;
- working capital;
- payback;
- survival implication.

---

## 14. Paso 13 — Evaluar customer lifetime impact

Si category/business model lo soporta:
- repeat;
- future contribution;
- cohort value;
- switching;
- trust.

---

## 15. Paso 14 — Evaluar operational capacity

Medir:
- throughput loss;
- team hours;
- engineering/creative capacity;
- management attention.

---

## 16. Paso 15 — Evaluar strategic/enabling value

Preguntar:
- what objective is blocked?
- what high-value work is unlocked?
- what decisions improve?
- what infrastructure enables value?

---

## 17. Paso 16 — Evaluar learning value

Preguntar:
> Would this information materially change resource allocation?

Si no:
- learning value bajo.

---

## 18. Paso 17 — Evaluar optionality / resilience

Analizar:
- options preserved;
- lock-in created;
- redundancy;
- downside reduction.

---

## 19. Paso 18 — Evaluar blast radius

Identificar:
- direct scope;
- downstream capabilities;
- critical path;
- common dependencies.

---

## 20. Paso 19 — Evaluar concentration

Por:
- revenue;
- contribution;
- customer;
- channel;
- supplier;
- payment;
- market.

---

## 21. Paso 20 — Evaluar impact amplifiers/dampeners

Amplifiers:
- concentration;
- critical path;
- recurrence;
- compounding;
- low recoverability;
- low redundancy.

Dampeners:
- substitution;
- redundancy;
- recoverability;
- low exposure;
- short duration.

---

## 22. Paso 21 — Evaluar frequency × magnitude × duration

Especialmente para pequeñas ineficiencias repetidas.

---

## 23. Paso 22 — Evaluar second-order effects

Seguir la cadena hasta efectos materiales.

Detener:
- narrative explosion;
- speculative cascades.

---

## 24. Paso 23 — Evaluar attribution

Preguntar:

> How much of the outcome is actually caused by this candidate?

No confundir affected con caused.

---

## 25. Paso 24 — Detectar multi-causality

Permitir:
- partial contribution;
- multiple causes;
- residual/uncertain share.

---

## 26. Paso 25 — Detectar overlap

Comparar candidatos para evitar double counting.

---

## 27. Paso 26 — Evaluar correlated downside

Buscar common dependencies.

---

## 28. Paso 27 — Evaluar constraint release

Si candidate está ligado a constraint:
- throughput prevented;
- throughput unlocked;
- next constraint.

---

## 29. Paso 28 — Evaluar marginal value

No usar historical average como proxy del siguiente euro/unidad.

---

## 30. Paso 29 — Evaluar opportunity capacity

Preguntar:
- how scalable?
- what is saturating?
- what is capturable?

---

## 31. Paso 30 — Construir scenarios

Cuando uncertainty material:
- best;
- base;
- downside;
- upside.

---

## 32. Paso 31 — Evaluar sensitivity

Identificar:
- assumptions that most change estimate.

---

## 33. Paso 32 — Calibrar confidence

Separar:
- diagnosis confidence;
- impact confidence;
- attribution confidence.

---

## 34. Paso 33 — Construir Impact Profile

Devolver:
- value type;
- magnitude;
- exposure;
- incremental value;
- recoverability;
- overlap;
- second-order effects;
- scenarios;
- confidence;
- assumptions.

---

## 35. Paso 34 — Explicar impacto

Debe poder responder:

- what value;
- how much;
- for whom;
- over what horizon;
- what is incremental;
- what is recoverable;
- what can be protected/recovered/unlocked;
- with what confidence.

---

## 36. Boundary

P04 no:
- ordena por effort;
- calcula ranking final;
- monetiza todo;
- inventa probabilities;
- atribuye sin counterfactual;
- suma overlapping impacts.
