# FASE 7.10 — Contexto del tenant para recomendaciones por madurez

## Propósito

La adaptación por madurez depende de contexto específico del ecommerce.

Este documento recoge categorías de información.

No contiene valores concretos.

---

## 1. Business Size

Puede incluir:

- revenue;
- orders;
- customers;
- traffic.

No debe confundirse con maturity.

---

## 2. Company Age

Puede aportar contexto.

No determina maturity.

---

## 3. Growth Rate

Puede existir:

```text
High Growth
+
Low Maturity
```

---

## 4. Functional Maturity

Puede variar por:

- acquisition;
- CRO;
- retention;
- operations;
- measurement;
- merchandising;
- finance;
- data;
- experimentation.

---

## 5. Intervention Readiness

Debe evaluarse por intervención concreta.

---

## 6. Traffic Volume

Afecta experimentation capability.

---

## 7. Conversion Volume

Afecta testability.

---

## 8. Data Reliability

Puede incluir:

- tracking;
- identity;
- catalog data;
- cohorts;
- reporting.

---

## 9. Historical Data

Mature systems pueden aprovechar más internal benchmarks.

---

## 10. Experimentation Capability

Puede depender de:

- volume;
- measurement;
- discipline;
- hypothesis quality;
- implementation.

---

## 11. Acquisition Repeatability

Necesario para distinguir signal de repeatability.

---

## 12. Unit Economics

Puede incluir:

- contribution;
- CAC;
- margin;
- payback.

---

## 13. Marginal Economics

Especialmente relevante en scaling/maturity.

---

## 14. Cash

Early-stage puede tener survival constraints.

---

## 15. Founder Attention

Puede ser critical bottleneck.

---

## 16. Team Capacity

Puede limitar complexity y parallelism.

---

## 17. Organizational Structure

Puede incluir:

- generalists;
- hybrid roles;
- specialists;
- multiple teams.

---

## 18. Ownership

Puede afectar intervention readiness.

---

## 19. Approval Processes

Relevantes en complex organizations.

---

## 20. Management Attention

Recurso escaso incluso en empresas grandes.

---

## 21. Tool Stack

No demuestra maturity por sí solo.

---

## 22. Tool Usage Capacity

Debe evaluarse si:

- owner exists;
- data exists;
- usage exists;
- maintenance exists.

---

## 23. Process Standardization

Puede indicar repeatability.

---

## 24. Automation Level

Debe corresponder a process maturity.

---

## 25. Operational Stability

Puede incluir:

- fulfilment;
- support;
- exceptions;
- warehouse;
- returns.

---

## 26. Inventory Discipline

Relevante para scaling maturity.

---

## 27. Forecasting Capability

Depende de historical stability.

---

## 28. Channel Concentration

Puede ser fragility signal.

---

## 29. Supplier Concentration

Puede afectar resilience.

---

## 30. Product Concentration

Puede afectar portfolio maturity.

---

## 31. Geographic Complexity

Puede aumentar organizational complexity.

---

## 32. Market / Category Novelty

Una empresa madura puede tener local maturity baja en un nuevo market/category.

---

## 33. Current Business Mode

Puede ser:

- validation;
- stabilization;
- scaling;
- optimization;
- expansion.

---

## 34. Current Bottleneck

Debe orientar stage-appropriate recommendation.

---

## 35. Current Debt

Puede incluir:

- technical;
- operational;
- data;
- organizational;
- process debt.

---

## 36. Current Parallel Initiatives

Ayuda a estimar attention/coordination capacity.

---

## 37. Current Risk Profile

Early y mature pueden tener risk distinto.

---

## 38. Resilience Requirements

Pueden incluir:

- redundancy;
- diversification;
- vendor alternatives;
- operational buffers.

---

## 39. Customer Base Maturity

Puede incluir:

- repeat history;
- cohorts;
- lifecycle data;
- LTV evidence.

---

## 40. Pricing Maturity

Puede ir desde WTP learning hasta price architecture/elasticity.

---

## 41. Retention Maturity

Puede ir desde basic flows hasta predictive/value-based lifecycle.

---

## 42. Analytics Maturity

Puede ir desde:

```text
Visibility
→ Diagnostic
→ Incremental / Predictive
```

---

## 43. Contexto no definido por la fuente

Este bloque no define:

- exact fields;
- stage thresholds;
- maturity scores;
- update cadence;
- canonical sources;
- org IDs;
- capability scoring;
- intervention readiness score.
