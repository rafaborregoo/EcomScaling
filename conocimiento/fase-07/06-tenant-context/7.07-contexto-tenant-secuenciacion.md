# FASE 7.7 — Contexto del tenant para secuenciación

## Propósito

La secuencia depende del estado real del ecommerce.

Este documento recoge categorías de tenant context que pueden cambiar prerequisites, blockers, gates y parallelization.

No contiene valores concretos.

---

## 1. Current Strategic Priority

Necesario para saber cuál es la intervención principal que se intenta desbloquear.

---

## 2. Current Business Mode

Puede ser:

- crisis;
- early-stage;
- growth;
- mature;
- stabilize;
- scale;
- expand.

---

## 3. Current Bottleneck

Puede estar en:

- acquisition;
- conversion;
- inventory;
- operations;
- cash;
- creative;
- technical;
- data;
- retention.

---

## 4. Data Readiness

Puede incluir:

- tracking reliability;
- customer history;
- cohort quality;
- attribution;
- event coverage.

---

## 5. Unit Economics Readiness

Puede incluir:

- contribution;
- marginal CAC;
- margin;
- payback;
- channel economics.

---

## 6. Inventory Readiness

Puede incluir:

- stock coverage;
- replenishment status;
- lead time;
- reorder timing;
- supplier reliability.

---

## 7. Operational Readiness

Puede incluir:

- fulfilment capacity;
- support;
- warehouse;
- returns;
- process stability.

---

## 8. Cash / Working Capital

Puede bloquear o habilitar:

- inventory purchase;
- demand scale;
- expansion.

---

## 9. Technical Capacity

Puede incluir:

- developer availability;
- stack constraints;
- release capacity;
- feature flags;
- rollback capability.

---

## 10. Creative Capacity

Puede ser prerequisite de:

- testing cadence;
- channel expansion;
- paid media scale.

---

## 11. Ownership

Puede requerirse:

- owner;
- authority;
- process responsibility.

---

## 12. Current Projects

Necesario para detectar:

- resource contention;
- parallelization limits;
- management overload.

---

## 13. Management Bandwidth

Puede bloquear parallel tracks aunque los equipos sean distintos.

---

## 14. Traffic / Data Volume

Determina si pueden ejecutarse:

- multiple concurrent experiments;
- one material hypothesis at a time.

---

## 15. Current Experiments

Puede ser necesario saber:

- active test;
- variable under test;
- expected observation window;
- contamination risk.

---

## 16. Purchase / Retention Cycle

Afecta observation windows.

---

## 17. Lead Times

Puede incluir:

- hiring;
- inventory;
- supplier;
- warehouse;
- localization;
- logistics.

---

## 18. External Dependencies

Puede incluir:

- supplier;
- vendor;
- platform;
- regulator;
- payment provider;
- logistics partner.

---

## 19. Platform / Tooling Constraints

Puede definir qué prerequisites son necesarias o qué parallelization es viable.

---

## 20. Guardrails

Pueden actuar como:

- hard gate;
- soft gate;
- safety interrupt.

---

## 21. Current Risk State

Puede justificar:

- containment;
- protective action;
- rollback;
- delayed progression.

---

## 22. Seasonality / Opportunity Window

Puede cambiar:

- lead-time priority;
- wait cost;
- sequence compression.

---

## 23. Market Expansion Context

Puede requerir:

- demand validation;
- pricing;
- payment;
- logistics;
- support;
- compliance.

---

## 24. Supplier / Inventory Context

Puede cambiar si demand scaling debe esperar.

---

## 25. Process Maturity

Puede definir si se necesita:

```text
Understand
→ Standardize
→ Automate
```

---

## 26. CRM / Customer Data Maturity

Puede definir orden en retention y lifecycle.

---

## 27. Internationalization Readiness

Puede incluir:

- localized offer;
- pricing;
- payment methods;
- logistics;
- customer support.

---

## 28. Tooling Readiness

No debe asumirse que tool nuevo es prerequisite si current stack ya resuelve lo necesario.

---

## 29. Current Capability Gaps

Puede incluir:

- creative;
- analytics;
- operations;
- development;
- lifecycle;
- supply planning.

---

## 30. Current Dependency Status

Debe poder conocerse si una prerequisite está:

- unresolved;
- partially resolved;
- sufficiently ready.

Sin definir enum técnico.

---

## 31. Contexto no definido por la fuente

Este bloque no define:

- fields;
- data sources;
- update cadence;
- dependency status schema;
- readiness thresholds;
- calendars;
- owners model;
- project database;
- workflow engine.
