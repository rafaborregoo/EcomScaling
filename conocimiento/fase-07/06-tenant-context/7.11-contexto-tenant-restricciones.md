# FASE 7.11 — Contexto del tenant para recomendaciones bajo restricciones

## Propósito

Las constraints son específicas del negocio y cambian con el tiempo.

Este documento recoge categorías de tenant context relevantes.

No contiene valores concretos.

---

## 1. Cash

Puede incluir:

- current liquidity;
- runway;
- available capital;
- financing capacity.

---

## 2. Working Capital

Puede incluir:

- inventory cash cycle;
- supplier terms;
- payment delays;
- return/refund lag.

---

## 3. Payback Tolerance

Puede cambiar según liquidity y stage.

---

## 4. People

No solo headcount.

Puede incluir:

- skill;
- availability;
- ownership;
- execution quality.

---

## 5. Founder Attention

Puede ser bottleneck principal en SMB.

---

## 6. Executive Attention

Puede limitar cross-functional initiatives en empresas grandes.

---

## 7. Developer Capacity

Puede incluir:

- available weeks;
- current roadmap;
- technical debt;
- access constraints.

---

## 8. Creative Capacity

Puede incluir:

- production throughput;
- concepts;
- UGC availability;
- approval bottlenecks;
- key-person dependency.

---

## 9. Operations Capacity

Puede incluir:

- fulfilment;
- warehouse;
- support;
- returns;
- QA;
- packaging;
- procurement.

---

## 10. Operational Load

Necesario para estimar headroom.

---

## 11. Inventory

Puede incluir:

- stock by SKU;
- hero SKU availability;
- overstock;
- replenishment;
- lead times;
- seasonal risk.

---

## 12. Data Availability

Puede incluir:

- tracking;
- cohorts;
- attribution;
- customer identity;
- catalog data;
- forecasting inputs.

---

## 13. Data Quality

Missing data puede bloquear o solo reducir confidence.

---

## 14. Management Bandwidth

Puede limitar initiative count.

---

## 15. Legal / Compliance Context

Puede incluir:

- privacy;
- consent;
- advertising rules;
- promotions;
- claims;
- consumer protection.

---

## 16. Channel Capacity

Puede incluir:

- saturation;
- audience scale;
- platform restrictions;
- tracking constraints.

---

## 17. Market Capacity

Puede incluir:

- TAM;
- reachable demand;
- purchase frequency;
- seasonality.

---

## 18. Brand Positioning

Puede limitar:

- discounting;
- promo depth;
- messaging tactics.

---

## 19. Customer Experience Capacity

Puede incluir:

- support SLA;
- delivery performance;
- complaint load;
- reviews.

---

## 20. Current Constraints

Debe poder conocerse qué limita actualmente.

---

## 21. Constraint Granularity

Puede ser específica de:

- SKU;
- channel;
- market;
- team;
- geography;
- segment;
- period.

---

## 22. Constraint Duration

Puede ser:

- temporary;
- structural.

---

## 23. Constraint Predictability

Puede ser:

- predictable;
- uncertain;
- volatile.

---

## 24. Constraint Slack

Debe conocerse si todavía existe margin.

---

## 25. Constraint Controllability

Puede ser:

- controllable;
- partially controllable;
- uncontrollable.

---

## 26. Resource Elasticity

Qué recursos pueden ampliarse rápidamente.

---

## 27. External Capacity

Puede incluir:

- supplier;
- agency;
- marketplace;
- logistics provider;
- platform.

---

## 28. Current Allocation

Necesario para distinguir scarcity de misallocation.

---

## 29. Waste

Puede existir capacidad recuperable eliminando low-value work.

---

## 30. Current Priorities

Ayuda a calcular opportunity cost.

---

## 31. Active Initiatives

Necesario para aggregate resource load.

---

## 32. Current Bottleneck

Debe guiar best feasible action.

---

## 33. Secondary / Latent Constraints

Puede ser útil anticipar próximos límites.

---

## 34. Current Growth Mode

Puede ser:

- growth;
- cash preservation;
- stabilization;
- optimization.

---

## 35. Survival / Runway State

Puede actuar como critical constraint.

---

## 36. Current Intervention Exposure

Puede ayudar a decidir si:

- reduce;
- maintain;
- reallocate;
- wait.

---

## 37. Replenishment Schedule

Puede activar deferred scaling.

---

## 38. Opportunity Window

Puede afectar timing response.

---

## 39. Existing Workarounds

Debe conocerse si una temporary solution se está volviendo structural debt.

---

## 40. Buffer / Safety Margin

Puede incluir:

- cash;
- inventory;
- operations;
- creative backlog;
- team availability.

---

## 41. Contexto no definido por la fuente

Este bloque no define:

- exact fields;
- constraint IDs;
- thresholds;
- source systems;
- update cadence;
- allocation rules;
- resource ownership schema;
- capacity formulas;
- forecasting implementation.
