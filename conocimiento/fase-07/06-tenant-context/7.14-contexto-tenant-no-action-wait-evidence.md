# FASE 7.14 — Contexto del tenant para No-action / Wait / Gather Evidence

## Propósito

Estas decisiones dependen del estado real del ecommerce.

No contiene valores concretos.

---

## 1. Current Performance

Puede incluir:

- revenue;
- CVR;
- AOV;
- CAC;
- margin;
- contribution;
- retention.

---

## 2. Historical Baseline

Necesario para distinguir noise de structural change.

---

## 3. Current Variability

Puede incluir:

- daily swings;
- seasonality;
- promotion effects;
- mix shifts.

---

## 4. Current Economics

Para valorar status quo vs intervention.

---

## 5. Current Opportunity Cost

Qué otra área compite por:

- money;
- time;
- people;
- attention.

---

## 6. Current Portfolio

Ayuda a decidir si debe evitarse añadir trabajo.

---

## 7. Current Business Mode

Puede ser:

- crisis;
- stabilize;
- optimize;
- scale;
- expand.

---

## 8. Current Maturity

Afecta available evidence y rigor possible.

---

## 9. Current Risk Capacity

Puede reducir tolerancia a uncertain actions.

---

## 10. Reversibility

Clave para decidir Act Small vs Gather Evidence.

---

## 11. Cost of Delay

Puede depender de:

- seasonality;
- outage;
- stock aging;
- competitor move;
- legal deadline.

---

## 12. Known Upcoming Events

Puede incluir:

- supplier confirmation;
- stock arrival;
- campaign completion;
- regulatory decision;
- cohort maturity.

---

## 13. Stabilization State

Si previous intervention aún se está asentando.

---

## 14. Natural Outcome Latency

Puede incluir:

- repurchase cycle;
- returns window;
- LTV maturation.

---

## 15. Data Availability

Puede incluir:

- analytics;
- customer research;
- cohorts;
- financial data;
- operational data.

---

## 16. Data Quality

Determina qué evidence es usable.

---

## 17. Evidence Freshness

Especialmente para:

- media;
- demand;
- inventory.

---

## 18. Customer Feedback

Puede ser qualitative evidence.

---

## 19. Behavioral Evidence

Qué customers realmente hacen.

---

## 20. Financial Evidence

Si outcome crea economic value.

---

## 21. Operational Evidence

Si system puede absorber recommendation.

---

## 22. Incrementality Evidence

Relevante para:

- branded;
- promotions;
- retargeting;
- loyalty.

---

## 23. Segment Heterogeneity

Puede requerir evidence a granularidad útil.

---

## 24. Sample Size / Volume

Afecta testability.

---

## 25. Traffic Volume

Puede limitar formal experimentation.

---

## 26. Customer Purchase Cycle

Afecta wait horizon.

---

## 27. Inventory Context

Puede justificar:

- wait;
- no-action;
- demand shaping.

---

## 28. Supplier Lead Times

Puede definir known resolution points.

---

## 29. Team Capacity

Research and change also consume resources.

---

## 30. Analytical Capacity

Puede limitar número de open investigations.

---

## 31. Founder / Management Attention

Puede ser evidence-budget constraint.

---

## 32. Change Capacity

Puede justificar temporary portfolio freeze.

---

## 33. External Uncertainty

Puede incluir:

- platform;
- regulation;
- economy;
- market;
- competitor;
- supplier.

---

## 34. Brand Stability

Frequent changes can create harm.

---

## 35. Pricing Stability

Frequent price changes can train customers.

---

## 36. Algorithmic Learning Context

Puede justificar Wait after material campaign changes.

---

## 37. Technical Stability

Replatforming o migrations pueden requerir stabilization window.

---

## 38. Process Repeatability

Relevante antes de automation.

---

## 39. Hiring Demand Durability

Necesario para permanent headcount decisions.

---

## 40. Tooling Need

Debe basarse en workflow bottleneck real.

---

## 41. International Market Evidence

Puede faltar localized demand.

---

## 42. Portfolio Uncertainty

Si ya existen muchas uncertain bets, puede ser mejor no añadir otra.

---

## 43. Risk Budget

Capacidad finita para concurrent downside.

---

## 44. Decision Triggers

Puede haber condiciones específicas que hagan revisar decisión.

---

## 45. Contexto no definido

No se definen:

- exact fields;
- data source mappings;
- trigger thresholds;
- review cadence;
- evidence scores;
- research workflow;
- monitoring implementation;
- alert rules.
