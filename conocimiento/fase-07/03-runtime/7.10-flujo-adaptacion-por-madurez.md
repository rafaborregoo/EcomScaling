# FASE 7.10 — Runtime conceptual de adaptación por madurez

## Propósito

Este documento extrae la lógica operacional implícita de 7.10.

No define un maturity scoring engine.

---

## 1. Entrada

El flujo recibe:

- prioritized problem;
- cause;
- candidate intervention;
- business maturity context;
- functional maturity;
- capabilities;
- evidence;
- constraints;
- risk;
- resources.

---

## 2. No inferir maturity por revenue

Nunca asumir:

```text
High Revenue
→ High Maturity
```

---

## 3. No inferir maturity por company age

Nunca asumir:

```text
Old Company
→ Mature Organization
```

---

## 4. No inferir maturity por tooling

Nunca asumir:

```text
Advanced Tools
→ Advanced Capabilities
```

---

## 5. Identificar Functional Maturity

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué tan madura es el área concreta afectada?

---

## 6. Identificar Intervention Readiness

Preguntar:

> ¿Está esta organización preparada para esta intervención específica?

---

## 7. Detectar missing capability

Si:

```text
Desired Intervention
requires
Missing Capability
```

entonces:

```text
Build Capability First
```

puede convertirse en la recomendación actual.

---

## 8. Identificar Next Constraint

No intentar resolver el estado final del ecommerce.

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué capability o constraint bloquea el siguiente avance útil?

---

## 9. Determinar mode

Conceptualmente decidir si el área está principalmente en:

- learning;
- validation;
- repeatability;
- scaling;
- optimization;
- stabilization;
- coordination.

---

## 10. Ajustar complexity

Aplicar:

```text
Recommendation Complexity
≤
Absorption Capacity
```

---

## 11. Detectar Sophistication Debt

Buscar señales como:

- unused tools;
- unusable dashboards;
- too-fine segmentation;
- automation without supervision;
- tests without volume;
- false precision;
- unmaintainable processes.

---

## 12. Prefer Minimum Sufficient Complexity

Seleccionar la solución menos compleja que resuelva suficientemente el problema.

---

## 13. Detectar premature optimization

Preguntar:

> ¿Estamos optimizando algo que todavía no es el bottleneck?

---

## 14. Detectar foundation gaps

Antes de sophistication revisar:

- value proposition;
- product;
- economics;
- acquisition;
- basic conversion;
- operations;
- sufficient measurement.

---

## 15. Avoid perfection trap

No bloquear avance por falta de perfección.

Preguntar:

> ¿Existe suficiente estabilidad para avanzar de forma controlada?

---

## 16. Stage 1 logic — Validation

Favorecer:

- learning;
- demand validation;
- offer validation;
- customer feedback;
- willingness-to-pay;
- basic economics.

Evitar:

- overbuilding;
- advanced optimization;
- irreversible bets.

---

## 17. Stage 2 logic — Commercial Fit

Favorecer:

- channel/message/offer learning;
- pricing;
- merchandising;
- landing improvements;
- initial retention.

No confundir:

```text
Positive Signal
```

con:

```text
Repeatability
```

---

## 18. Stage 3 logic — Repeatability

Favorecer:

- standardization;
- recurring analysis;
- funnel improvement;
- lifecycle;
- measurement;
- inventory planning;
- unit economics.

---

## 19. Stage 4 logic — Scaling

Favorecer:

- marginal economics;
- capacity;
- cash conversion;
- saturation;
- inventory;
- operations;
- creative capacity;
- concentration risk.

---

## 20. Stage 5 logic — Mature

Favorecer:

- marginal efficiency;
- incrementality;
- portfolio optimization;
- advanced retention;
- channel mix;
- pricing;
- resilience.

---

## 21. Stage 6 logic — Complex Organization

Añadir:

- ownership;
- cross-functional effects;
- approvals;
- systems dependencies;
- coordination cost;
- blast radius.

---

## 22. Detect Local Reset

Si un negocio maduro entra en:

- new category;
- new market;
- new channel;
- new model;

tratar ese dominio parcialmente como validation.

---

## 23. Adjust evidence requirement

Early + low data:

```text
Directional Evidence
+
Qualitative Evidence
+
Small Exposure
```

Mature + large blast radius:

```text
Stronger Evidence
```

---

## 24. Adjust experimentation method

Low volume:

- qualitative;
- usability;
- aggregated tests;
- direct fixes.

High volume:

- controlled experimentation;
- finer segmentation.

---

## 25. Adjust intervention size

Early under uncertainty:

- smaller;
- reversible;
- informative.

Mature with evidence:

- larger;
- systematic;
- cross-functional.

Siempre subordinado a 7.6/7.9.

---

## 26. Adjust horizon

Early:

- faster learning.

Repeatable:

- faster time-to-value.

Mature:

- short-term value + strategic capability.

---

## 27. Adjust resource perspective

Early:

- cash;
- founder time;
- speed.

Mature:

- team capacity;
- coordination;
- roadmap;
- resilience;
- management attention.

---

## 28. Detect founder bottleneck

Si todo depende de una o dos personas:

- avoid manual workload explosion;
- prefer focused intervention.

---

## 29. Detect coordination bottleneck

En organizations avanzadas:

- identify cross-functional cost;
- ownership;
- approvals;
- management bandwidth.

---

## 30. Adjust focus

Lower maturity:

> fewer simultaneous strategic priorities.

Higher maturity:

> potentially more parallelism, but finite.

---

## 31. Determine Now vs Later

Separar:

```text
What should happen now?
```

de:

```text
What becomes sensible later?
```

---

## 32. Emit Not Yet when appropriate

Una idea puede ser:

```text
Good
but
Premature
```

---

## 33. Identify Capability-Building Recommendation

Cuando la mejor acción no genera revenue directo pero desbloquea future value.

---

## 34. Estimate Unlocking Value conceptually

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué futuras intervenciones habilita esta capability?

---

## 35. Detect maturity regression

Re-evaluar después de:

- replatform;
- new market;
- acquisition;
- logistics crisis;
- key-team loss.

---

## 36. Detect stabilization need

Si accumulated debt domina:

```text
Grow
→ Stabilize
```

temporalmente.

---

## 37. Avoid stabilization forever

Distinguir:

```text
Critical Instability
```

de:

```text
Normal Imperfection
```

---

## 38. System-Level Outcome

En maturity avanzada, evitar local optimization.

---

## 39. Resilience Check

Puede ser racional sacrificar una pequeña cantidad de efficiency por lower systemic risk.

---

## 40. Opportunity Cost Check

Mature organizations pueden tener múltiples good opportunities.

Comparar con:

```text
Best Alternative Use of Current Capabilities
```

---

## 41. Produce maturity-adjusted output

Debe explicar:

- maturity context;
- current capability;
- missing capability;
- appropriate recommendation now;
- what to defer;
- why;
- what unlocks next.

---

## 42. Hand-off

La salida alimenta:

- 7.11 constraints;
- 7.12 portfolio;
- 7.13 progression;
- 7.16 trade-offs;
- 7.17 confidence;
- Fase 8 execution.

---

## 43. Frontera operacional

Este runtime NO:

- asigna maturity scores;
- cambia org design;
- contrata equipo;
- compra tooling;
- ejecuta automation;
- crea project plans;
- cambia procesos automáticamente.
