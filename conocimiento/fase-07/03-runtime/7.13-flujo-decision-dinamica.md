# FASE 7.13 — Runtime conceptual para Stop / Continue / Scale / Rollback

## Propósito

Este documento extrae la lógica operacional implícita de 7.13.

No define automatización, monitoring infrastructure ni state machine técnico.

---

## 1. Entrada

El flujo recibe:

- original recommendation;
- intervention state;
- observed outcomes;
- baseline;
- expected mechanism;
- expected outcome;
- economics;
- guardrails;
- constraints;
- risk;
- confidence;
- headroom;
- portfolio context.

---

## 2. Verify Intervention Fidelity

Antes de concluir strategy failure:

> ¿Se ejecutó como estaba diseñada?

Si no:

- correct implementation;
- re-evaluate;
- avoid invalid inference.

---

## 3. Compare Intended Mechanism vs Reality

Preguntar:

> ¿Se activó el mechanism esperado?

---

## 4. Compare Expected vs Observed Outcome

Evaluar:

- primary outcome;
- secondary effects;
- economics;
- quality.

---

## 5. Validate Baseline

Preguntar si el reference period es comparable.

Considerar:

- seasonality;
- promotions;
- market shifts;
- channel changes.

---

## 6. Evaluate Trend

No solo current level.

Preguntar si outcome está:

- improving;
- stable;
- deteriorating.

---

## 7. Evaluate Attribution Confidence

Distinguir observed improvement de causal confidence.

---

## 8. Evaluate Incrementality

Especialmente en:

- paid media;
- promotions;
- retargeting;
- CRM.

---

## 9. Evaluate Guardrails

Si guardrail material se viola:

- do not scale;
- consider hold / de-scale / stop / rollback.

---

## 10. Evaluate Constraints

Preguntar:

> ¿Ha aparecido un new binding constraint?

---

## 11. Evaluate Risk

Incluir:

- current downside;
- delayed downside;
- second-order effects.

---

## 12. Evaluate Customer / Outcome Quality

No solo volume.

Revisar, cuando sea material:

- refunds;
- retention;
- complaints;
- support burden;
- margin.

---

## 13. Evaluate Remaining Headroom

Preguntar:

> ¿Cuánto upside queda?

---

## 14. Evaluate Marginal Economics

No decidir Scale con average economics.

---

## 15. Evaluate Scale Readiness

Revisar:

- inventory;
- operations;
- cash;
- creative;
- process;
- technical robustness.

---

## 16. Material Harm Branch

Si existe material harm:

- Stop;
- Rollback;
- Fix Forward;
- Mitigate;

según reversibility y prior state.

---

## 17. Positive Value Branch

Si value es positivo:

### Enough Evidence + Headroom + Readiness

Consider:

```text
SCALE
```

### Value positive but evidence limited

Consider:

```text
CONTINUE
```

### Value positive but no headroom

Consider:

```text
CONTINUE
```

or:

```text
STOP SCALING
```

---

## 18. Neutral / Mixed Branch

Puede justificar:

- Continue;
- Adjust;
- Gather Evidence;
- Hold.

---

## 19. Negative Branch

Si value es negativo:

### Reversible + Previous State Better

Consider:

```text
ROLLBACK
```

### Irreversible / Rollback worse

Consider:

```text
STOP
+
FIX FORWARD / MITIGATE
```

---

## 20. Stop Decision

Preguntar:

- has thesis failed?
- future value unattractive?
- opportunity cost too high?
- context changed?
- would more learning justify exposure?

---

## 21. Continue Decision

Preguntar:

- current performance acceptable?
- evidence incomplete?
- scale premature?
- stopping would destroy useful learning?
- constraints block expansion?

---

## 22. Scale Decision

Preguntar:

- do we trust effect?
- incremental?
- repeatable?
- headroom?
- marginal economics healthy?
- system can absorb?
- what breaks first?

---

## 23. Rollback Decision

Preguntar:

- current state materially worse?
- previous state known?
- rollback feasible?
- rollback safer than fix-forward?
- residual damage?

---

## 24. Partial Decisions

Permitir:

```text
Scale A
Hold B
Stop C
```

cuando effects son heterogeneous.

---

## 25. Avoid Globalizing Local Failure

Segmentar cuando causal context lo justifica.

---

## 26. Leading vs Lagging

No Scale agresivamente solo con early indicators si final outcome importa.

---

## 27. Respect Evaluation Window

No:

- premature stop;
- premature scale;
- permanent experimentation.

---

## 28. Apply Asymmetric Evidence Requirement

High downside puede justificar Stop con menos evidence.

Large Scale requires stronger evidence.

---

## 29. Adjust Before Stop

Si mechanism parece válido pero execution no:

- tune;
- fix;
- reduce scope;
- retry.

---

## 30. Stop Scaling vs Stop Intervention

Diferenciar explícitamente.

---

## 31. De-scale

Cuando marginal economics empeoran pero current intervention sigue valiosa:

```text
Reduce Exposure
```

---

## 32. Conditional Scale

Expresar:

```text
Scale
provided that
Guardrails remain intact
```

---

## 33. Pre-mortem

Antes de Scale:

> ¿Qué puede romperse primero?

---

## 34. Portfolio Reallocation

Si Scale consume resources, identificar de dónde salen.

Si Stop libera resources, identificar potential reallocation.

---

## 35. Rollback vs Fix Forward

Comparar:

```text
Cost of Rollback
+
Residual Damage
```

vs:

```text
Cost / Risk of Fix Forward
```

conceptualmente.

---

## 36. Check Intervention Type

No aplicar la misma semántica a:

- fix;
- experiment;
- optimization;
- structural change;
- scaling action.

---

## 37. Temporary Intervention Check

Preguntar si debe:

- continue;
- sunset;
- normalize.

No permanentizar por inercia.

---

## 38. BAU Graduation

Si improvement está validada y estable:

```text
Strategic Project
→ Standardized / BAU
```

---

## 39. Regime Change Check

Si environment cambió materialmente:

- reduce trust in old evidence;
- re-evaluate.

---

## 40. Seasonality Check

Puede existir:

```text
Seasonal Scale
→ Normalize
```

sin ser rollback.

---

## 41. Data Quality Check

Si measurement dudosa:

- do not scale aggressively;
- fix measurement if decision-critical;
- Continue / Pause when necessary.

---

## 42. Output Decision First

Primero:

```text
STOP / CONTINUE / SCALE / ROLLBACK
```

Después:

- why;
- why not alternatives;
- scope;
- limits;
- what to observe;
- what changes the decision.

---

## 43. Avoid Fake Precision

No emitir thresholds universales.

---

## 44. Dynamic Update Loop

Conceptualmente:

```text
Recommend
↓
Execute
↓
Observe
↓
Update Belief
↓
Stop / Continue / Scale / Rollback
↓
Observe Again
```

---

## 45. Handoff

La salida alimenta:

- 7.14 no-action/wait;
- 7.15 success criteria;
- 7.16 trade-offs;
- 7.17 confidence;
- Fase 8 execution/monitoring;
- Fase 9 learning.

---

## 46. Frontera operacional

Este runtime NO:

- monitors systems automatically;
- triggers rollback;
- changes budgets;
- adjusts prices;
- deploys code;
- pauses campaigns;
- allocates resources;
- changes inventory;
- sends alerts;
- implements state transitions technically.
