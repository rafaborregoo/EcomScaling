# FASE 7.14 — Runtime conceptual para No-action / Wait / Gather Evidence

## Propósito

Extrae la secuencia operacional implícita de 7.14.

No define un motor técnico de investigación, monitorización ni scheduling.

---

## 1. Entrada

Recibe:

- potential intervention;
- current performance;
- economics;
- problem evidence;
- timing;
- constraints;
- uncertainty;
- risk;
- reversibility;
- portfolio context.

---

## 2. Check Meaningful Problem / Opportunity

Preguntar:

> ¿Existe realmente un problema u oportunidad material?

Si no:

```text
NO-ACTION
```

---

## 3. Check Status Quo

Evaluar:

- current performance;
- stability;
- economics;
- downside of inaction.

---

## 4. Check Signal vs Noise

Preguntar:

> ¿El cambio observado es material y representativo?

Si parece noise:

```text
NO-ACTION / OBSERVE
```

---

## 5. Check Headroom / Marginal Value

Si additional improvement tiene low marginal value:

```text
NO-ACTION
```

y reallocate attention.

---

## 6. Check Timing Blocker

Preguntar:

> ¿La acción podría ser correcta, pero el timing aún no?

Si sí:

```text
WAIT
```

---

## 7. Identify What We Are Waiting For

Puede ser:

- cohort maturity;
- stabilization;
- stock arrival;
- supplier decision;
- regulatory event;
- another intervention outcome.

---

## 8. Check Known Resolution Point

Wait es más fuerte cuando existe una condición/evento concreto.

---

## 9. Check Cost of Delay

Si esperar destruye demasiado value:

- do not Wait by default;
- consider Act / Act Small.

---

## 10. Prepare While Waiting

Puede avanzar:

- planning;
- creative;
- readiness;
- data cleanup.

---

## 11. Check Decision-Critical Uncertainty

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué desconocemos que podría cambiar la recomendación?

---

## 12. If no decision-critical uncertainty

No Gather Evidence.

Decidir con lo conocido.

---

## 13. Define Decision Boundary

Especificar qué missing fact separa Action A de Action B.

---

## 14. Check Existing Evidence First

Antes de test nuevo:

> ¿Ya existe suficiente información?

---

## 15. Choose Cheapest Reliable Evidence Path

Puede ser:

- analytics;
- qualitative research;
- observation;
- customer feedback;
- targeted test;
- pilot.

---

## 16. Check Evidence Freshness

No usar datos obsoletos sin contextualización.

---

## 17. Check Evidence Relevance

No sobreponderar datos simplemente porque estén disponibles.

---

## 18. Match Evidence Standard to Decision

Considerar:

- commitment;
- downside;
- irreversibility;
- urgency;
- cost of delay.

---

## 19. Consider Act Small

Si uncertainty es alta pero acción es:

- cheap;
- reversible;
- informative;

puede superar Gather Evidence pasivo.

---

## 20. Consider Wait

Si información relevante llegará pronto naturalmente y delay cost es bajo.

---

## 21. Consider No-action

Si:

- expected upside low;
- downside high;
- opportunity cost high;
- status quo acceptable.

---

## 22. Avoid Analysis Paralysis

Stop researching when:

```text
Remaining uncertainty
would not materially change
the next decision
```

---

## 23. Avoid Premature Action

No actuar solo para “hacer algo”.

---

## 24. Preserve Interpretability

Si una intervención previa sigue generando evidence:

- wait;
- avoid contamination.

---

## 25. Consider Representative Data

No tomar one-off spike/drop como structural signal.

---

## 26. Consider Regime Change

Si contexto cambió materialmente:

- old evidence may lose relevance;
- return to learning mode locally.

---

## 27. Consider Optionality

Wait puede preservar options bajo irreversibility.

---

## 28. Consider Evidence Budget

No investigar low-value questions.

---

## 29. Prioritize High-Value Uncertainty

Aquella capaz de alterar una high-value decision.

---

## 30. Check Portfolio Focus

No-action puede proteger focus en primary bottleneck.

---

## 31. Distinguish Deferred vs Wait

Deferred:

- lower current priority.

Wait:

- timing not ready.

---

## 32. Distinguish Gather Evidence vs Monitor

Monitor:

- passive observation.

Gather Evidence:

- active information collection when needed.

---

## 33. Apply Decision Error Thinking

Considerar:

- false action;
- false wait;
- unnecessary research;
- false no-action.

---

## 34. Apply Robustness

Si information unavailable:

- reduce exposure;
- choose robust option;
- preserve reversibility.

---

## 35. Output Decision First

Debe concluir claramente:

```text
NO-ACTION
WAIT
GATHER EVIDENCE
ACT SMALL
ACT
```

conceptualmente.

---

## 36. Explain Why

Después indicar:

- rationale;
- what we know;
- what we don't know;
- what changes decision.

---

## 37. For Wait

Debe indicar:

- what are we waiting for;
- review condition;
- cost of delay;
- preparation possible.

---

## 38. For Gather Evidence

Debe indicar:

- exact uncertainty;
- evidence needed;
- cheapest reliable path;
- what decision follows.

---

## 39. For No-action

Debe indicar:

- why change is not justified;
- what to monitor;
- future trigger.

---

## 40. Handoff

La salida alimenta:

- 7.15 expected outcome / success criteria;
- 7.16 trade-offs;
- 7.17 confidence;
- Fase 8 execution/monitoring;
- Fase 9 learning.

---

## 41. Frontera operacional

Este runtime NO:

- runs experiments;
- monitors automatically;
- gathers customer research automatically;
- creates dashboards;
- schedules review;
- changes campaigns;
- executes changes;
- launches tools;
- automates evidence collection.
