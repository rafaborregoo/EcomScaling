# FASE 7.11 — Runtime conceptual de recomendación bajo restricciones

## Propósito

Este documento extrae el comportamiento operacional implícito de 7.11.

No define un solver matemático ni un resource-allocation engine.

---

## 1. Entrada

El flujo recibe:

- desired intervention;
- required resources;
- constraints;
- business context;
- evidence;
- expected impact;
- risk;
- dependencies;
- guardrails.

---

## 2. Identificar resource requirements

Preguntar qué necesita la intervención en:

- cash;
- people;
- time;
- technology;
- creative;
- operations;
- inventory;
- data;
- attention;
- external capacity.

---

## 3. Discover constraints before recommendation final

No decidir primero y descubrir restricciones después.

---

## 4. Classify hard vs soft

### Hard

No puede ejecutarse ahora.

### Soft

Puede adaptarse.

---

## 5. Determine temporary vs structural

Temporary:

- wait;
- prepare;
- reduce;
- reallocate.

Structural:

- change capability;
- change economics;
- redesign system.

---

## 6. Determine binding constraint

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué constraint limita realmente la acción de mayor valor?

---

## 7. Check constraint slack/headroom

No limitar innecesariamente si existe suficiente margen.

---

## 8. Challenge vague constraints

Convertir:

```text
“No resources”
```

en algo específico.

Ejemplo:

```text
No developer capacity for 4 weeks
```

---

## 9. Distinguish real constraint from assumption

Preguntar:

- fixed?
- negotiable?
- expandable?
- temporary?
- assumed?

---

## 10. Distinguish scarcity from misallocation

Antes de pedir más resources:

- identify low-value uses;
- waste;
- reallocatable capacity.

---

## 11. Evaluate success feasibility

Preguntar:

> Si la intervención funciona, ¿el sistema puede absorberla?

---

## 12. Check cash path

No mirar solo final profitability.

Evaluar conceptualmente:

```text
Cash Out
→ Delay
→ Cash In
```

---

## 13. Check working capital

Growth puede aumentar cash need antes de producir liquidez.

---

## 14. Check people capacity

Evaluar:

```text
Skill
+
Availability
+
Ownership
+
Execution Quality
```

---

## 15. Check management attention

Especialmente si la intervención es cross-functional.

---

## 16. Check time feasibility

Distinguir:

- implementation;
- impact;
- learning;
- deadline;
- execution window.

---

## 17. Check technical feasibility

No asumir que simple business idea implica simple implementation.

---

## 18. Check creative capacity

No scale paid cuando creative throughput es binding.

---

## 19. Check operational capacity

No generar demand beyond fulfilment capacity.

---

## 20. Check inventory constraint

Evaluar:

- stock;
- demand;
- replenishment;
- SKU-specific constraints.

---

## 21. Check data constraint

Preguntar:

> ¿Missing data blocks decision or only lowers confidence?

---

## 22. Check legal/compliance

Una acción comercialmente atractiva puede requerir adaptación o veto.

---

## 23. Check channel capacity

No extrapolar performance linealmente.

---

## 24. Check market ceiling

No recomendar infinite growth en small market.

---

## 25. Check brand/CX constraints

No destruir positioning o service quality.

---

## 26. Check constraint interactions

Buscar combinaciones que multiplican fricción.

---

## 27. Decide if constraint should be removed

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué valuable actions unlocks removing it?

---

## 28. If removable and high-unlock-value

Puede recomendar:

```text
Remove Constraint First
```

---

## 29. If not removable, reduce intervention

Opciones:

- narrower scope;
- lower intensity;
- slower velocity;
- shorter duration.

---

## 30. If mechanism can be substituted

Buscar alternative tactic manteniendo:

```text
Cause
→ Lever
→ Mechanism
```

---

## 31. If constraint will disappear soon

Puede ser correcto:

```text
Delay Execution
+
Prepare Now
```

---

## 32. If resources are misallocated

Reallocate before asking for more.

---

## 33. Compute conceptually resource productivity

Preguntar:

> ¿Dónde produce más value la próxima unidad del recurso escaso?

---

## 34. Apply survival override

Si constraint amenaza runway/survival:

- preservation may dominate growth.

---

## 35. Apply constraint severity to portfolio breadth

Más severidad:

- fewer simultaneous actions;
- higher selectivity.

---

## 36. Localize constraints

No globalizar una limitación local.

Ejemplo:

```text
UK stock constrained
≠
stop EU growth
```

---

## 37. Consider resource substitution

Ejemplo:

```text
Low engineering
→ temporary manual process
```

si no destruye system capacity.

---

## 38. Check hidden cost of substitution

No mover constraint sin reconocerlo.

---

## 39. Avoid constraint whack-a-mole

Preguntar:

> ¿Aumenta la capacidad total del sistema?

---

## 40. Identify secondary/latent constraint

¿Qué aparece después si el primary constraint desaparece?

---

## 41. Avoid premature future-bottleneck solving

Monitor and prepare.

No overbuild.

---

## 42. Maintain buffers where justified

No operar critical resources a 100 % by default.

---

## 43. Evaluate forward-looking feasibility

Una intervención viable hoy puede dejar de serlo pronto.

---

## 44. Adapt recommendation when constraint changes

Constraints son dinámicas.

---

## 45. Produce best feasible action

Salida debe explicar:

- desired intervention;
- binding constraint;
- adaptation;
- feasible action now;
- deferred action;
- activation trigger;
- next constraint.

---

## 46. Adjust expected outcome

Si scope o intensity se reducen:

- reduce expected result accordingly.

---

## 47. Distinguish validity from feasibility confidence

Una recomendación puede ser causalmente fuerte y operacionalmente incierta.

---

## 48. Hand-off

La salida alimenta:

- 7.12 portfolio;
- 7.13 progression;
- 7.14 no-action;
- 7.16 trade-offs;
- 7.17 confidence;
- Fase 8 execution.

---

## 49. Frontera operacional

Este runtime NO:

- asigna recursos;
- ejecuta hiring;
- cambia budgets;
- compra inventory;
- crea contracts;
- modifica org structure;
- negocia suppliers;
- ejecuta tooling changes;
- implementa scheduler;
- resuelve portfolio matemáticamente.
