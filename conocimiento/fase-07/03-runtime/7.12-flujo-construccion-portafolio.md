# FASE 7.12 — Runtime conceptual de construcción de portafolio

## Propósito

Este documento extrae el flujo operacional implícito de 7.12.

No define un portfolio optimizer ni un resource scheduler.

---

## 1. Entrada

El flujo recibe:

- prioritized problems;
- candidate interventions;
- causal relationships;
- dependencies;
- evidence/confidence;
- constraints;
- maturity;
- resources;
- risk;
- existing initiatives.

---

## 2. Identify dominant business priority

Primero:

> What matters most now?

No empezar por departments.

---

## 3. Select Primary

Elegir la intervención con mayor leverage sobre la priority actual.

Debe existir conexión:

```text
Priority
→ Driver
→ Cause
→ Lever
→ Intervention
```

---

## 4. Ensure Primary is truly dominant

Si aparecen demasiadas “primary”:

- re-prioritize;
- consolidate;
- separate objectives.

---

## 5. Identify Enablers

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué debe estar resuelto para que Primary funcione?

Añadir solo minimum necessary enablers.

---

## 6. Avoid Enabling Debt

No construir capability infinita antes de value delivery.

---

## 7. Identify Supporting Actions

Añadir solo las que:

- materially improve Primary impact;
- increase speed;
- improve quality;
- improve sustainability.

---

## 8. Identify Parallel Actions

Permitirlas cuando:

- resource pools distinct;
- low coordination;
- no learning interference;
- no dependency;
- acceptable risk.

---

## 9. Identify Deferred Actions

Mover fuera del active portfolio acciones válidas pero:

- lower marginal value;
- dependent;
- resource constrained;
- low evidence;
- wrong timing.

---

## 10. Explicitly Exclude

Definir qué no se hará ahora.

---

## 11. Deduplicate

Detectar:

```text
Different Labels
→ Same Mechanism
```

y consolidar.

---

## 12. Cluster Supporting Tactics

No elevar microtácticas a strategic interventions.

---

## 13. Check Intervention Granularity

Evitar:

- too abstract;
- too fragmented.

---

## 14. Check Complementarity

Preguntar si A y B realmente se potencian.

No asumir synergy.

---

## 15. Check Conflict / Interference

Si dos acciones:

- alter same KPI;
- contaminate learning;
- compete for audience;
- compete for inventory;

puede ser necesario secuenciar.

---

## 16. Check Execution Parallelism vs Learning Parallelism

Puede existir operational parallelism sin causal compatibility.

---

## 17. Aggregate Resource Load

Sumar conceptualmente demands sobre:

- cash;
- people;
- developers;
- creatives;
- attention;
- inventory;
- external capacity.

---

## 18. Validate Portfolio Feasibility

No basta:

```text
A feasible
B feasible
C feasible
```

Debe comprobarse:

```text
A + B + C feasible together?
```

---

## 19. Apply WIP Limit

Respetar finite execution capacity.

Sin número universal.

---

## 20. Check Coordination Tax

Más iniciativas aumentan complexity no lineal.

---

## 21. Check Portfolio Dilution

Preguntar si cada acción recibe minimum effective allocation.

---

## 22. Check Management Attention

Aunque teams sean independientes, leadership puede ser shared constraint.

---

## 23. Check Existing WIP

Antes de añadir:

> What is already underway?

---

## 24. Consider Finish vs Start

Puede ser mejor terminar un proyecto casi completo.

Pero evitar sunk-cost fallacy.

Comparar conceptualmente:

```text
Remaining Cost
vs
Remaining Value
```

---

## 25. Check Portfolio Entry Criteria

Una nueva acción debe justificar su:

- resource cost;
- risk;
- coordination cost;
- opportunity cost.

---

## 26. Check Marginal Portfolio Value

Preguntar:

> ¿Añadir esta recomendación incrementa suficiente total expected value?

---

## 27. Stop Adding When Marginal Complexity > Marginal Value

Aplicar simplicity premium.

---

## 28. Check Strategic Theme

La cartera debe contar una historia comprensible.

---

## 29. Check Dominant Objective

Si hay objetivos múltiples, determinar si:

- independent;
- conflicting;
- diluting focus.

---

## 30. Check Correlated Risk

Detectar shared assumptions.

---

## 31. Check Risk Stacking

Evitar múltiples irreversible/high-downside changes simultáneos sin razón.

---

## 32. Check Portfolio Blast Radius

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué ocurre si varias acciones fallan juntas?

---

## 33. Mix Reversibility when useful

Combinar:

- core commitment;
- reversible experiments;

puede reducir downside.

---

## 34. Weight by Evidence

Conceptualmente:

```text
Strong Evidence → Commit
Moderate Evidence → Pilot
Low Evidence → Learn
```

---

## 35. Avoid Speculation-Dominated Portfolio

Known defects y high-confidence actions deberían desplazar five uncertain bets.

---

## 36. Build Sequence

Definir:

- what first;
- what together;
- what later.

---

## 37. Build Conditional Branches

No activar todas.

Ejemplo:

```text
Primary
→ Observe
→ If X: Scale
→ If not X: Diagnose
```

---

## 38. Check Seasonal Timing

Ajustar entry según:

- pre-peak;
- peak;
- post-peak;
- launches.

---

## 39. Protect Strategic Work

Si todo capacity está en urgencias, considerar reservar espacio para capability building cuando maturity lo permita.

---

## 40. Balance Explore vs Exploit

Según stage/context.

---

## 41. Check Economic Coherence

No aceptar portfolio revenue-positive pero margin/cash-negative sin entender trade-off.

---

## 42. Check Local Metric Conflicts

Evaluar business outcome final.

No solo departmental KPI.

---

## 43. Check Cross-functional Dependencies

One priority can involve multiple teams.

No convertir cada function en priority.

---

## 44. Check Ownership

Toda intervención activa debe tener clear accountability conceptualmente.

---

## 45. Check External Dependencies

Vendor/platform/supplier concentration puede generar portfolio fragility.

---

## 46. Check Optionality

Evitar lock-in innecesario bajo uncertainty.

---

## 47. Produce Final Portfolio

Salida conceptual:

- Primary;
- Enabling;
- Supporting;
- Parallel;
- Deferred;
- Explicitly Not Now.

Solo incluir categorías necesarias.

---

## 48. Produce Portfolio Narrative

Explicar:

- main problem;
- why this Primary;
- what unlocks it;
- what supports it;
- what can run safely in parallel;
- what is deferred;
- what is excluded.

---

## 49. Produce Quality Check

La cartera debe quedar:

- focused;
- coherent;
- feasible;
- causal;
- sequenced;
- observable;
- adaptable.

---

## 50. Dynamic Refresh

Revisar cuando cambien materialmente:

- priority;
- constraints;
- evidence;
- results;
- capacity;
- risk.

No reaccionar a daily noise.

---

## 51. Avoid Portfolio Thrashing

Necesita continuidad suficiente para mostrar mechanism.

---

## 52. Avoid Portfolio Inertia

No mantener action por sunk cost o hábito.

---

## 53. Hand-off

La salida alimenta:

- 7.13 stop/continue/scale/rollback;
- 7.14 no-action;
- 7.15 success criteria;
- 7.16 trade-offs;
- 7.17 confidence;
- Fase 8 execution.

---

## 54. Frontera operacional

Este runtime NO:

- asigna budgets;
- asigna owners;
- crea Jira tasks;
- ejecuta actions;
- calcula optimal portfolio matemáticamente;
- fija WIP limit;
- resuelve resource allocation automáticamente;
- implementa project management.
