### Wait

- RAG: timing logic.
- Contract: wait reason/review condition.
- Runtime: known resolution branch.
- Policies: no open-ended wait.
- Experience: wait outcomes.
- Tenant Context: upcoming events/latency.

### Gather Evidence

- RAG: decision-critical uncertainty reduction.
- Contract: question/evidence/completion.
- Runtime: evidence path selection.
- Policies: no research for its own sake.
- Experience: evidence value.
- Tenant Context: available data/research capacity.

### Cost of Delay

- RAG: counterweight to waiting.
- Contract: decision input.
- Runtime: urgency branch.
- Policies: cannot be ignored.
- Experience: historical lost opportunity.
- Tenant Context: current opportunity window.

### Decision Sufficiency

- RAG: enough information for next commitment.
- Contract: completion condition.
- Runtime: stop researching.
- Policies: perfect certainty prohibited.
- Experience: calibration.
