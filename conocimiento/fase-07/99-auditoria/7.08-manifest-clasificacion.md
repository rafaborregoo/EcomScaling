## 3. Conceptos clasificados en varias capas

### Fix vs Test

- RAG: distinction.
- Contract: future decision output.
- Runtime: first branch.
- Policies: do not test confirmed defects.
- Experience: outcomes of wrong mode.
- Tenant Context: defect/evidence reality.

### Value of Information

- RAG: compare learning value vs test cost.
- Contract: input/output requirement.
- Runtime: decides if test is worth it.
- Policies: experiment only if decision changes.
- Experience: whether learning changed action.
- Tenant Context: cost/resources/traffic.

### Experimentability

- RAG: conceptual property.
- Contract: information requirement.
- Runtime: can we measure and limit exposure?
- Tenant Context: tooling, volume, segmentation.

### Guardrails

- RAG: primary vs guardrail outcomes.
- Contract: needed outputs.
- Runtime: pre-test check and result interpretation.
- Policies: strong safety rules.
- Tenant Context: concrete limits.

### Test-to-Scale

- RAG: scale is separate decision.
- Runtime: handoff after evidence.
- Policies: no linear extrapolation.
- Experience: pilot-to-scale outcomes.

### Execute-and-Monitor

- RAG: valid alternative to formal testing.
- Runtime: selected when enough evidence + low downside.
- Policies: not causal proof.
- Experience: can reveal whether this mode was sufficient.

## 8. Riesgos arquitectónicos detectados

### Riesgo 1 — convertir todo en A/B test

La fuente lo rechaza explícitamente.

### Riesgo 2 — convertir todo en direct execution

También se rechaza.

### Riesgo 3 — confundir monitoring con experiment

Execute-and-monitor no es causal experiment.

### Riesgo 4 — construir un motor estadístico demasiado pronto

No corresponde a 7.8.

### Riesgo 5 — hacer test de bugs conocidos

Puede ser dañino e innecesario.

### Riesgo 6 — meter traffic/tooling real en RAG

Pertenece a tenant context.

### Riesgo 7 — tratar statistical significance como business value

La materialidad económica es distinta.

### Riesgo 8 — extrapolar pilot linealmente

Scale es otra decisión.

### Riesgo 9 — experimentalism bias

No todo lo importante cabe en un A/B.

### Riesgo 10 — research theater

Experimento sin decisión posterior tiene bajo valor.
