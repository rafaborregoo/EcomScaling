# FASE 7.18 — Contexto del tenant para comunicación de recomendaciones

## Propósito

La comunicación debe reflejar el estado real del ecommerce y del usuario, sin alterar la calidad del reasoning.

No contiene valores concretos.

---

## 1. Current Business Priority

Determina prominencia de la recommendation.

---

## 2. Current Bottleneck

Ayuda a explicar Why Now.

---

## 3. Current Constraints

Permiten comunicar best feasible intervention.

---

## 4. Current Maturity

Afecta lenguaje sobre:

- validation;
- learning;
- marginal economics;
- incrementality.

---

## 5. Current Portfolio

Necesario para comunicar:

- Primary;
- Deferred;
- What Not To Do.

---

## 6. Current Active Initiatives

Evita Recommendation Creep.

---

## 7. Current Resource Constraints

Puede incluir:

- cash;
- people;
- developers;
- creative;
- inventory;
- operations;
- management attention.

---

## 8. Current Risk State

Afecta explanation depth y guardrail prominence.

---

## 9. Current Confidence

Afecta wording y exposure recommendation.

---

## 10. Current Evidence Quality

Afecta strength of claim.

---

## 11. Current Decision-Critical Uncertainty

Debe guiar main uncertainty.

---

## 12. Expected Outcome

Debe comunicarse con el scope correcto.

---

## 13. Success Criteria

Determina What to Observe.

---

## 14. Current Trade-Off

Debe comunicarse cuando pueda generar metric conflict.

---

## 15. Current Guardrails

Hard guardrails requieren lenguaje claro.

---

## 16. Current Sequencing

Ayuda a explicar:

- First;
- Then;
- Later.

---

## 17. Current Dependencies

No deben ocultarse.

---

## 18. Temporary vs Structural Context

Afecta si recommendation es:

- containment;
- root fix.

---

## 19. User Expertise

Puede ser:

- executive;
- specialist;
- less technical.

Afecta terminology y detail.

No reasoning quality.

---

## 20. User Need

Puede requerir:

- executive summary;
- detailed rationale.

---

## 21. Business Model

Ayuda a contextualizar:

- retention;
- frequency;
- margin;
- cash;
- scaling logic.

---

## 22. Category Context

Puede cambiar expected behavior de metrics.

---

## 23. Channel Context

Puede cambiar language about causality/incrementality.

---

## 24. Market Context

Puede limitar transferability claims.

---

## 25. Segment Context

Puede requerir scope-bounded recommendation.

---

## 26. Current Scale

Puede afectar advice on Scale / Hold / Stop.

---

## 27. Current Cash Position

Puede hacer cash constraint explicit.

---

## 28. Current Inventory

Puede hacer demand scaling inappropriate.

---

## 29. Current Operational Capacity

Puede limitar scope.

---

## 30. Current Technical Capacity

Puede justificar simpler feasible option.

---

## 31. Current Data Quality

Puede limitar certainty language.

---

## 32. Current Time / Opportunity Window

Puede justificar Why Now.

---

## 33. Current Seasonality

Puede alterar interpretation.

---

## 34. Current User Decision Horizon

Puede ser:

- immediate;
- near-term;
- strategic.

---

## 35. Current Plan Tier

Growth / Scale puede existir como commercial context.

Pero:

```text
Plan Tier
must not alter
Reasoning Quality
```

---

## 36. Future Feature Availability

Puede diferir por plan en el futuro.

Debe permanecer separada de intelligence depth/quality.

---

## 37. Contexto no definido

No se definen:

- UI preferences;
- exact verbosity levels;
- response-format fields;
- tone presets;
- localization schema;
- plan feature matrix;
- communication personalization engine.
