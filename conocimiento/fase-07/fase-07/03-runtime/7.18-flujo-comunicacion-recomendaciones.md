# FASE 7.18 — Runtime conceptual de comunicación de recomendaciones

## Propósito

Extrae el flujo operacional implícito de 7.18.

No define UI, rendering engine ni execution layer.

---

## 1. Entrada

Recibe:

- diagnosis;
- priority;
- candidate recommendation;
- portfolio role;
- constraints;
- evidence;
- trade-offs;
- confidence;
- expected outcome;
- success criteria;
- sequencing;
- next decision.

---

## 2. Identify Main Recommendation

Preguntar:

> What should the user do first?

---

## 3. Identify Main Reason

Conectar recommendation con:

```text
Problem
→ Cause
→ Lever
→ Intervention
```

---

## 4. Identify Why Now

Explicar timing a partir de:

- current bottleneck;
- constraint;
- opportunity window;
- maturity;
- sequence.

---

## 5. Identify What Not To Do

Eliminar:

- distractions;
- premature sophistication;
- competing actions;
- low-value work.

---

## 6. Determine Portfolio Hierarchy

Si existen varias acciones:

- Primary;
- Enabling;
- Supporting;
- Parallel;
- Deferred.

No mostrar taxonomía si no mejora claridad.

---

## 7. Compress Internal Complexity

No trasladar al usuario todas las hypotheses/alternatives.

---

## 8. Select Decision-Relevant Evidence

Elegir:

- strongest evidence;
- meaningful contradiction;
- relevant context.

---

## 9. Distinguish Fact vs Inference

No presentar causal inference como observation.

---

## 10. Select Main Uncertainty

Preguntar:

> Which uncertainty could actually change the recommendation?

---

## 11. Translate Confidence into Recommendation Form

High confidence:

- more direct language.

Moderate:

- limited exposure / validation.

Low:

- gather evidence / pilot / wait.

---

## 12. Define Expected Outcome

Comunicar qué debería ocurrir if mechanism is correct.

---

## 13. Define Success Signal

Seleccionar pocas señales que cambian next decision.

---

## 14. Define Main Risk / Trade-Off

No listar todos.

---

## 15. Define Guardrail

Si existe un non-negotiable o constraint material, hacerlo explícito.

---

## 16. Define Invalidating Condition

Qué outcome haría cambiar de dirección.

---

## 17. Define Next Decision

Solo one-step-ahead.

---

## 18. Determine Appropriate Scope of Output

Simple problem:

- compact.

Complex/high-risk problem:

- more structured.

---

## 19. Adapt Language to User Sophistication

Simplificar wording, no reasoning.

---

## 20. Adapt Depth, Not Intelligence

Executive vs specialist cambia presentation depth.

No cambia decision quality.

---

## 21. Check Plan-Tier Neutrality

Growth y Scale deben recibir el mismo reasoning quality.

---

## 22. Remove Recommendation Creep

Eliminar recomendaciones añadidas sin función clara.

---

## 23. Remove Framework Dump

Internal labels solo si aportan claridad.

---

## 24. Remove KPI Dump

Mantener solo decision-relevant signals.

---

## 25. Remove Jargon

Mantener technical terms solo si mejoran precisión.

---

## 26. Remove False Precision

No inventar:

- uplift;
- ROI;
- exact timeline;
- thresholds.

---

## 27. Check Sequence

Si B precede A:

> First B, then A.

---

## 28. Check Scope

Si recommendation solo aplica a:

- segment;
- market;
- SKU;
- channel;

hacerlo explícito.

---

## 29. Check Decision Stage

Comunicar correctamente si se trata de:

- test;
- pilot;
- execution;
- scale;
- stop;
- rollback;
- no-action;
- wait;
- gather evidence.

---

## 30. Check Temporary vs Structural

No presentar containment como permanent strategy.

---

## 31. Check Next Observation

Preguntar:

> What matters next?

---

## 32. Produce Recommendation First

La salida debe permitir identificar main action en segundos.

---

## 33. Add Reasoning Second

Just enough to understand causality.

---

## 34. Add Evidence Third

Solo lo necesario.

---

## 35. Add Risk / Trade-Off

Cuando material.

---

## 36. Add Next Step

Cuando decision is conditional.

---

## 37. Quality Check

Verificar:

- main recommendation clear?
- why clear?
- why now clear?
- what not to do clear?
- expected outcome clear?
- main risk clear?
- what to observe clear?
- next decision clear?

---

## 38. Handoff

La output alimenta:

- user interaction;
- Fase 8 execution;
- later monitoring;
- learning capture.

---

## 39. Frontera operacional

Este runtime NO:

- executes actions;
- changes budgets;
- edits campaigns;
- schedules projects;
- assigns owners;
- renders UI;
- sends notifications;
- creates dashboards;
- performs API calls.

Solo transforma reasoning en decision-support communication.
