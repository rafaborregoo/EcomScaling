# FASE 7.15 — Runtime conceptual de Expected Outcome y Success Criteria

## Propósito

Extrae el flujo operacional implícito de 7.15.

No implementa monitoring, experiment platform ni automatic evaluation.

---

## 1. Entrada

Recibe:

- recommendation;
- cause;
- mechanism;
- intervention type;
- baseline;
- business objective;
- maturity;
- constraints;
- risk;
- resources;
- time horizon.

---

## 2. Build Causal Thesis

Formular:

```text
If we do X
because Y is true,
we expect Z.
```

---

## 3. Identify Immediate Mechanism Outcome

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué debería cambiar primero?

---

## 4. Identify Behavior / System Change

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué behavior o system state debería cambiar después?

---

## 5. Identify Business Outcome

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué value debería producir?

Puede ser:

- economic;
- strategic;
- defensive;
- capability;
- learning.

---

## 6. Determine Outcome Hierarchy

No exigir todas las capas.

Usar solo las necesarias.

---

## 7. Determine Outcome Direction

Especificar qué debería:

- improve;
- stabilize;
- decline intentionally;
- be prevented.

---

## 8. Determine Magnitude only if supported

Si no existe basis:

- do not fabricate numeric uplift.

---

## 9. Determine Time Horizon

Puede existir:

- immediate;
- intermediate;
- delayed.

---

## 10. Determine Baseline / Counterfactual

Preguntar:

> Improvement relative to what?

---

## 11. Select Primary Success Criterion

Debe representar el main desired value.

---

## 12. Select Guardrails

Qué no debe deteriorarse inaceptablemente.

---

## 13. Check Materiality

Preguntar:

> Would this improvement be worth the intervention?

---

## 14. Check Incrementality

Especialmente para growth/promotions/channels.

---

## 15. Check Economics

Según intervention:

- margin;
- contribution;
- cash;
- payback;
- LTV.

---

## 16. Check Trade-Offs

No exigir que todo mejore.

Definir acceptable sacrifice conceptualmente.

---

## 17. Check Risk

¿El additional value justifica additional risk?

---

## 18. Check Sustainability

¿Puede mantenerse bajo normal operating conditions?

---

## 19. Check Repeatability

¿Puede repetirse sin heroics?

---

## 20. Check Scalability

¿Success sigue siendo attractive a mayor exposure?

---

## 21. Check Durability

¿Es temporary lift o structural improvement?

---

## 22. Check Maintenance Cost

¿Ongoing value justifica upkeep?

---

## 23. Check Fidelity / Adoption

Para processes/tools:

- was intervention actually implemented?
- was it adopted?
- did adoption create value?

---

## 24. Define Failure Evidence

Qué outcome contradiría causal thesis.

---

## 25. Define Ambiguous Evidence

Qué resultado no permitiría concluir aún.

---

## 26. Define Decision Consequences

### Strong success

Potential Scale / Continue / Graduate.

### Partial success

May reveal next constraint.

### Inconclusive

Continue / Gather Evidence / Stop if learning cost too high.

### Failure

Stop / Adjust.

### Harmful

Rollback / Stop / Mitigate.

---

## 27. Check Intervention Type

No aplicar mismo success model a:

- defect;
- experiment;
- pilot;
- structural change;
- scaling;
- capability.

---

## 28. Check Pilot vs Scale Readiness

No confundir contained success con scalable success.

---

## 29. Check Scope Heterogeneity

Puede haber outcomes distintos por segment/channel/SKU.

---

## 30. Avoid Average Masking

Segmentar solo cuando contextual differences importan.

---

## 31. Check Pull-Forward / Cannibalization

Cuando relevant.

---

## 32. Check Gross vs Net Lift

No valorar solo gross effect.

---

## 33. Check External Factors

Considerar context changes sin usarlos como universal excuse.

---

## 34. Precommit Criteria When Practical

Antes de result:

- success;
- failure;
- inconclusive.

---

## 35. Record Unexpected Outcomes Separately

No reescribir original thesis.

---

## 36. Prevent Goalpost Moving

Criteria revision requiere contextual justification.

---

## 37. Check Opportunity Cost After Success

Successful intervention may no longer be highest priority.

---

## 38. Check Graduation / Good Enough

Preguntar:

> Is this sufficiently solved for now?

---

## 39. Portfolio-Level Check

Avoid double-counting across interventions.

---

## 40. Constraint-Adjust Expected Outcome

Reduced scope → reduced expectation.

---

## 41. Maturity-Adjust Success Logic

Early may emphasize learning.

Scaling may emphasize marginal economics.

Mature may emphasize allocation/resilience.

---

## 42. Output

Debe producir conceptualmente:

- expected outcome;
- mechanism outcome;
- business outcome;
- horizon;
- primary success criterion;
- guardrails;
- failure evidence;
- ambiguous evidence;
- decision implications.

---

## 43. Handoff

La salida alimenta:

- 7.13 Stop / Continue / Scale / Rollback;
- 7.16 trade-offs;
- 7.17 confidence;
- Fase 8 monitoring/execution;
- Fase 9 learning.

---

## 44. Frontera operacional

Este runtime NO:

- calculates thresholds;
- monitors KPIs;
- runs tests;
- creates dashboards;
- emits alerts;
- triggers actions;
- performs rollback/scale;
- stores metrics.
