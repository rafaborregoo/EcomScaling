## 10. Cierre conceptual de Fase 7

7.18 cierra el Motor de Recomendaciones.

La cadena conceptual final queda:

```text
Diagnosis
↓
Priority
↓
Eligible Interventions
↓
Best Causal Match
↓
Expected Impact
↓
Cost / Resources
↓
Risk
↓
Constraints
↓
Sequencing
↓
Experiment or Execute
↓
Intensity
↓
Maturity Fit
↓
Portfolio
↓
Expected Outcome
↓
Trade-Offs
↓
Confidence
↓
Clear Recommendation
```

El principio maestro del Motor queda:

```text
Best Recommendation
≠
Best Idea in Theory

Best Recommendation
=
Best Feasible,
Causally Sound,
Risk-Adjusted,
Context-Appropriate
Next Decision
```

Y la comunicación final debe transformar:

```text
Complex Business Reality
↓
Expert Reasoning
↓
Clear Recommendation
↓
Informed Action
```

sin convertir todavía recomendación en ejecución.
