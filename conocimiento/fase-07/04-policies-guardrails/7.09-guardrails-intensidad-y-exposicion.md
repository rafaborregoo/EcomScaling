# FASE 7.9 — Policies y guardrails de intensidad y exposición

## Propósito

Estas reglas evitan que una intervención correcta se vuelva mala por exceso, defecto o velocidad inadecuada.

---

## 1. Intervention ≠ Intensity

No confundir dirección con tamaño.

---

## 2. Bigger ≠ Better

Más intensidad no implica más valor.

---

## 3. Smaller ≠ Safer if useless

Una intervención tan pequeña que no puede aprender o impactar no es válida.

---

## 4. No universal percentages

No recomendar:

```text
always +20%
```

sin contexto.

---

## 5. No universal caps

Los límites dependen del negocio.

---

## 6. No universal ramps

No hardcodear escalones.

---

## 7. Minimum Effective Exposure

La intensidad debe ser suficiente para aprender o importar.

---

## 8. Avoid Underpowered Intervention

No declarar failure cuando el commitment era insuficiente.

---

## 9. Avoid Massive Test Before Validation

No asumir full blast radius cuando puede limitarse.

---

## 10. Evidence-gated Scale

Más exposure debe ganarse con evidencia.

---

## 11. Do not scale on Average ROAS alone

Usar marginal economics.

---

## 12. Average ≠ Marginal

Regla fuerte.

---

## 13. Do not extrapolate linearly

Un resultado local no valida intensidades muy superiores.

---

## 14. Local Validity

El test valida el rango probado.

---

## 15. Scope as Risk Control

Limitar exposure cuando sea posible.

---

## 16. System Capacity limits Intensity

No escalar más allá de:

- cash;
- inventory;
- operations;
- team;
- CX.

---

## 17. Success Capacity required

No crear más demanda de la que el sistema puede absorber.

---

## 18. Guardrail-limited Scale

Si un guardrail material se deteriora:

- slow;
- maintain;
- reduce;
- stop increasing.

---

## 19. Targeted Intensity preferred when problem is concentrated

Evitar global intervention bias.

---

## 20. Severity does not cancel uncertainty

Large problem + weak diagnosis no justifica automáticamente large intervention.

---

## 21. Active Damage can justify strong action

Cuando defect/cause está confirmado.

---

## 22. Greater Downside → Lower Initial Commitment

Principio fuerte.

---

## 23. Greater Cost of Error → Lower Initial Commitment

---

## 24. Greater Reversibility → Greater Tolerance

Sin ignorar cumulative effect.

---

## 25. Poor Observability → Smaller Scope

---

## 26. Fast Feedback permits faster ramp

---

## 27. Slow Feedback requires patience

No stackear cambios antes de learning.

---

## 28. Scale Readiness is multidimensional

No single KPI.

---

## 29. Do not over-cut

Cost reduction puede destruir essential capability.

---

## 30. Cash-rich ≠ permission to overspend

---

## 31. Long Lead Time ≠ full early commitment

Puede usarse staged commitment.

---

## 32. Do not replatform to solve microproblem

---

## 33. Do not patch structural failure forever

---

## 34. Scope should match root cause

---

## 35. Magnitude should match problem magnitude and confidence

---

## 36. Do not automate whole organization at once

Empezar donde volume high + risk low.

---

## 37. Greater AI Autonomy = Greater Blast Radius

La autonomía debe crecer progresivamente cuando corresponda.

---

## 38. Avoid change saturation

La organización tiene limited change capacity.

---

## 39. Sustainable intensity matters

No diseñar steady-state basado en heroic effort.

---

## 40. Peak intensity ≠ Sustainable intensity

---

## 41. More complexity may grow nonlinearly

No añadir scope sin necesidad.

---

## 42. Preserve optionality under uncertainty

---

## 43. Larger commitment increases lock-in

---

## 44. Strong internal evidence > external benchmark for commitment

External-only evidence debe justificar initial exposure más prudente.

---

## 45. Correlational signal alone should not justify aggressive scale

---

## 46. Mixed signals → smaller action or more evidence

---

## 47. Avoid peanut-butter spreading

No repartir recursos débilmente entre todas las causas posibles.

---

## 48. Dominant driver deserves disproportionate focus

---

## 49. Bridge solutions should not be overbuilt

---

## 50. Aggregate exposure matters

Varias acciones moderadas pueden sumar demasiado change load.

---

## 51. Do not ignore cannibalization

---

## 52. Incrementality should guide scale

---

## 53. Saturation should reduce intensity or shift lever

---

## 54. Reallocation is valid

No toda decisión de scale exige mayor spend total.

---

## 55. Same percentage ≠ same risk

Absolute business size matters.

---

## 56. Thin margin reduces safe intensity

---

## 57. High LTV may support greater CAC exposure only if validated

---

## 58. Growth intensity must respect cash conversion cycle

---

## 59. Delayed returns/costs should limit early ramp

---

## 60. Do not wait for perfect lifetime data

Use sufficient proxies/historical evidence.

---

## 61. Duration matters

Temporary y permanent no son equivalentes.

---

## 62. Frequency creates cumulative intensity

Pequeñas acciones repetidas pueden cambiar posicionamiento o expectativas.

---

## 63. Step Size + Frequency determines Velocity

No gestionar una sin la otra.

---

## 64. Do not stack increases too fast

Preservar observability.

---

## 65. Avoid under-scaling validated winners

Over-conservatism también destruye valor.

---

## 66. Scale what works within marginal guardrails

---

## 67. Intensity is bidirectional

Reducir también es una recomendación válida.

---

## 68. No set-and-forget

Re-evaluate after material changes.

---

## 69. No full autonomy from intensity recommendation alone

7.9 no autoriza ejecución.

---

## 70. Frontera de autonomía

Este bloque no autoriza:

- changing spend;
- changing prices;
- buying inventory;
- changing communication frequency;
- deploying automation;
- expanding markets;
- increasing AI autonomy.

Solo determina conceptualmente el tamaño razonable de la apuesta.
