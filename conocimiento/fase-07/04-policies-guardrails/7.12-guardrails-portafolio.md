# FASE 7.12 — Policies y guardrails de portafolio

## Propósito

Estas reglas evitan que el Motor convierta múltiples buenas ideas en una cartera incoherente o imposible de ejecutar.

---

## 1. Valid Action ≠ Portfolio Action

---

## 2. More Recommendations ≠ Better Recommendation

---

## 3. One clearly dominant Primary should usually exist

---

## 4. Supporting must materially support Primary

No añadir por decoración.

---

## 5. Enabling should be minimum necessary

Evitar infrastructure trap.

---

## 6. Parallel only when resources, learning and risk permit

---

## 7. Deferred ≠ Rejected

---

## 8. Framework ≠ Checklist

No rellenar Primary/Supporting/Enabling/Parallel/Deferred obligatoriamente.

---

## 9. Empty category is allowed

---

## 10. Portfolio ≠ Full Roadmap

---

## 11. Strategy requires Exclusion

Debe existir claridad sobre qué no hacer ahora.

---

## 12. No Portfolio by Department

No una acción por team.

---

## 13. No Balanced Department Coverage as goal

---

## 14. Strategic Imbalance can be correct

---

## 15. Each Active Recommendation Must Earn Its Place

---

## 16. Different Labels ≠ Different Interventions

Deduplicate.

---

## 17. Avoid Recommendation Inflation

No fragmentar microdetalles.

---

## 18. Granularity should be strategic and coherent

---

## 19. Individual Feasibility ≠ Portfolio Feasibility

---

## 20. Aggregate Resource Load must be checked

---

## 21. Respect finite WIP

No universal numeric limit.

---

## 22. Management Attention is part of portfolio capacity

---

## 23. Parallel Feasibility ≠ Learning Compatibility

---

## 24. Avoid Learning Interference

Sequence when needed.

---

## 25. Avoid Parallel Everything

---

## 26. Avoid Sequential Everything

Independent work can coexist.

---

## 27. Portfolio Complexity > Sum of Project Complexity

Because interactions exist.

---

## 28. Good Initiative can be Bad Portfolio Addition

---

## 29. Avoid Portfolio Dilution

Cada acción debe recibir enough intensity.

---

## 30. Finish loops when possible

Reduce open work.

---

## 31. Avoid Sunk-Cost Portfolios

---

## 32. Pause ≠ Failure

---

## 33. Deferred Graveyard is prohibited

Deferred should have reason and, when possible, re-entry trigger.

---

## 34. Avoid Legacy Portfolio

Old roadmap item is not automatically priority.

---

## 35. Avoid Executive Wish List

Stakeholder importance ≠ project importance.

---

## 36. Avoid Portfolio by Trend

No añadir AI/personalization/TikTok/loyalty por moda.

---

## 37. Avoid Initiative Cannibalization

Recognize competition for:

- audience;
- budget;
- inventory;
- team.

---

## 38. Portfolio Impact ≠ Sum of Individual Impact

---

## 39. Do not Double Count Impact

---

## 40. More Moving Parts → More Ways to Fail

---

## 41. Simplicity Premium

Si expected value es similar, preferir menos moving parts.

---

## 42. Simple ≠ Simplistic

---

## 43. Do not Stack Multiple High-Downside Interventions without reason

---

## 44. Portfolio Blast Radius must be considered

---

## 45. Strong Evidence actions should dominate weak speculative bets

---

## 46. Fix Known Defects first when material

---

## 47. Avoid Enabling Debt

Capability building must unlock value.

---

## 48. The Primary must remain visible

Supporting/Enabling cannot eclipse it.

---

## 49. Communication must reflect priority

---

## 50. Local Win ≠ Portfolio Win

Final unit is business outcome.

---

## 51. One Priority may have Multiple Functional Contributions

---

## 52. Shared Ownership risk

Everyone owns it → nobody owns it.

---

## 53. External Single Point of Failure should be recognized

---

## 54. Urgent ≠ Strategic

Critical incident can coexist with strategic Primary.

---

## 55. No need to fill portfolio slots

---

## 56. No Action / Gather Evidence can be valid Primary

---

## 57. No internally contradictory portfolio

Trade-offs must be explicit.

---

## 58. BAU load must be considered

Do not assume 100% project capacity.

---

## 59. Preserve emergency buffer when justified

---

## 60. Remove zombie initiatives

If an action:

- creates no value;
- generates no learning;
- unlocks nothing;

question it.

---

## 61. Optimal Portfolio ≠ Maximum Portfolio

---

## 62. Add action only if marginal value exceeds marginal complexity/risk/opportunity cost

---

## 63. Finite Priority Capacity

Focus is scarce.

---

## 64. Avoid Portfolio Debt

Too many open initiatives degrade execution.

---

## 65. Avoid Thrashing

Do not reprioritize on daily noise.

---

## 66. Avoid Inertia

Do not keep weak work because it already started.

---

## 67. Portfolio Today ≠ Portfolio Forever

---

## 68. No autonomous execution

7.12 does not authorize:

- resource allocation;
- budget changes;
- campaign changes;
- hiring;
- project creation;
- scheduling;
- execution.

It only constructs the decision portfolio.
