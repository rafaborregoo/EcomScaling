# FASE 7.8 — Policies y guardrails de experimentación y ejecución

## Propósito

Estas reglas evitan tanto el `test everything` como el `ship everything`.

---

## 1. No testear defectos confirmados

Known harmful defect:

```text
FIX
```

---

## 2. No exponer deliberadamente usuarios a un bug conocido

Especialmente:

- checkout;
- payment;
- data;
- safety;
- compliance.

---

## 3. Test only if learning can change a decision

Regla central.

---

## 4. Experiment without decision = research theater

No crear tests sin decisión posterior.

---

## 5. Hypothesis required

No:

```text
“change page and see”
```

Sí:

- intervention;
- mechanism;
- expected outcome;
- guardrails.

---

## 6. No testing for sophistication signaling

El test no es un fin.

---

## 7. Over-testing es un fallo

No ralentizar decisiones obvias.

---

## 8. Under-testing también es un fallo

No lanzar big bets sobre hipótesis débiles.

---

## 9. Value of Learning debe justificar Cost of Learning

No gastar más en aprender que el valor razonable de la incertidumbre.

---

## 10. Reuse Evidence

No repetir pruebas sin necesidad.

---

## 11. Revalidate transferability

Evidence antigua no es dogma si cambió contexto.

---

## 12. Gather Evidence before Experiment when cheaper

No intervenir sobre clientes si logs/cohorts/análisis ya resuelven la pregunta.

---

## 13. Measurement prerequisite

No llamar “experiment” a un cambio no evaluable.

---

## 14. No forzar A/B tests en low-volume stores

Evitar random winners y tests interminables.

---

## 15. Statistical significance ≠ business significance

No celebrar micro-uplifts irrelevantes.

---

## 16. Primary outcome + guardrails

Todo experiment material debe proteger dimensiones importantes.

---

## 17. Do not optimize proxy only

Clicks, opens, ATC o engagement no sustituyen business outcome.

---

## 18. Do not stop too early

Respetar latency.

---

## 19. Do not run forever

Cuando learning value ya es bajo, decidir.

---

## 20. Detect contamination

No ignorar cambios concurrentes.

---

## 21. Do not change multiple variables unnecessarily

Preservar causal learning.

---

## 22. Do not artificially isolate coherent packages

La metodología debe servir a la decisión, no al ritual.

---

## 23. Experiment result is evidence, not permanent truth

Context changes.

---

## 24. Test uplift may decay at scale

No extrapolar linealmente.

---

## 25. Scale is another decision

Un winner pequeño no autoriza full rollout automático.

---

## 26. Safety / Compliance should be fixed, not tested

No experimentar con incumplimiento.

---

## 27. Clear user harm should not be tested

Experimentation must not require deliberate harm.

---

## 28. More irreversible → stronger pre-validation

Principio fuerte.

---

## 29. Proxy evidence ≠ final proof

Mantener incertidumbre explícita.

---

## 30. Do not experiment forever to avoid entrepreneurial decisions

Sufficient evidence, not perfect evidence.

---

## 31. Active Damage can override Testing

Contain/fix first.

---

## 32. Protective Action ≠ final diagnosis

Debe seguir investigación cuando proceda.

---

## 33. Time-boxed opportunity matters

No testear hasta que desaparezca la oportunidad.

---

## 34. Experimentation capacity is scarce

Priorizar highest-value uncertainty.

---

## 35. Don't test trivia while bottleneck unresolved

Regla fuerte.

---

## 36. Test the bottleneck when possible

No optimizar non-constraint.

---

## 37. Materiality before Experiment

No testear solo porque puede medirse.

---

## 38. Avoid random segmentation

Segmentar solo con causal relevance.

---

## 39. Avoid data mining for winners

No depender de random slices.

---

## 40. Incrementality matters

Attributed revenue no basta.

---

## 41. Short tests may miss long-term harm

Especialmente:

- brand;
- retention;
- discount conditioning.

---

## 42. Experimentalism bias prohibited

No asumir que only measurable matters.

---

## 43. Strategic judgment remains necessary

Experiments support, not replace, judgment.

---

## 44. Before/After ≠ causal proof

No sobreafirmar causalidad.

---

## 45. Implementation fidelity required

Half-implemented test puede invalidar conclusión.

---

## 46. Measurement integrity required

No correr consequential test con instrumentation rota.

---

## 47. Full-funnel outcome required when material

No declarar winner por stage proxy.

---

## 48. Revenue win can be Profit Loss

Incluir economics.

---

## 49. Pre-test guardrail check required

Bad intervention no se vuelve buena por llamarla test.

---

## 50. Experiment must be eligible first

7.1 sigue aplicando.

---

## 51. Exposure should grow with evidence

No hardcodear porcentajes.

---

## 52. Limited exposure preferred under uncertainty

Cuando es posible.

---

## 53. Test-and-learn is not endless

Debe terminar en decisión.

---

## 54. Test quality > test quantity

No usar count de experiments como KPI intelectual.

---

## 55. Decision velocity matters

El objetivo es mejor/faster decision-making.

---

## 56. Exploration should not prevent exploitation

Escalar winners validados cuando corresponda.

---

## 57. Exploitation should not eliminate exploration

Evitar saturation/dependency.

---

## 58. Testing infrastructure needs repeated use case

No comprar tooling sofisticado demasiado pronto.

---

## 59. Execute-and-monitor is valid

Cuando low downside + reversible + enough evidence.

---

## 60. Execute-and-monitor ≠ causal proof

Mantener humildad causal.

---

## 61. Do not require research purity

Evidence standard debe corresponder al riesgo.

---

## 62. Do not test economic impossibility

Rechazar antes si best plausible outcome sigue siendo destructivo.

---

## 63. Do not test at full blast radius when limited exposure exists

---

## 64. Do not remain at tiny exposure forever once evidence is strong

---

## 65. No moving goalposts

No redefinir success arbitrariamente después del resultado.

---

## 66. Pre-commitment to interpretation is useful

Sin impedir adaptación a nueva evidencia real.

---

## 67. Do not fish for a winner

Multiple comparisons pueden producir patrones falsos.

---

## 68. Test what the business can act on

No generar learning inútil.

---

## 69. Mechanism matters

Una hipótesis sin mecanismo es menos transferible.

---

## 70. Frontera de autonomía

7.8 no autoriza:

- lanzar experimentos;
- randomizar traffic;
- cambiar pricing;
- publicar cambios;
- asignar budgets;
- escalar rollouts;
- ejecutar rollback.

Eso pertenece a ejecución posterior.
