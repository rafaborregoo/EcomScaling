# 9.38 — Consolidation, Compression y Revalidation

## Consolidation
Convierte episodios repetidos en knowledge reusable.

## Compression
Reduce volumen preservando:
- mechanism;
- scope;
- exceptions;
- uncertainty;
- lineage.

## Revalidation
Confirma si knowledge sigue aplicando.

## Principios
- Compression no debe borrar condiciones.
- Revalidation cadence depende de volatility.
- Consolidation debe reducir duplication.

## Regla
**Long-term memory should become smaller and smarter, not merely larger.**
