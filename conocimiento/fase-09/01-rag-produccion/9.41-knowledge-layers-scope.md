# 9.41 — Knowledge Layers y Scope

## Capas

- stable expert knowledge;
- tenant knowledge;
- cohort knowledge;
- global experiential knowledge;
- policies;
- experience history.

## Scope

Todo learning debe indicar:

- tenant;
- cohort;
- channel;
- market;
- temporal;
- global.

## Principios

- Global knowledge puede actuar como prior.
- Local evidence puede crear excepciones locales.
- Promotion requiere broader evidence.
- Policy no es knowledge recuperable equivalente a RAG.

## Regla

Knowledge without scope is unsafe knowledge.
