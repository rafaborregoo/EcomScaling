# 9.31 — Explainable Promotion, Rejection y Supersession

## Promotion

Debe explicar:

- target;
- evidence;
- gates;
- scope;
- reviewer;
- limitations.

## Rejection

Debe explicar:

- insufficient evidence;
- contradiction;
- contamination;
- governance restriction.

## Supersession

Debe explicar:

- predecessor;
- successor;
- evidence change;
- scope change;
- effective period.

## Principio

No solo las conclusiones positivas deben ser explicables.

## Regla

Toda transición importante del ciclo de knowledge necesita rationale trazable.
