# 9.36 — Overgeneralization y Local Overfitting

## Global Overgeneralization

Aplicar knowledge demasiado amplio a contextos incompatibles.

## Local Overfitting

Adaptar el sistema a ruido o episodios idiosincráticos del tenant.

## Principios

- Global knowledge necesita applicability checks.
- Tenant learning necesita repeatability y evidence quality.
- Contradictions deben preservarse.
- Exploration evita lock-in local.

## Regla

The system must avoid both generic consulting and tenant memorization.
