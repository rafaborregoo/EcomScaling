# 9.39 — Strategic Assumptions, Scenarios y Reviews

## Assumptions

Toda strategic thesis depende de condiciones esperadas.

## Scenarios

Puede evaluarse bajo:

- base;
- upside;
- downside;
- stress.

## Review Triggers

- structural break;
- assumption failure;
- constraint migration;
- market shock;
- material underperformance;
- new opportunity.

## Principios

- Registrar qué assumptions se confirmaron.
- No juzgar estrategias largas con outcomes inmaduros.
- Reabrir decisiones cuando cambia el regime.

## Regla

A strategy should remain active because its thesis still holds, not because it was once approved.
