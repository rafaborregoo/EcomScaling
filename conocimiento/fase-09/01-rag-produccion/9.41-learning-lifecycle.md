# 9.41 — Learning Lifecycle

## Estados posibles

- observed;
- candidate;
- validated;
- promoted;
- active;
- degraded;
- contradicted;
- superseded;
- invalidated;
- dormant;
- archived;
- withdrawn.

## Transiciones

Un learning puede:

- ganar confidence;
- perder scope;
- fragmentarse;
- degradarse;
- supersede;
- reactivarse;
- retirarse.

## Principio

El lifecycle debe ser reversible cuando la evidencia lo exija.

## Regla

Knowledge is a versioned lifecycle, not a permanent fact.
