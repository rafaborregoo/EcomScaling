# 9.37 — Privacy-Safe Cross-Tenant Learning

## Permitido conceptualmente

- generalized mechanisms;
- aggregated distributions;
- cohort patterns;
- abstract failure modes.

## No transferir directamente

- raw customer data;
- private documents;
- identifiable metrics;
- confidential internal decisions.

## Principios

- Cross-tenant knowledge debe tener explicit transfer eligibility.
- Small cohorts necesitan mayor protección.
- Global promotion requiere sanitization y review.

## Regla

Transfer knowledge, not tenant secrets.
