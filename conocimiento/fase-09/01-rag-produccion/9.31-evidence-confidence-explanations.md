# 9.31 — Evidence y Confidence Explanations

## Evidence Explanation

Debe distinguir:

- observation;
- experiment;
- correlation;
- causal evidence;
- human feedback;
- operational evidence.

## Confidence Explanation

Debe mostrar factores como:

- support;
- coverage;
- independence;
- repeatability;
- stability;
- recency;
- contradictions.

## Principio

Un score sin rationale es poco auditable.

## Regla

Confidence debe ser explicable por componentes de evidencia, no presentada como propiedad mágica del sistema.
