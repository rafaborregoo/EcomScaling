# 9.41 — Evidence, Confidence y Governance

## Evidence Quality

Debe considerar:

- support;
- coverage;
- independence;
- repeatability;
- stability;
- outcome maturity;
- provenance;
- contradiction level.

## Confidence

Debe ser multidimensional y explicable.

## Governance

La authority depende de:

- risk;
- scope;
- reversibility;
- privacy;
- policy impact.

## Principio

High confidence no equivale a permission.

## Regla

**Evidence may justify belief; governance authorizes action.**
