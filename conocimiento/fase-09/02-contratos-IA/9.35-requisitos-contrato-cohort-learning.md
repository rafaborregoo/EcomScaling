# 9.35 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para Cohort Learning

## Objetivo

Definir qué información debe poder representarse para cohortes y sus aprendizajes.

## 1. Cohort Definition

Debe representar:

- cohort id;
- purpose;
- relevant dimensions;
- inclusion criteria;
- exclusion criteria;
- version.

## 2. Membership

Debe incluir:

- tenant reference abstracta;
- membership state;
- membership confidence;
- effective period.

## 3. Cohort Evidence

Debe representar:

- tenant count;
- support;
- independence;
- coverage;
- variance;
- exceptions.

## 4. Cohort Pattern

Debe incluir:

- claim;
- applicability;
- confidence;
- contradictions;
- temporal validity.

## 5. Lifecycle

Debe soportar:

- candidate;
- observed;
- validated;
- active;
- splitting;
- merging;
- degraded;
- retired.

## 6. Drift

Debe poder representar cambios en:

- definition;
- membership;
- behavior.

## 7. Benchmark

Debe incluir:

- metric;
- distribution;
- period;
- cohort version.

## 8. Playbook Applicability

Debe representar prerequisites y cohort fit.

## 9. Privacy Boundary

Debe impedir exposición identificable de tenants.

## 10. Provenance

Debe enlazar:

- tenant learnings;
- transfer episodes;
- patterns;
- reviews;
- promotions.

## Regla crítica

El contrato debe distinguir:

**cohort membership**

de:

**cohort pattern applicability**.
