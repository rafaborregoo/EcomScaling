# 9.16 — Requisitos conceptuales para Prioritization Learning Contract

## Priority Decision

Debe poder representar:

- priority reference;
- target problem;
- decision timestamp;
- decision horizon.

## Evaluation Basis

- expected impact;
- urgency;
- constraint;
- bottleneck hypothesis;
- confidence;
- risk;
- effort;
- dependencies.

## Alternatives

Debe poder representar:

- alternative actions;
- estimated value;
- reason not selected;
- opportunity cost.

## Outcome

- selected action;
- execution;
- observed impact;
- remaining gap;
- next bottleneck.

## Learning

- prioritization quality;
- bottleneck accuracy;
- impact calibration;
- urgency calibration;
- opportunity-cost insight;
- bottleneck migration.

## History

Debe permitir reconstruir:

```text
priority at T0
→ intervention
→ outcome
→ reprioritization at T1
```

## Regla

El contrato debe mantener separadas la calidad de la acción y la calidad de haberla priorizado.
