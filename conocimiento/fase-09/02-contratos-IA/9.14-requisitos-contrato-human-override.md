# 9.14 — Requisitos conceptuales para Human Override Contract

## AI Proposal

Debe poder representar:

- recommendation / decision reference;
- proposed action;
- rationale;
- confidence;
- evidence;
- risk assessment.

## Human Intervention

Debe distinguir:

```text
APPROVAL
REJECTION
MODIFICATION
OVERRIDE
TAKEOVER
```

## Review Context

- mandatory or optional;
- reviewer role;
- authority;
- timestamp.

## Rationale

Debe poder representar:

- reason;
- reason category;
- supporting context;
- evidence;
- uncertainty.

## Final Decision

- final action;
- changes vs AI proposal;
- scope;
- timing.

## Outcome

Debe poder vincular:

- execution;
- outcome;
- maturity;
- later evaluation.

## Learning

- context gap;
- AI error;
- human bias;
- risk correction;
- operational adaptation;
- strategic disagreement;
- autonomy implication.

## Regla

El contrato no debe marcar automáticamente una decisión humana como `correct=true`.
