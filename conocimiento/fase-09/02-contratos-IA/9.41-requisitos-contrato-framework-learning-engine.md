# 9.41 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato del Learning Engine

## Objetivo

Definir los elementos mínimos que los futuros contratos deben poder representar de extremo a extremo.

## Learning Identity
- learning id;
- version;
- state;
- knowledge layer.

## Claim
- statement;
- scope;
- context;
- applicability;
- limitations.

## Evidence
- source;
- type;
- quality;
- support;
- coverage;
- independence;
- maturity.

## Outcome
- metric;
- direction;
- magnitude;
- maturity;
- attribution state.

## Confidence
- evidence confidence;
- causal confidence;
- generalization confidence;
- applicability confidence.

## Contradiction
- conflicting claim;
- overlap;
- resolution state.

## Provenance
- source;
- episode;
- intervention;
- actor;
- tool/model version;
- transformation.

## Lifecycle
- current state;
- predecessor;
- successor;
- effective period.

## Promotion
- source scope;
- target scope;
- eligibility;
- review;
- decision.

## Governance
- risk;
- authority level;
- review state;
- approver;
- policy impact.

## Privacy
- tenant scope;
- sensitivity;
- cross-tenant eligibility;
- aggregation state.

## Runtime References
- decisions influenced;
- actions;
- outcomes;
- revalidation triggers.

## Regla crítica

El contrato debe permitir reconstruir:
**qué se aprendió, por qué, dónde aplica, quién puede usarlo y qué ocurrió después**.
