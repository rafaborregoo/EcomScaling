# 9.31 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para Explainable Learning

## Objetivo

Definir qué información debe poder representarse para explicar aprendizajes.

## 1. Explanation Identity

Debe representar:

- explanation id;
- learning id;
- knowledge version;
- audience;
- explanation version.

## 2. Claim

Debe incluir:

- statement;
- context;
- scope;
- applicability.

## 3. Evidence

Debe representar:

- supporting evidence;
- evidence type;
- strength;
- provenance;
- weight.

## 4. Counterevidence

Debe incluir:

- contradictory evidence;
- exceptions;
- unresolved conflicts.

## 5. Confidence Rationale

Debe poder expresar:

- confidence level;
- contributing factors;
- limiting factors.

## 6. Assumptions

Debe representar assumptions relevantes.

## 7. Uncertainty

Debe incluir:

- type;
- source;
- impact;
- unresolved questions.

## 8. Influence

Debe poder expresar cómo el learning afectó:

- recommendation;
- prioritization;
- playbook selection;
- autonomy;
- review.

## 9. Non-Influence

Debe poder explicar por qué knowledge recuperado fue ignorado.

## 10. Provenance

Debe conservar:

- data source;
- episode;
- experiment;
- human feedback;
- model/tool version.

## 11. Transition Explanation

Debe cubrir:

- promotion;
- rejection;
- supersession;
- invalidation;
- quarantine;
- abstention.

## Regla crítica

El contrato debe impedir una explicación sin vínculo verificable con evidence y version.
