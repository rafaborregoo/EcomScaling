# 9.12 — Requisitos conceptuales para Outcome Learning Contract

## Identidad

- intervention reference;
- outcome reference;
- assessment version;
- period.

## Target

Debe poder representar:

- primary outcome;
- secondary outcomes;
- guardrail outcomes;
- expected effect.

## Observación

- observed effect;
- direction;
- magnitude;
- materiality;
- maturity.

## Evaluación

Debe poder representar:

```text
POSITIVE
NEGATIVE
NEUTRAL
INCONCLUSIVE
MIXED
```

además de:

- attribution strength;
- evidence strength;
- rationale;
- context.

## Heterogeneidad

Debe poder conservar:

- segment-specific outcomes;
- horizon-specific outcomes;
- metric-specific outcomes.

## Informational value

Debe poder indicar:

- uncertainty reduced;
- assumption challenged;
- exclusion condition discovered;
- next evidence need.

## History

Debe permitir reclassification cuando maduren nuevos outcomes.

## Regla

El contrato debe impedir que `NEUTRAL` e `INCONCLUSIVE` sean representados como estados equivalentes.
