# 9.29 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para Feedback Loops peligrosos

## Objetivo

Definir qué información debe representarse para detectar y controlar loops auto-reforzados.

## 1. Feedback Relation

Debe representar:

- source action;
- resulting state;
- future decision affected;
- direction;
- amplification.

## 2. Exposure

Debe incluir:

- option;
- traffic;
- spend;
- frequency;
- duration;
- share.

## 3. Allocation

Debe representar:

- resource;
- current allocation;
- previous allocation;
- growth rate.

## 4. Concentration

Debe poder expresar:

- concentration level;
- diversity;
- dominant option;
- trend.

## 5. Feedback State

Debe soportar:

- stable;
- amplifying;
- runaway;
- constrained;
- broken.

## 6. Safeguards

Debe representar:

- caps;
- rate limits;
- holdouts;
- exploration share;
- circuit breaker;
- rollback.

## 7. Secondary Outcomes

Debe incluir:

- metric;
- direction;
- severity;
- time horizon.

## 8. Governance

Debe expresar:

- max exposure;
- review threshold;
- human approval;
- prohibited amplification.

## 9. Provenance

Debe conservar:

- decisions;
- allocations;
- outcomes;
- model versions;
- human interventions.

## Regla crítica

El contrato debe permitir distinguir:

**evidence generated by performance**

de:

**evidence generated by exposure**.
