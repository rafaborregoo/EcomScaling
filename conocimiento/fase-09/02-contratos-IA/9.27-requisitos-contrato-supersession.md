# 9.27 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para Knowledge Contradiction y Supersession

## Objetivo

Definir qué información debe representarse para gestionar conflicto y reemplazo de conocimiento.

## 1. Knowledge Entity

Debe representar:

- id;
- type;
- version;
- status;
- scope;
- effective period.

## 2. Contradiction Relation

Debe incluir:

- source knowledge;
- conflicting knowledge;
- overlap;
- type;
- severity;
- evidence.

## 3. Supersession Relation

Debe representar:

- predecessor;
- successor;
- full/partial;
- scope;
- effective date;
- reason.

## 4. Evidence Basis

Debe incluir:

- support;
- coverage;
- quality;
- independence;
- recency;
- contradiction strength.

## 5. Status

Debe soportar:

- active;
- degraded;
- superseded;
- invalidated;
- historical.

## 6. Review

Debe representar:

- reviewer;
- review state;
- rationale;
- approval;
- rollback eligibility.

## 7. Retrieval State

Debe diferenciar:

- active for decision;
- available for audit;
- excluded from operational retrieval.

## 8. Provenance

Debe conservar:

- original source;
- promotion history;
- contradiction evidence;
- supersession history.

## 9. Rollback

Debe permitir:

- revert supersession;
- reactivate predecessor;
- degrade successor.

## Regla crítica

El contrato debe impedir sobrescritura sin lineage.
