# 9.39 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para Strategic Learning

## Objetivo

Definir qué información debe poder representarse para aprender de decisiones estratégicas.

## Strategic Question
- problem;
- objective;
- scope;
- horizon.

## Strategic Thesis
- hypothesis;
- expected mechanism;
- desired outcome;
- applicability.

## Assumptions
- assumption;
- importance;
- validation state;
- evidence.

## Trade-offs
- benefited dimension;
- sacrificed dimension;
- rationale.

## Constraint
- type;
- severity;
- evidence;
- migration state.

## Resource Allocation
- resource;
- allocated amount conceptual;
- alternative use;
- opportunity cost.

## Strategic Bet
- upside;
- uncertainty;
- commitment;
- reversibility;
- maturity window.

## Kill Criteria
- condition;
- threshold conceptual;
- review state.

## Strategic Outcome
- horizon;
- primary outcomes;
- secondary outcomes;
- maturity;
- causal confidence.

## Review
- trigger;
- evidence;
- recommendation;
- governance decision.

## Provenance
Debe preservar thesis, evidence, versions, decisions y outcomes.

## Regla crítica
El contrato debe diferenciar strategic hypothesis, strategic decision y validated strategic learning.
