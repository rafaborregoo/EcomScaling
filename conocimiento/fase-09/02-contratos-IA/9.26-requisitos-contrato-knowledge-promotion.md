# 9.26 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para Knowledge Promotion

## Objetivo

Definir qué información debe poder representarse e intercambiarse durante promoción de conocimiento.

## 1. Knowledge Candidate

Debe representar:

- candidate id;
- source type;
- source learning;
- current state;
- proposed target;
- scope;
- version.

## 2. Evidence Basis

Debe incluir:

- support;
- coverage;
- independence;
- repeatability;
- stability;
- contradictions;
- maturity;
- freshness.

## 3. Promotion Target

Debe poder expresar:

- tenant;
- cohort;
- global;
- playbook candidate;
- benchmark candidate;
- RAG candidate;
- governance candidate.

## 4. Promotion Rationale

Debe contener:

- why;
- expected reuse;
- limits;
- uncertainty;
- risk.

## 5. Review State

Debe representar:

- eligible;
- under review;
- approved;
- rejected;
- deferred;
- reverted.

## 6. Scope

Debe incluir:

- tenant;
- cohort;
- channel;
- market;
- business model;
- temporal validity.

## 7. Provenance

Debe vincular:

- episodes;
- experiments;
- patterns;
- human reviews;
- model versions.

## 8. Versioning

Debe conservar:

- previous version;
- new version;
- effective date;
- reason.

## 9. Rollback

Debe representar:

- degradation;
- withdrawal;
- supersession;
- invalidation.

## 10. Cross-layer target

Debe diferenciar:

- RAG candidate;
- Experience knowledge;
- Policy review;
- Playbook candidate.

## Regla crítica

El contrato debe impedir confundir:

**promotion eligibility**

con:

**promotion approval**.
