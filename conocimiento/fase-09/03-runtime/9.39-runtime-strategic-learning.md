# 9.39 — Runtime de Strategic Learning

## Flujo conceptual

1. Resolver strategic question y tenant context.
2. Identificar binding constraints.
3. Formular strategic thesis y alternatives.
4. Registrar assumptions y trade-offs.
5. Estimar reversibility, risk y time horizon.
6. Comparar resource allocation y opportunity cost.
7. Definir maturity window y kill criteria.
8. Ejecutar solo dentro de governance aplicable.
9. Observar tactical y strategic outcomes por separado.
10. Revisar assumptions.
11. Detectar constraint migration o structural break.
12. Clasificar thesis como strengthened, weakened, inconclusive, superseded o invalidated.
13. Consolidar strategic learning solo tras evidence suficiente.

## Regla runtime

**Do not promote a tactic into strategy until its effect on the business system is understood.**
