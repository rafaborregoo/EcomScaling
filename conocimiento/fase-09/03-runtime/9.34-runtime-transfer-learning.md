# 9.34 — Runtime de Transfer Learning

## Objetivo

Definir cómo seleccionar, evaluar y validar learnings cross-tenant.

## 1. Candidate Retrieval

Ante un target:

- recuperar learnings relevantes;
- excluir tenant-specific knowledge no transferable;
- filtrar privacy constraints.

## 2. Similarity Evaluation

Comparar:

- mechanism;
- business model;
- maturity;
- economics;
- channels;
- operations;
- objective.

## 3. Transfer Classification

Clasificar como:

- direct candidate;
- adapted candidate;
- hypothesis only;
- non-transferable.

## 4. Risk Gate

Si impact es alto:

- reducir exposure;
- pedir review;
- exigir validación local.

## 5. Validation

Ejecutar cuando proceda:

- pilot;
- bounded experiment;
- gradual rollout.

## 6. Local Evidence Update

Actualizar:

- transfer confidence;
- applicability;
- target-specific learning.

## 7. Negative Transfer Handling

Si falla:

- registrar mismatch;
- no invalidar source automáticamente;
- limitar future transfer conditions.

## 8. Promotion

Si funciona repetidamente en cohortes similares:

- elevar cohort evidence;
- no convertir automáticamente en global rule.

## Regla runtime

> Retrieve broadly, transfer narrowly, validate locally.
