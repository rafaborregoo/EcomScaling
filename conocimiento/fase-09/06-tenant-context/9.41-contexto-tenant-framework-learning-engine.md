# 9.41 — Tenant Context final del Learning Engine

## Tenant Context debe poder aportar

### Business
- business model;
- maturity;
- markets;
- product/category;
- lifecycle.

### Economics
- AOV;
- margin;
- CAC;
- LTV;
- repeat rate;
- cash constraints.

### Channels
- paid mix;
- CRM;
- SEO;
- marketplaces;
- retail.

### Operations
- inventory;
- fulfillment;
- team;
- tooling;
- capacity.

### Data
- tracking;
- attribution;
- history depth;
- quality.

### Risk
- risk tolerance;
- approval thresholds;
- autonomy boundaries.

### Strategy
- objectives;
- priorities;
- time horizon;
- constraints.

### Privacy
- data sensitivity;
- retention;
- permitted processing;
- tenant-specific restrictions.

## Principios

- No inventar tenant values.
- Missing context debe reducir confidence.
- Tenant context condiciona applicability, no modifica global policies.
- Tenant-specific learning permanece tenant-scoped salvo promotion válida.

## Regla

The Learning Engine reasons globally, but must decide in the real context of the tenant.
