# 9.36 — Tenant Context para Global Knowledge vs Tenant-Specific Learning

## Objetivo

Definir qué contexto local es necesario para evaluar applicability de knowledge global.

## Contexto relevante

### Negocio
- business model;
- maturity;
- revenue scale;
- objectives.

### Economía
- AOV;
- margin;
- CAC;
- LTV;
- repeat rate.

### Producto
- category;
- assortment;
- seasonality;
- replenishment.

### Mercado
- geography;
- competition;
- demand.

### Canales
- acquisition mix;
- CRM maturity;
- marketplaces;
- retail.

### Operación
- inventory;
- fulfillment;
- capacity;
- tooling.

### Datos
- tracking;
- attribution;
- history depth;
- reliability.

## Principio

El contexto tenant-specific determina applicability; no debe inventarse.

## Prohibición

No tratar assumptions globales como facts del tenant.
