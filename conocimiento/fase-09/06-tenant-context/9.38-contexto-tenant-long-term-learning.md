# 9.38 — Tenant Context relevante para Long-Term Learning

## Contexto relevante
- business model history;
- maturity changes;
- market entries/exits;
- channel transitions;
- pricing model changes;
- category/product shifts;
- platform migrations;
- organizational changes;
- seasonality;
- strategic objectives;
- retention constraints.

## Principios
- Tenant continuity no debe asumirse.
- Structural changes deben registrarse.
- Historical knowledge debe interpretarse dentro del regime correcto.

## Prohibición
No aplicar automáticamente learnings históricos a un tenant cuyo contexto cambió materialmente.
