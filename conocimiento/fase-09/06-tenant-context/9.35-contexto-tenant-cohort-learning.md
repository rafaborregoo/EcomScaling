# 9.35 — Tenant Context relevante para Cohort Learning

## Objetivo

Definir qué atributos tenant-specific pueden utilizarse para evaluar cohort membership.

## Variables posibles

### Negocio
- business model;
- maturity;
- revenue scale;
- growth stage.

### Economía
- AOV;
- margin;
- CAC;
- LTV;
- repeat rate.

### Producto
- category;
- assortment;
- replenishment;
- returns;
- seasonality.

### Mercado
- geography;
- competition;
- demand.

### Canales
- acquisition mix;
- CRM maturity;
- marketplaces;
- retail.

### Operación
- inventory;
- fulfillment;
- team;
- tooling;
- capacity.

### Datos
- tracking quality;
- attribution;
- volume;
- history depth.

## Principio

Solo deben utilizarse variables relevantes al learning concreto.

## Prohibición

No inventar valores tenant para completar cohort membership.
