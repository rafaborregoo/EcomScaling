# 9.39 — Tenant Context relevante para Strategic Learning

## Contexto necesario

### Strategic position
- business model;
- maturity;
- differentiation;
- market position.

### Economics
- revenue;
- margin;
- CAC;
- LTV;
- cash;
- working capital.

### Growth system
- channel mix;
- concentration;
- retention;
- product demand.

### Operations
- inventory;
- fulfillment;
- team capacity;
- tooling;
- data quality.

### Risk
- tolerance;
- runway;
- dependency;
- irreversibility constraints.

### Objectives
- growth;
- profitability;
- cash generation;
- resilience;
- market expansion.

### Time horizon
- quarterly;
- annual;
- multi-year.

## Principios

- Strategic learning es altamente tenant-specific.
- No inventar strategic objectives ni risk tolerance.
- El mismo playbook puede tener valor estratégico distinto según economics y constraints.

## Regla

Strategy must be learned inside the actual economic and operational system of the tenant.
