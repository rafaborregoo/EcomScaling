# 9.14 — Human Override en Experience / Learning

## Cadena conceptual

```text
AI PROPOSAL
↓
HUMAN INTERVENTION
↓
FINAL DECISION
↓
EXECUTION
↓
OUTCOME
↓
LEARNING
```

## Cada experiencia debe conservar

- AI proposal;
- AI rationale;
- AI confidence;
- human intervention type;
- human rationale;
- final action;
- execution;
- outcome;
- evaluation.

## Learning possible

- context gap;
- AI reasoning weakness;
- human bias;
- risk correction;
- operational constraint;
- strategic preference;
- complementarity pattern.

## Pattern learning

Múltiples experiencias pueden revelar:

```text
when human intervention improves decisions
```

o:

```text
when it adds unnecessary friction
```

sin convertir esto en una regla global.

## Regla

La intervención humana debe tratarse como experiencia evaluable y no como ground truth inmutable.
