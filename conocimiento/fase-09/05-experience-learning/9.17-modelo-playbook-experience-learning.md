# 9.17 — Playbooks en Experience / Learning

## Cadena conceptual

```text
CONTEXT
↓
PLAYBOOK VERSION
↓
PRECONDITIONS
↓
BRANCH
↓
STEP SEQUENCE
↓
EXECUTION
↓
OUTCOME
↓
LEARNING
```

## Cada experiencia debe conservar

- playbook;
- version;
- context;
- preconditions;
- branch;
- planned steps;
- executed steps;
- sequence;
- modifications;
- fidelity;
- completion;
- outcome.

## Learnings posibles

- playbook effective in context C;
- step X likely critical;
- step Y likely redundant;
- branch B superior in context D;
- sequence A→B superior;
- precondition Z required;
- step K highly execution-sensitive.

## Regla

El Learning Engine debe aprender a varios niveles sin inventar granularidad que la evidencia no soporte.
