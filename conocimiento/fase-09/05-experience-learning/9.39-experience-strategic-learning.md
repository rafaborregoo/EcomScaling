# 9.39 — Experience / Learning de Strategic Learning

## Strategic Decision History

Debe conservar:

- thesis;
- alternatives;
- assumptions;
- chosen path;
- resource commitment;
- review decisions.

## Assumption History

Registrar:

- confirmed;
- weakened;
- falsified;
- unresolved assumptions.

## Constraint History

Permite aprender:

- qué constraint era realmente binding;
- cuándo migró;
- qué intervenciones lo resolvieron.

## Bet History

Debe conservar:

- scaling;
- persistence;
- kill;
- reversal;
- later interpretation.

## Strategic Failure Learning

Clasificar failure como:

- thesis failure;
- execution failure;
- timing failure;
- capability failure;
- external shock.

## Strategic Success Learning

Debe identificar:

- mechanism;
- enabling conditions;
- repeatability;
- dependency;
- hidden trade-offs.

## Meta-learning

Puede aprender qué tipos de strategic bets, assumptions y constraints suelen diagnosticarse bien o mal.

## Regla

El sistema debe aprender de la calidad de sus decisiones estratégicas, no solo de sus resultados.
