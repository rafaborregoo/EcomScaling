# 9.35 — Policies y Guardrails de Cohort Learning

## 1. Tenant isolation

No exponer datos identificables de cohort members.

## 2. No cohort-as-truth

Cohort knowledge no sustituye evidence local.

## 3. Independence requirement

No confundir event count con tenant diversity.

## 4. No superficial cohorts

No definir cohort solo por variables irrelevantes.

## 5. No permanent membership

Membership debe poder revisarse.

## 6. No hidden benchmark leakage

Benchmarks deben estar agregados y contextualizados.

## 7. Over-segmentation control

Evitar cohortes tan pequeñas que solo memoricen tenants.

## 8. Under-segmentation control

Evitar cohortes tan amplias que oculten heterogeneity.

## 9. Human review

Debe exigirse cuando:

- cohort affects high-risk decisions;
- cohort is used for global promotion;
- privacy risk exists;
- cohort is very small.

## Regla maestra

> Cohort Learning must improve contextual precision without compromising tenant isolation or local truth.
