# 9.34 — Policies y Guardrails de Transfer Learning

## 1. Tenant isolation

No revelar datos identificables o confidenciales de un tenant a otro.

## 2. No copy-paste transfer

No aplicar automáticamente el mismo playbook cross-tenant.

## 3. Prior not truth

Cross-tenant knowledge no se considera local truth sin evidence.

## 4. Similarity explainability

Transfer decisions deben explicar matching y mismatch.

## 5. High-risk validation

Transferencias de alto impacto requieren controles más fuertes.

## 6. No source invalidation from single target failure

Un negative transfer local no invalida source learning.

## 7. No global promotion from superficial similarity

La promoción necesita coverage e independence.

## 8. Privacy-safe aggregation

Cross-tenant patterns deben abstraerse o agregarse adecuadamente.

## 9. Human review

Debe exigirse cuando:

- high-risk transfer;
- privacy-sensitive inference;
- global promotion candidate;
- major mismatch.

## Regla maestra

> Cross-tenant learning must increase leverage without weakening isolation or epistemic discipline.
