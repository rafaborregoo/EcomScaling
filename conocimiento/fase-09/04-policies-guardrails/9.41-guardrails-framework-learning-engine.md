# 9.41 — Guardrails finales del Learning Engine

## Hard Rules

1. No self-granted authority.
2. No policy modification through learning.
3. No raw cross-tenant leakage.
4. No promotion without provenance.
5. No causal overstatement.
6. No global promotion from one tenant.
7. No use of invalidated knowledge as active.
8. No silent supersession.
9. No hidden contradictions.
10. No explanation fabrication.
11. No infinite active memory.
12. No critical threshold changes without governance.
13. No high-risk exploration without bounds.
14. No tenant value invention.
15. No RAG pollution with dynamic raw experience.

## Human Review Triggers

- high impact;
- irreversible action;
- global promotion;
- unresolved contradiction;
- autonomy expansion;
- privacy-sensitive transfer;
- policy implication;
- strategic reallocation.

## Fail-Safe Principle

Ante uncertainty material:

- reducir scope;
- reducir autonomy;
- abstain;
- quarantine;
- request review.

## Regla maestra

**When uncertainty rises, authority must not rise with it.**
