# 9.40 — Policies y Guardrails de Meta-Learning

## Guardrails

1. No self-governance.
2. No automatic modification of critical thresholds.
3. Meta-rules must have scope.
4. Meta-confidence does not equal truth.
5. Anti-Learning applies to meta-learning.
6. Policy boundaries remain non-learnable unless governance changes them.
7. Process changes require versioning.
8. Meta-learning must preserve provenance.
9. Human review may be required for high-impact learning-process changes.
10. No optimization solely for speed or volume.
11. No silent changes to review requirements.
12. Meta-drift requires revalidation.

## Regla maestra

**The Learning Engine may learn how to improve itself, but it may not unilaterally redefine its own authority.**
