# 9.38 — Policies y Guardrails de Long-Term Learning

## Guardrails
- No infinite active memory.
- No age-equals-validity.
- No recency absolutism.
- Structural breaks requieren review.
- Compression no puede borrar scope, exceptions o uncertainty.
- Long-lived knowledge necesita revalidation.
- Long-term retention debe respetar privacy y deletion.
- Consolidation no puede destruir lineage crítica.
- Strategic/global knowledge de alto impacto puede requerir human review.

## Regla maestra
**Preserve durable knowledge without freezing the past into the future.**
