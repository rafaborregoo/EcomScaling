## RAG EXTRAÍDO

Conocimiento estable sobre:

- jerarquía orientativa de evidencia;
- randomized experiments;
- controlled experiments;
- A/B tests;
- holdouts;
- quasi-experiments;
- natural experiments;
- matched comparisons;
- before/after;
- observational evidence;
- anecdotal evidence;
- incertidumbre;
- replicación;
- validez interna y externa.

## CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS

Representar:

- evidence type;
- design;
- treatment/control;
- assignment;
- unit of analysis;
- outcome;
- effect;
- uncertainty;
- quality;
- applicability;
- caveats;
- provenance.

## RUNTIME

Evaluar evidencia antes de promover resultados a learning.

## POLICIES

Evitar:

- anecdote promotion;
- winner absolutism;
- cherry-picking;
- false precision;
- ocultación de contradicciones;
- decisiones de alto riesgo con evidencia insuficiente.

## EXPERIENCE / LEARNING

Conservar:

```text
learning
→ evidence
→ evidence strength
→ learning confidence
```

## TENANT CONTEXT

Usar contexto para evaluar aplicabilidad y validez externa.
