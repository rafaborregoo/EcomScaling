## Conceptos multi-capa

### State
- RAG: significado.
- Contracts: representación.
- Runtime: authoritative state.
- Policies: allowed behavior.
- Learning: history/time-in-state.
- Tenant: extensions.

### Transition
- RAG: lógica conceptual.
- Contracts: from/to/event/reason.
- Runtime: validation/application.
- Policies: transition authority.
- Learning: invalid transition attempts.

### UNKNOWN
- RAG: high-risk semantic state.
- Contracts: explicit representation.
- Runtime: verify-before-retry.
- Policies: not equal failure.
- Learning: observability weakness.

### PAUSED
- RAG: temporary stop.
- Runtime: no normal writes.
- Policies: resume requires revalidation.
- Tenant: who may resume.
- Learning: pause effectiveness.

### BLOCKED
- RAG: cannot advance now.
- Runtime: wait + revalidate.
- Policies: not equal failure.
- Learning: bottleneck analysis.

### COMPLETED / VERIFIED
- RAG: procedural vs verified distinction.
- Runtime: separate transitions.
- Policies: no premature close.
- Learning: verification failure evidence.

### CLOSED
- RAG: terminal operational state.
- Runtime: closure gate.
- Policies: no execution after closure.
- Tenant: extra closure conditions.

### Rollback
- RAG: own lifecycle.
- Contracts: sub-lifecycle.
- Runtime: execution + verification.
- Policies: must verify.
- Learning: rollback performance.

### Ownership
- RAG: continuity and handoff.
- Contracts: actor/state owner.
- Runtime: transfer.
- Policies: no control vacuum.
- Tenant: ownership model.

## Redundancia consolidada

El documento repite de forma útil:

- state is not event;
- READY is not RUNNING;
- BLOCKED is not FAILED;
- UNKNOWN is not FAILED;
- ABORTED is not FAILED;
- COMPLETED is not VERIFIED;
- VERIFIED is not business success;
- resume requires revalidation;
- actual state must reflect reality;
- closed work should not resume.

Estas ideas se consolidaron en `01-rag-produccion`.
