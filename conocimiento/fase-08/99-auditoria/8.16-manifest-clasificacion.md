## Conceptos multi-capa

### Completion vs Closure
- RAG: semantic distinction.
- Contracts: separate state/result.
- Runtime: closure gate.
- Policies: no automatic collapse.
- Learning: closure lag.

### Final State
- RAG: actual reality over expected.
- Contracts: final-state representation.
- Runtime: capture.
- Policies: no false state.
- Learning: post-intervention baseline.

### Residual Risk
- RAG: closure may coexist with known risk.
- Contracts: owner/acceptance.
- Runtime: gate/handoff.
- Policies: explicit acceptance.
- Tenant: risk authority.
- Learning: later outcome.

### Handoff
- RAG: transfer of responsibility.
- Contracts: package/status/owner.
- Runtime: request/accept.
- Policies: no ownership vacuum.
- Tenant: actual roles.
- Learning: handoff quality.

### Cleanup
- RAG: closure hygiene.
- Contracts: cleanup state.
- Runtime: release/revoke.
- Policies: temporary authority/resource cleanup.
- Tenant: required resources.

### Operational / Business / Strategic Outcome
- RAG: semantic separation.
- Contracts: distinct outcome dimensions.
- Runtime: operational close vs ongoing observation.
- Policies: no premature success.
- Learning: interpretation.

### Learning Handoff
- RAG: what Phase 9 conceptually needs.
- Contracts: structured conceptual package.
- Runtime: generation/transfer.
- Policies: evidence/context honesty.
- Learning: core consumer.
- Tenant: depth/retention.

### Intervention Lineage
- RAG: pilot/rollout/follow-up/supersession relationships.
- Contracts: relation representation.
- Runtime: handoff/package.
- Learning: unit-of-analysis correctness.

## Redundancia consolidada

El documento repite de forma útil:

- execution finished is not intervention closed;
- closed is not successful;
- final state must reflect reality;
- closure can precede mature business outcome;
- handoff is ownership transfer, not merely a message;
- residual risk must be explicit;
- failed/rejected/blocked cases are learning data;
- operational outcome must stay separate from business outcome;
- closed work should not receive new execution writes;
- Phase 9 must receive evidence, not assumptions.

Estas ideas se consolidaron en `01-rag-produccion`.
