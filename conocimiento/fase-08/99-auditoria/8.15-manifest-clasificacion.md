## Redundancia consolidada

El documento repite de forma útil:

- requested is not executed;
- accepted is not applied;
- applied is not verified;
- propagation is not verification;
- no evidence is not verification;
- absence of error is not success;
- verification must preserve scope and invariants;
- high risk requires stronger evidence;
- completed is not verified;
- verification is not business outcome evaluation.

Estas ideas se consolidaron en `01-rag-produccion`.

---

## Contenido excluido del RAG como responsabilidad primaria

Se trasladaron fuera del RAG:

- current verification status;
- concrete evidence records;
- active propagation timers;
- retries/rechecks;
- verification timeouts;
- current source health;
- closure gating;
- current coverage;
- actual confidence;
- repair/rollback invocation;
- state persistence.

## Ejemplos no convertidos en defaults
