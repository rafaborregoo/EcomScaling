## Conceptos clasificados en más de una capa

- RAG: boundary concept.
- Contracts: decision package and target.
- Runtime: routing/lifecycle.
- Policies: when mandatory.
- Learning: usefulness/frequency.
- Tenant: routing and roles.

### Human Override
- RAG: meaning and types.
- Contracts: scope/duration/authority.
- Runtime: application.
- Policies: limits.
- Learning: outcomes.
- Tenant: override rights.

### Takeover
- RAG: human control.
- Contracts: ownership state.
- Runtime: suspension/assist/hand-back.
- Policies: no control vacuum.
- Learning: effectiveness.

### Decision Rights
- RAG: who can decide what.
- Contracts: required authority.
- Runtime: authority check.
- Policies: bounded human authority.
- Tenant: actual roles.

### Emergency Stop
- RAG: stop additional harm.
- Contracts: scope/result.
- Runtime: activation/verification.
- Policies: priority.
- Tenant: kill-switch levels.
- Learning: stop latency.

### Autonomy Regression
- RAG: revocable autonomy.
- Runtime: capability restriction.
- Policies: no automatic restoration.
- Tenant: regression rules.
- Learning: safety outcomes.

### Handoff
- RAG: control transfer.
- Contracts: requested/accepted.
- Runtime: ownership transition.
- Policies: no vacuum.
- Tenant: handoff protocol.
- Learning: handoff quality.

### Human Decision
- RAG: approval/modification/rejection semantics.
- Contracts: decision result.
- Runtime: apply/revalidate.
- Policies: hard constraints remain.
- Learning: compare with outcome.

## Redundancia consolidada

El documento reitera:

- autonomy must be revocable;
- escalation is not failure;
- low confidence alone does not always require human;
- high risk + low confidence usually should escalate;
- least necessary human authority;
- silence is not approval;
- handoff must not create control vacuum;
- approval does not override readiness;
- human authority is also bounded;
- return to AI requires revalidation.

Estas ideas se consolidaron en `01-rag-produccion`.
