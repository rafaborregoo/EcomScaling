## Redundancia consolidada

El documento repite deliberadamente:

- execution is not monitoring;
- observe before react;
- performance and safety must coexist;
- monitoring must include context;
- stale data is dangerous;
- monitoring cadence is risk-dependent;
- loss of observability reduces autonomy;
- pause does not stop monitoring;
- resume requires revalidation;
- monitoring is control, not constant optimization.

Estas ideas se consolidaron en `01-rag-produccion`.
