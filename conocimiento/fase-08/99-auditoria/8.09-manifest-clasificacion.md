### Override

### Containment
- RAG: contain before diagnose.
- Contracts: response type.

- Policies: emergency behavior.
- Tenant context: preauthorization.

### Guardrail Confidence
- RAG: uncertain evaluation.

- Runtime: effective envelope recalculation.

## Redundancia consolidada

El documento repite de forma útil:

- safety overrides performance;
- objective is not guardrail;
- warning is not breach;
- hard guardrails are binding;
- more conservative response wins;
- aggregate effects matter;
- uncertainty reduces autonomy;
- resume needs stabilization;
- guardrails can shrink envelope;
- containment before optimization.

Estas ideas se consolidaron en `01-rag-produccion`.
