# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.16 — Políticas de cierre, handoff y learning handoff

### Principio rector

No cerrar porque “ya no quedan tool calls”.

Cerrar cuando el estado final es suficientemente conocido, seguro, trazable y cualquier responsabilidad restante está controlada.

---

## 1. Execution Finished ≠ Closed

Completion operacional no autoriza cierre automático.

---

## 2. Closed ≠ Successful

No usar CLOSED como sinónimo de SUCCESS.

---

## 3. No Active Critical Actions at Closure

No cerrar con:

- active material writes;
- pending material retry;
- active rollback;
- active recovery.

---

## 4. Critical UNKNOWN Blocks Closure

Salvo handoff explícito permitido por governance.

---

## 5. Verification Status Must Be Explicit

No cerrar ocultando incertidumbre.

---

## 6. Inconclusive Must Remain Inconclusive

No convertir falta de evidence en success.

---

## 7. Final State Must Reflect Reality

No registrar como final lo que se esperaba si el sistema terminó en otro estado.

---

## 8. Critical Incident Blocks Closure

Hasta:

- containment/resolution;
- or explicit safe handoff where permitted.

---

## 9. Active Recovery Blocks Closure

No cerrar durante rollback/recovery activo.

---

## 10. Residual Risk Must Be Known

No cierre silencioso con risk oculto.

---

## 11. Residual Risk Acceptance Must Be Explicit When Required

No inferir aceptación.

---

## 12. No Ownership Vacuum

No cerrar si queda responsabilidad crítica sin owner.

---

## 13. Handoff Requires Acceptance When Material

Mensaje enviado ≠ responsibility transferred.

---

## 14. Handoff Does Not Expand Authority

El receptor solo hereda authority necesaria.

---

## 15. Cleanup Is Part of Closure

No dejar:

- stale locks;
- orphaned reservations;
- temporary permissions;
- unnecessary temporary config.

---

## 16. Temporary Authority Must Be Revoked

Si ya no es necesaria.

---

## 17. Resource Release Must Be Verified When Material

Requested release ≠ released.

---

## 18. Closure Should Be Timely

No mantener intervención activa indefinidamente sin necesidad.

---

## 19. Do Not Close Early to Improve Metrics

No metric gaming de throughput/completion rate.

---

## 20. Do Not Keep Runtime Open for Long-Horizon Learning

Outcome monitoring puede continuar fuera de Fase 8.

---

## 21. Operational Outcome and Business Outcome Must Stay Separate

Evita aprendizaje incorrecto.

---

## 22. No Premature Causal Claims

Observation ≠ causation.

---

## 23. Preserve Failed / Rejected / Blocked Cases

No registrar solo éxitos.

---

## 24. Human Override Is Evidence, Not Ground Truth

Debe evaluarse con outcome posterior.

---

## 25. Learning Handoff Must Preserve Context

No generalizar sin:

- market;
- channel;
- product;
- segment;
- constraints;
- concurrent work.

---

## 26. Closed Intervention Is Immutable Operationally

No nuevas writes dentro del mismo lifecycle.

---

## 27. Append, Do Not Rewrite History

Nueva evidence puede añadirse.

Los hechos originales no deben retroeditarse.

---

## 28. New Work Requires New Intervention

Preserva causalidad, authority y audit trail.

---

## 29. Evidence Minimization

Conservar lo necesario para:

- audit;
- recovery understanding;
- learning.

No retener datos irrelevantes indefinidamente.

---

## 30. Least Necessary Personal Data

El learning handoff no justifica trasladar customer data innecesaria.

---

## 31. Closure Reason Must Be Specific

Evitar `done` o `completed` sin contexto.

---

## 32. Closure Communication Must Be Honest

Debe reflejar:

- partial execution;
- residual risk;
- inconclusive verification;
- ongoing outcome observation.

---

## 33. High-Risk Closure Can Require Human Sign-Off

Según governance.

---

## 34. Low-Risk Closure May Be Automated

Solo cuando gate y verification suficientes se cumplen.

---

## 35. Closure Exception Must Be Explicit

Si se cierra con un pendiente externo:

- owner;
- responsibility;
- follow-up;
- risk;

deben quedar claros.

---

## 36. Final Policy

> **Una intervención no se cierra porque hayan terminado las acciones; se cierra cuando el estado final es suficientemente conocido, seguro, verificado y cualquier responsabilidad restante ha sido transferida explícitamente.**
