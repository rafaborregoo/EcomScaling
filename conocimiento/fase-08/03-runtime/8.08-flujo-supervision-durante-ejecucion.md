# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.08 — Flujo de supervisión durante la ejecución

### Propósito

Este runtime convierte una ejecución activa en un circuito cerrado de control.

---

## 1. Flujo base

1. observe progress;
2. observe tool/system health;
3. observe business KPIs;
4. observe safety KPIs;
5. observe context;
6. observe dependencies;
7. observe envelope;
8. detect anomalies;
9. assess signal quality;
10. interpret against goal/guardrails;
11. choose response;
12. continue / hold / reduce / pause / abort / escalate.

---

## 2. Progress Monitoring

Para cada step:

- update status;
- compare planned vs actual;
- detect lag;
- detect stall.

---

## 3. Tool Health Monitoring

Observar:

- latency;
- failure rate;
- rate-limit pressure;
- degradation.

Si health empeora:

- reduce throughput;
- increase monitoring;
- consider pause.

---

## 4. Business Monitoring

Leer métricas relevantes según monitoring plan.

Comparar contra:

- baseline;
- expected range;
- target;
- guardrails.

---

## 5. Leading / Lagging Handling

No esperar lagging metrics demasiado pronto.

No tomar decisiones rápidas sobre señales que requieren más ventana.

---

## 6. Trend Analysis

Calcular conceptualmente:

- direction;
- velocity;
- acceleration.

---

## 7. Multi-Metric Interpretation

Si señales entran en conflicto:

- return to intent;
- assess risk;
- avoid single-metric decision.

---

## 8. Context Monitoring

Detectar cambios externos que invaliden assumptions del plan.

---

## 9. Continuous Preconditions

Revalidar condiciones críticas que deban mantenerse durante ejecución.

---

## 10. Authorization Monitoring

Detectar revocation o scope change.

---

## 11. Envelope Monitoring

Actualizar:

- consumed;
- remaining;
- exhausted.

Si se agota:

- stop;
- request expansion if allowed.

---

## 12. Human Override

Ante instrucción válida:

- apply immediately according to authority;
- pause/cancel/reduce/re-scope as required.

---

## 13. Dependency Monitoring

Si falla una dependency:

- pause dependent actions;
- localize impact when possible.

---

## 14. Queue Monitoring

Para async operations:

- track backlog;
- throughput;
- failed jobs;
- stuck jobs.

---

## 15. Error Pattern Analysis

Si error localized:

- reduce scope to affected segment.

Si global:

- global pause may be required.

---

## 16. Monitoring Cadence

Adaptar frecuencia a:

- risk;
- damage velocity;
- business sensitivity;
- current health state.

---

## 17. Adaptive Monitoring

Normal:
- standard cadence.

Warning:
- higher cadence.

Critical:
- near-real-time / pause.

---

## 18. Observation Window

No introducir nuevas mutaciones durante una ventana de medición salvo que exista riesgo suficiente para intervenir.

---

## 19. Threshold Persistence

Cuando policy lo requiera:

- require persistence across windows before acting.

---

## 20. Debounce / Hysteresis

Aplicar para evitar oscillation cerca de thresholds.

---

## 21. Signal Confidence

Antes de actuar:

- evaluate source quality;
- data freshness;
- sample size;
- cross-source consistency.

---

## 22. Monitoring Blindness

Si se pierde visibilidad crítica:

- reduce autonomy;
- reduce scope;
- hold/pause according to risk.

---

## 23. Health State

Mantener un estado conceptual:

- healthy;
- watch;
- degraded;
- critical.

---

## 24. Alert Generation

Para warning/high/critical:

- summarize signal;
- explain why material;
- include affected scope;
- include suggested response.

---

## 25. Alert Fatigue Control

No escalar informational events que no requieren decisión.

---

## 26. Automatic Response

Si policy permite respuesta preautorizada:

- execute containment/hold/reduce.

---

## 27. Human Response

Si requiere humano:

- notify;
- include decision context;
- wait or pause as policy requires.

---

## 28. Drift Monitoring

Comparar continuamente:

- current behavior;
- approved plan;
- active envelope;
- execution intent.

---

## 29. Runaway Detection

Detectar:

- writes above expected volume;
- repeated same action;
- scope expansion;
- retries without verification.

---

## 30. Write Kill Switch

Ante runaway or critical anomaly:

- disable mutations;
- keep read/diagnostic capabilities when safe.

---

## 31. Monitoring During Pause

Seguir observando:

- system recovery;
- metrics;
- dependencies;
- context.

---

## 32. Resume

Antes de reanudar:

- revalidate current state;
- context;
- scope;
- authorization;
- dependency health;
- monitoring health.

---

## 33. Recovery Monitoring

Si rollback/recovery inicia:

- track actual recovery;
- verify safe state;
- do not equate initiated with recovered.

---

## 34. Cross-Source Comparison

Cuando fuentes discrepan:

- lower confidence;
- investigate authority/source freshness.

---

## 35. Aggregate Monitoring

Observar cumulative exposure e impacto global de la intervención.

---

## 36. Portfolio Monitoring

Cuando varias intervenciones se solapan:

- assess aggregate impact.

---

## 37. Decision Matrix

Normal:
- continue.

Warning:
- hold / increase observation.

Guardrail breach:
- reduce / pause.

Critical:
- abort / contain / escalate.

---

## 38. Monitoring-Action Latency

Medir el tiempo entre:

- detection;
- protective response.

---

## 39. High-Velocity Risk

Si damage velocity supera capacidad humana de respuesta:

- use preauthorized containment if available.

---

## 40. Intervention Timeline

Registrar:

- action;
- observed response;
- warning;
- decision;
- next action.

---

## 41. Final Transition

La supervisión no ejecuta ciegamente.

Convierte:

**EXECUTION ACTIVE**

en:

**OBSERVE → INTERPRET → DECIDE → CONTROLLED NEXT STATE**
