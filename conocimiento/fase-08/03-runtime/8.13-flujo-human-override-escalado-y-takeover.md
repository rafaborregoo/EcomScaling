# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.13 — Flujo de Human Override, escalado y takeover

### Propósito

Este runtime decide cuándo reducir autonomía, cómo transferir control a una persona y cómo devolverlo de forma segura.

---

## 1. Flujo principal

1. decision required;
2. check AI authority;
3. check risk;
4. check certainty;
5. check policy conflicts;
6. check novelty/incident/judgment;
7. continue autonomously or escalate;
8. maintain safe state;
9. route to correct authority;
10. receive human decision;
11. validate authority;
12. apply decision;
13. verify result;
14. revalidate;
15. resume / close / takeover.

---

## 2. Authority Boundary

If outside delegated authority:

- do not execute;
- escalate.

---

## 3. Risk Boundary

If risk exceeds autonomy ceiling:

- reduce or pause;
- escalate.

---

## 4. Uncertainty Boundary

If high-risk state is uncertain:

- hold;
- gather evidence;
- escalate when judgment/authority required.

---

## 5. Policy Conflict

If policies conflict and precedence is unclear:

- pause affected actions;
- escalate conflict.

---

## 6. Novelty Boundary

For novel failure mode:

- reduce autonomy;
- increase monitoring;
- escalate if material.

---

## 7. Judgment Boundary

If decision requires commercial/legal/reputational judgment:

- prepare options;
- route to correct human.

---

## 8. Specialized Routing

Resolve escalation target by domain:

- marketing;
- finance;
- operations;
- legal;
- security;
- engineering;
- management.

---

## 9. Least Necessary Human Authority

Choose lowest level with sufficient decision rights.

---

## 10. Build Decision-Ready Package

Include:

- issue;
- current state;
- risk;
- scope;
- options;
- recommendation;
- explicit question.

---

## 11. Safe State While Waiting

Depending on risk:

- continue safe independent work;
- hold;
- pause;
- maintain containment;
- monitor only.

---

## 12. Escalation State

Create/update conceptual state:

- OPEN;
- ACKNOWLEDGED;
- DECIDED;
- APPLIED;
- CLOSED.

---

## 13. Deduplicate

If same issue continues:

- update existing escalation rather than create storm.

---

## 14. Aggregate Related Events

Group related signals when they represent one decision.

---

## 15. Human Response Timeout

If no response:

- maintain safe behavior;
- escalate further if critical and policy permits;
- never infer approval.

---

## 16. Human Decision Validation

Verify:

- actor identity;
- authority;
- domain;
- scope;
- freshness;
- policy compatibility.

---

## 17. Partial Approval

Apply only approved subset.

---

## 18. Conditional Approval

Translate conditions into active runtime constraints.

---

## 19. Modified Approval

Replace requested parameters with actual approved values.

---

## 20. Rejection

Stop corresponding action.

---

## 21. Deferred

Move to hold/wait state.

---

## 22. Override: Pause

Request pause and verify actual stop.

---

## 23. Override: Restrict

Shrink scope/envelope.

---

## 24. Override: Expand

Require stronger authority check before expanding.

---

## 25. Override: Modify

Update effective plan and revalidate dependencies/readiness.

---

## 26. Override: Abort

Transition to safe abort flow.

---

## 27. Override: Rollback

Enter recovery flow with authority validation.

---

## 28. Takeover

On human takeover:

- suspend AI writes;
- keep monitoring/assist/report if allowed;
- mark human ownership active.

---

## 29. Handoff

Conceptual flow:

AI CONTROL  
→ HANDOFF REQUESTED  
→ HUMAN ACKNOWLEDGED  
→ HUMAN CONTROL

---

## 30. No Control Vacuum

Until acceptance:

- maintain containment;
- preserve monitoring;
- retain current safety owner.

---

## 31. Single Effective Controller

For critical targets:

- prevent simultaneous conflicting writes by human and AI.

---

## 32. Emergency Stop

On valid stop:

- prioritize over normal autonomous actions;
- disable affected writes quickly;
- verify actual stop.

---

## 33. Failed Emergency Stop

If writes continue:

- classify as critical incident;
- escalate immediately.

---

## 34. Override Race

If pause and AI write race:

- human stop wins where authorized;
- reconcile final state.

---

## 35. Command Freshness

Ignore stale resume/pause commands superseded by newer valid state.

---

## 36. Challenge Function

If human instruction conflicts with hard boundary:

- block;
- explain;
- escalate to higher authority if applicable.

---

## 37. Dual Control

If action requires two approvers:

- hold until required independent approvals exist.

---

## 38. Break-Glass

If emergency policy invoked:

- verify high authority;
- limit scope;
- limit duration;
- record reason;
- trigger post-event review.

---

## 39. Autonomy Revocation

After incident:

- disable/restrict affected capabilities;
- do not restore automatically unless policy allows.

---

## 40. Selective Reduction

Prefer local autonomy reduction when risk is localized.

---

## 41. Human Action Detection

During takeover:

- observe manual changes;
- update current state;
- invalidate stale plan if needed.

---

## 42. Hand Back

Flow:

HUMAN CONTROL  
→ HAND BACK  
→ REVALIDATE STATE/PLAN/SCOPE/GUARDRAILS/PERMISSIONS  
→ AI CONTROL

---

## 43. Replanning

If human changes materially altered context:

- create new/revised plan before AI resumes.

---

## 44. Override Expiry

When temporary override expires:

- restore normal policy;
- revalidate before new execution.

---

## 45. Escalation Closure

Close only when:

- decision exists;
- applied state confirmed;
- no unresolved decision remains.

---

## 46. Output

Runtime should always be able to answer:

- who owns control;
- why autonomy stopped;
- who must decide;
- what safe state is active;
- what decision is pending;
- whether AI may resume.
