# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.09 — Flujo de evaluación y respuesta de guardrails

### Propósito

Este runtime transforma señales de monitoring en decisiones de control.

---

## 1. Flujo base

1. receive monitoring signal;
2. validate signal;
3. load active guardrails;
4. evaluate applicable conditions;
5. classify severity;
6. resolve conflicts/priorities;
7. check policy and response authority;
8. select response;
9. execute response;
10. verify safe state;
11. record event.

---

## 2. Signal Validation

Antes de actuar:

- check freshness;
- source health;
- confidence;
- sample sufficiency;
- cross-source consistency.

---

## 3. Active Guardrail Resolution

Resolver qué guardrails aplican por:

- tenant;
- intervention;
- action;
- channel;
- object;
- environment.

---

## 4. Condition Evaluation

Evaluar:

- absolute;
- relative;
- rate-of-change;
- trend;
- persistence;
- instant;
- compound rules.

---

## 5. State Classification

Mapear a un estado conceptual:

- normal;
- warning;
- degraded;
- unsafe;
- critical.

---

## 6. Priority Resolution

Si varias reglas se activan:

- legal/safety first;
- financial constraints next;
- operational constraints;
- business performance;
- optimization last.

---

## 7. Most Restrictive Response

Cuando respuestas válidas entren en conflicto:

- prefer more conservative safe response.

---

## 8. Continue

Si condiciones permanecen sanas:

- continue current plan;
- do not expand automatically.

---

## 9. Hold

Si señal es incierta o insuficiente:

- freeze expansion;
- keep current safe state;
- collect more evidence.

---

## 10. Reduce

Si deterioro moderado:

- reduce scope/magnitude/batch/exposure.

---

## 11. Pause

Si safety uncertainty material:

- stop new material actions;
- keep monitoring.

---

## 12. Abort

Si intervention no longer acceptable:

- terminate further execution.

---

## 13. Rollback

Si safe state anterior es recuperable y policy lo exige:

- invoke rollback flow.

---

## 14. Escalate

Si respuesta requerida excede autonomy:

- notify correct authority;
- preserve safe state.

---

## 15. Containment

Ante critical breach:

- contain first;
- diagnose after.

---

## 16. Safety Mode

Durante incidentes:

- no expansion;
- no nonessential writes;
- read + containment only.

---

## 17. Conditional Authority Collapse

Si una condición de autorización deja de cumplirse:

- shrink effective envelope;
- block affected actions.

---

## 18. Guardrail Tightening

Ante degradación:

- reduce action space;
- reduce frequency;
- reduce autonomous options.

---

## 19. Guardrail Relaxation

Solo tras:

- stable recovery;
- valid authority;
- resume conditions satisfied.

---

## 20. Resume Check

Antes de reanudar:

- current signal healthy;
- stabilization achieved;
- authorization valid;
- monitoring healthy;
- context revalidated.

---

## 21. Hysteresis

Usar thresholds distintos para stop y resume cuando policy lo requiera.

---

## 22. Cooldown

Después de reduce/pause:

- wait before further adaptation unless emergency.

---

## 23. Recheck Before Retry

Antes de retry material:

- state;
- scope;
- active guardrails;
- remaining authority.

---

## 24. Runaway Protection

Evaluar guardrails sobre:

- action count;
- tool-call volume;
- retry count;
- selection size;
- scope drift.

---

## 25. Human Intervention

Si humano modifica target:

- pause conflicting writes;
- re-read;
- reconcile;
- replan if needed.

---

## 26. Missing Verification

Si último cambio no está verificado:

- block expansion.

---

## 27. Unknown State

Si outcome crítico está UNKNOWN:

- block non-idempotent continuation.

---

## 28. Recovery Readiness

Si rollback/recovery capability desaparece:

- reduce exposure;
- block risky progression.

---

## 29. Human Availability

Si high-risk stage requiere humano y no está disponible:

- do not enter stage.

---

## 30. Business Capacity

Aplicar guardrails cross-domain:

- fulfilment;
- support;
- cash;
- stock;
- customer experience.

---

## 31. Observability Guardrail

Si signal source falla:

- mark rule blind;
- reduce autonomy;
- pause adaptive execution where necessary.

---

## 32. Guardrail Action Verification

Después de protective action:

- read/observe state;
- confirm containment.

---

## 33. Failed Protection

Si pause/containment no funciona:

- severity up;
- incident/escalation.

---

## 34. Guardrail Versioning

Evaluar siempre con versión vigente y conservar la usada.

---

## 35. Guardrail Event Record

Registrar:

- trigger;
- threshold;
- value;
- severity;
- response;
- result.

---

## 36. Final Decision Ladder

**HEALTHY → CONTINUE**  
**UNCERTAIN → HOLD**  
**DEGRADED → REDUCE**  
**UNSAFE → PAUSE**  
**CRITICAL → ABORT / ROLLBACK / CONTAIN / ESCALATE**
