# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.07 — Flujo de ejecución segura y control de cambios

### Propósito

Este runtime aplica cambios reales de forma controlada, incremental y verificable.

---

## 1. Flujo base

1. receive ready action;
2. read current state;
3. verify target identity;
4. revalidate authorization and envelope;
5. capture pre-state;
6. minimize mutation;
7. estimate blast radius;
8. select rollout/batch strategy;
9. pre-commit validate;
10. commit;
11. read-after-write;
12. verify final state;
13. record actual change;
14. detect deviation;
15. continue / pause / abort.

---

## 2. Minimum Mutation

Antes de ejecutar:

- identificar campos estrictamente necesarios;
- preferir patch frente a full replacement;
- evitar side changes.

---

## 3. Blast Radius Control

Ajustar:

- scope;
- batch;
- rollout;
- duration;
- magnitude;

al nivel de riesgo.

---

## 4. Canary / Pilot

Para cambios relevantes:

- start small;
- verify;
- expand only if conditions remain valid.

---

## 5. Current-State Read

Antes de write:

- read current state;
- compare with expected state;
- detectar state drift.

---

## 6. Target Verification

Confirmar:

- tenant;
- account;
- object;
- environment.

---

## 7. Scope Revalidation

Justo antes de la mutación:

- comprobar envelope vigente;
- confirmar remaining authority.

---

## 8. Pre-State Capture

Conservar snapshot o evidencia suficiente del estado previo cuando el riesgo lo requiera.

---

## 9. Idempotency Check

Determinar:

- idempotent?;
- duplicate possible?;
- at-most-once?;
- safe to retry?.

---

## 10. Pre-Commit Validation

Antes de commit:

- target;
- scope;
- authorization;
- parameters;
- dependencies;
- freeze/window;
- current state.

---

## 11. Commit

Aplicar únicamente el cambio aprobado y previsto.

---

## 12. Read-After-Write

Después:

- read state;
- compare;
- confirm expected mutation.

---

## 13. Unknown Outcome Flow

Si request outcome es ambiguo:

- mark UNKNOWN;
- do not assume failure;
- inspect current state;
- decide retry only after verification.

---

## 14. Retry Control

Retries:

- bounded;
- conditional;
- only when retry-safe.

---

## 15. Duplicate Prevention

Para sensitive actions:

- check previous execution evidence;
- check desired state;
- prevent duplicate transaction.

---

## 16. Safe Sequencing

Aplicar:

**prepare → verify → activate**

cuando reduzca riesgo.

---

## 17. Coupled Change Execution

Si varias mutaciones deben permanecer sincronizadas:

- tratar como logical group;
- verificar consistencia antes de continuar.

---

## 18. Cross-System Execution

Preferir:

- mutate source of truth;
- verify source;
- wait expected propagation;
- verify downstream.

---

## 19. Batch Flow

1. preview target set;
2. choose batch size;
3. execute batch;
4. verify;
5. stop on anomaly;
6. expand batch only if safe.

---

## 20. Unexpected Selection

Si count real difiere materialmente del esperado:

- stop before commit;
- investigate.

---

## 21. Change Window

Antes de write:

- check execution window;
- check freeze;
- apply emergency exception only if policy permits.

---

## 22. Collision Detection

Antes de overwrite:

- detect recent human/agent/automation changes;
- re-read;
- compare;
- replan if needed.

---

## 23. Change Lock

Cuando exista soporte:

- acquire minimal lock;
- hold only as long as necessary;
- release after safe boundary.

---

## 24. Checkpoint Loop

Después de cada paso material:

**ACTION → VERIFY → DECIDE**

---

## 25. Stop Condition

Si un guardrail o checkpoint marca stop:

- do not continue.

---

## 26. Pause

Usar cuando:

- investigation needed;
- state uncertain;
- context may recover.

---

## 27. Abort

Usar cuando:

- continuing creates unacceptable risk;
- intervention no longer valid.

---

## 28. Safe Stop

No detener mid-operation si eso deja un estado peor.

Llegar primero a atomic boundary cuando sea seguro.

---

## 29. Partial Execution

Si parte del cambio se aplica:

- record completed;
- failed;
- unknown;
- current consistency state.

---

## 30. Partial Success Decision

Dependiendo del plan:

- retry failed;
- rollback successful;
- accept degraded;
- escalate.

---

## 31. Change Journal

Registrar durante ejecución:

- action;
- previous state;
- request;
- result;
- new state;
- timestamp;
- verification.

---

## 32. Planned vs Actual

Comparar después de cada cambio importante.

---

## 33. Deviation Detection

Si la tool produce una mutación no prevista:

- pause;
- investigate;
- escalate if material.

---

## 34. Throttle

Aplicar rate limit operacional aunque la plataforma permita mayor throughput.

---

## 35. Cumulative Effect Recheck

Después de cada mutación:

- recalculate consumed envelope;
- recalculate remaining authority.

---

## 36. Anti-Circumvention

Detectar secuencias destinadas a superar indirectamente cumulative limits.

---

## 37. Cooldown

Tras cambios sensibles:

- wait observation window;
- avoid immediate counter-change unless guardrail requires it.

---

## 38. Anti-Thrashing

No alternar decisiones por ruido corto.

Aplicar thresholds/hysteresis cuando corresponda.

---

## 39. Containment Flow

Ante incidente:

1. reduce scope;
2. apply low-complexity containment;
3. verify containment;
4. switch to safe/degraded mode;
5. diagnose later.

---

## 40. Safety Mode

Reducir a:

- read-only;
- containment actions;
- low-risk writes.

---

## 41. Emergency Change

Debe conservar:

- reason;
- scope;
- result;
- evidence.

---

## 42. Plan Fidelity

Ejecutar la versión aprobada.

Si se necesita adaptación fuera del decision space:

- pause;
- replan;
- reapprove.

---

## 43. Execution Drift

Comparar continuamente:

- actual actions;
- approved plan;
- active envelope.

---

## 44. Handoff During Execution

Antes de transferir:

- stabilize;
- document completed/pending;
- provide current state;
- highlight risks.

---

## 45. Safe Resume

Antes de continuar:

- re-read current state;
- revalidate authorization;
- revalidate envelope;
- identify last verified checkpoint;
- avoid duplicate completed steps.

---

## 46. Completion Check

No cerrar hasta que:

- actions accounted for;
- desired state verified;
- no unresolved critical error.
