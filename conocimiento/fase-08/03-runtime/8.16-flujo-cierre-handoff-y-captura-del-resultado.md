# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.16 — Flujo de cierre, handoff y captura del resultado

### Propósito

Este runtime decide cuándo una intervención puede abandonar control operacional activo y prepara su transferencia a Fase 9 u otro owner.

---

## 1. Flujo principal

1. detect execution completion;
2. confirm no active critical actions;
3. capture final actual state;
4. load verification result;
5. inspect incidents;
6. inspect recovery;
7. inspect residual risks;
8. inspect pending dependencies;
9. clean operational artifacts;
10. release resources;
11. perform/confirm handoffs;
12. build outcome package;
13. evaluate closure gate;
14. transition to CLOSED;
15. transfer learning package to Phase 9.

---

## 2. Execution Completion

Confirm:

- no required action still running;
- no material retry pending;
- no rollback/recovery still active.

If not:
- remain active.

---

## 3. Critical Unknown Check

If critical action state is UNKNOWN:

- reconcile/verify;
- or explicit handoff if governance permits.

Do not silently close.

---

## 4. Verification Check

Load final verification status:

- verified;
- partially verified;
- failed;
- inconclusive.

Status must be explicit.

---

## 5. Final State Capture

Read and persist conceptually:

- current real state;
- actual scope;
- relevant constraints.

Do not substitute expected state for current reality.

---

## 6. Incident Check

If critical incident remains active:

- block full closure;
- resolve or handoff according to policy.

---

## 7. Recovery Check

If rollback/recovery is running:

- remain active.

If recovery completed:
- verify safe state.

---

## 8. Residual Risk Assessment

Identify:

- remaining risk;
- severity;
- acceptability;
- owner.

---

## 9. Residual Work Classification

Ask:

- is remaining work critical to original objective?
- does it require current intervention to remain active?

If no:
- create/prepare follow-up handoff.

---

## 10. Cleanup Phase

Perform, when applicable:

- release locks;
- release reserved capacity;
- revoke temporary permissions;
- remove temporary configuration;
- clear scheduled/pending operational artifacts.

---

## 11. Cleanup Verification

Verify important releases:

- lock gone;
- reservation gone;
- temporary authority revoked.

---

## 12. Handoff Need

If responsibility remains:

- build handoff package;
- route to correct owner.

---

## 13. Handoff Request

Set conceptual state:

- handoff requested.

Keep ownership until acceptance if critical.

---

## 14. Handoff Acceptance

Confirm receiving actor:

- accepted responsibility;
- has necessary context;
- has appropriate authority.

---

## 15. No Ownership Vacuum

If critical residual work has no accepted owner:

- closure blocked.

---

## 16. Phase 9 Learning Handoff

Build package containing:

- recommendation;
- context;
- effective plan;
- actual execution;
- verification;
- incidents;
- recovery;
- observed outcome;
- confidence;
- known unknowns.

---

## 17. Outcome Maturity

Classify current business evidence:

- early;
- mature;
- incomplete.

Do not keep Phase 8 runtime open only to wait for long-horizon learning.

---

## 18. Concurrent Context

Attach known concurrent interventions/confounders relevant to interpretation.

---

## 19. Operational Outcome

Classify actual execution honestly:

- executed and verified;
- executed with deviations;
- partially executed;
- aborted before effect;
- rolled back;
- failed;
- not executed;
- inconclusive.

---

## 20. Closure Reason

Record specific reason.

Avoid generic `done`.

---

## 21. Closure Quality

Classify conceptually:

- clean;
- with residuals;
- degraded;
- inconclusive.

---

## 22. Risk Acceptance

If closing with residual risk:

- require explicit accepted risk when policy demands;
- record owner and monitoring expectations.

---

## 23. Conditional Closure

If external dependency remains:

- confirm system safe;
- confirm owner;
- confirm follow-up;
- then allow conditional closure if policy permits.

---

## 24. Closure Gate

Evaluate:

- no active critical actions;
- final state known;
- verification status known;
- no unresolved critical incident without handoff;
- no active recovery;
- cleanup complete;
- residual risk handled;
- handoff complete;
- learning evidence captured.

---

## 25. Closure Decision

If gate passes:

**COMPLETED → CLOSURE_READY → CLOSING → CLOSED**

If not:
- remain active;
- specify blocking condition.

---

## 26. Human Sign-Off

For high-risk intervention:

- request sign-off when configured.

---

## 27. Low-Risk Auto-Closure

Low-risk fully verified interventions may close automatically if policy permits.

---

## 28. Closure Timeout

If stuck in VERIFYING/CLOSING:

- identify bottleneck;
- escalate or close as INCONCLUSIVE with valid handoff if allowed.

---

## 29. Closed-State Enforcement

After CLOSED:

- reject new execution writes inside same intervention;
- allow read/audit/learning.

---

## 30. Post-Closure Evidence

New observations:

- append as later evidence;
- do not rewrite original execution facts.

---

## 31. Outcome Revision

Business outcome may evolve:

- early → mature;
- inconclusive → positive/negative.

Operational closure remains stable.

---

## 32. New Work

Create linked intervention instead of mutating closed one.

---

## 33. Archive Transition

Closed work may later be archived conceptually for historical storage/search.

---

## 34. Output

At closure runtime must answer:

- why it is safe to close;
- what actually happened;
- what state remains;
- what risk remains;
- who owns anything pending;
- what Phase 9 receives.
