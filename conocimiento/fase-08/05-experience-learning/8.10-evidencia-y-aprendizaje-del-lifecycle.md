# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.10 — Evidencia y aprendizaje del lifecycle

### Alcance

El lifecycle genera evidencia operacional valiosa para Fase 9.

No convierte estados concretos de un tenant en reglas globales.

---

## 1. State History

Analizar:

- sequence;
- duration;
- actor;
- reason;
- transition outcome.

---

## 2. Time-in-State

Estados prolongados pueden revelar:

- process bottlenecks;
- approval latency;
- tool issues;
- recovery weakness.

---

## 3. Stuck State Patterns

Ejemplos:

- blocked too long;
- rollback too long;
- handoff pending too long.

---

## 4. Invalid Transition Attempts

Pueden revelar:

- weak runtime controls;
- bad agent reasoning;
- stale events;
- policy gaps.

---

## 5. Unknown State Frequency

Muchos UNKNOWN indican:

- poor observability;
- non-idempotent risk;
- weak verification.

---

## 6. Retry Patterns

Analizar:

- retries from correct state;
- duplicate prevention;
- recovery rate;
- escalation rate.

---

## 7. Blocked vs Failed Calibration

Puede evaluarse si problemas temporales fueron clasificados correctamente.

---

## 8. Degraded State Outcomes

Analizar cuándo DEGRADED:

- recovered;
- paused;
- failed;
- completed safely.

---

## 9. Pause Effectiveness

Medir si pause:

- contained risk;
- enabled recovery;
- produced excessive downtime.

---

## 10. Resume Quality

Incidentes tras resume pueden indicar:

- insufficient revalidation;
- stale assumptions;
- wrong state restoration.

---

## 11. Rollback Lifecycle Quality

Analizar:

- requested;
- duration;
- success;
- failure;
- verification.

---

## 12. Compensation Outcomes

Comparar mitigación con expected recovery.

---

## 13. Verification Failure

Casos donde COMPLETED no llegó a VERIFIED revelan:

- execution defects;
- side effects;
- stale assumptions.

---

## 14. Closure Quality

Cierres con issues posteriores pueden indicar closure gate débil.

---

## 15. Handoff Quality

Analizar:

- pending time;
- acceptance latency;
- control gaps;
- context completeness.

---

## 16. State Transition Latency

Puede medirse desde event hasta actual state confirmation.

---

## 17. Orphaned Interventions

Son señal crítica de ownership/governance defectuoso.

---

## 18. Replanning Frequency

Puede indicar:

- volatile context;
- weak initial plan;
- excessive long-running interventions.

---

## 19. Execution vs Business Outcome

Mantener separados:

- lifecycle success;
- execution success;
- business result;
- strategic result.

---

## 20. Inconclusive Outcomes

No etiquetar como failure cuando falta evidencia suficiente.

---

## 21. Tenant-Bound Learning

La frecuencia y utilidad de estados/transiciones pertenece inicialmente al contexto que los produjo.

No debe generalizarse automáticamente.
