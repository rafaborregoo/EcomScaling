# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.13 — Requisitos contractuales de Human Override y escalado

> Documento conceptual. No define schemas, tablas, APIs, roles técnicos ni workflows concretos.

---

## 1. Escalation Need

Debe poder representarse:

- qué decisión no puede tomar la IA;
- por qué;
- qué boundary se alcanzó.

---

## 2. Escalation Boundary Type

Debe poder distinguir:

- authority;
- risk;
- uncertainty;
- policy;
- novelty;
- incident;
- judgment;
- conflict.

---

## 3. Escalation Target

Debe poder representar qué autoridad humana es apropiada.

---

## 4. Decision Rights

Debe poder asociarse conceptualmente:

- decision domain;
- required authority;
- available human authority.

---

## 5. Escalation Level

Debe poder representar niveles conceptuales de escalado.

---

## 6. Escalation Package

Debe poder incluir:

- what happened;
- current state;
- why autonomy stopped;
- risk;
- impact;
- options;
- recommendation;
- decision needed.

---

## 7. Escalation Urgency

Debe poder distinguir:

- informational;
- decision required;
- urgent decision required.

---

## 8. Blocking Nature

Debe poder indicar si la escalada:

- blocks all execution;
- blocks affected scope only;
- is non-blocking.

---

## 9. Safe State While Waiting

Debe poder especificar:

- hold;
- pause;
- containment;
- continued monitoring;
- reduced operation.

---

## 10. Escalation Owner

Debe poder existir un current responsible party.

---

## 11. Human Acknowledgement

Debe poder registrarse si la persona:

- has not seen;
- acknowledged;
- decided.

---

## 12. Escalation Lifecycle

Debe poder representar conceptualmente:

- open;
- acknowledged;
- decided;
- applied;
- closed.

---

## 13. Decision Deadline

Debe poder asociarse un límite temporal cuando la decisión pierde valor o aumenta riesgo.

---

## 14. Escalation Expiry

Debe poder representarse una decisión o solicitud stale.

---

## 15. Escalation Deduplication

Debe poder agrupar eventos repetidos del mismo problema.

---

## 16. Escalation Aggregation

Debe poder consolidar eventos relacionados de un mismo incidente.

---

## 17. Severity Update

Debe poder actualizar urgency/criticality sin crear escalaciones duplicadas innecesarias.

---

## 18. Human Decision

Debe poder representar outcomes como:

- approved;
- conditionally approved;
- modified;
- rejected;
- deferred;
- taken over.

---

## 19. Partial Decision

Debe poder indicar qué objetos/acciones fueron:

- approved;
- rejected;
- held.

---

## 20. Conditional Decision

Debe poder asociar condiciones que se convierten en runtime constraints.

---

## 21. Override Request / Override Action

Debe poder distinguir:

- requested override;
- observed human action.

---

## 22. Override Type

Debe poder representar:

- pause;
- resume;
- restrict;
- expand;
- modify;
- abort;
- rollback;
- takeover.

---

## 23. Override Scope

Debe poder expresar:

- object;
- action;
- intervention;
- capability;
- account;
- system.

---

## 24. Override Duration

Debe poder representar:

- one-time;
- temporary;
- stage-scoped;
- persistent until revoked.

---

## 25. Override Reason

Debe poder conservarse una razón material.

---

## 26. Override Authority

Debe poder comprobar:

- actor identity;
- role/authority;
- scope of authority.

---

## 27. Override Precedence

Debe poder relacionarse con:

- higher policy;
- legal constraint;
- security boundary;
- organizational hierarchy.

---

## 28. Authority Conflict

Debe poder representar instrucciones humanas incompatibles.

---

## 29. Conflict Escalation

Debe poder comunicar:

- instruction A;
- instruction B;
- why incompatible;
- decision needed.

---

## 30. Takeover State

Debe poder representar:

- AI execution suspended;
- AI monitoring active;
- human control active.

---

## 31. Control Ownership

Debe poder indicar quién tiene control efectivo.

---

## 32. Handoff

Debe poder representar:

- handoff requested;
- handoff accepted;
- hand-back requested;
- hand-back accepted.

---

## 33. Control Vacuum Prevention

Debe poder mantenerse ownership hasta que el receptor acepte.

---

## 34. Emergency Stop

Debe poder representar activación de stop y su scope.

---

## 35. Kill Switch Scope

Debe poder aplicarse conceptualmente a:

- action;
- capability;
- agent;
- intervention;
- system.

---

## 36. Emergency Stop Verification

Debe poder registrar si writes realmente se detuvieron.

---

## 37. Failed Stop

Debe poder representar que la orden de stop no alcanzó el estado deseado.

---

## 38. Break-Glass

Debe poder representar un override excepcional con:

- high authority;
- explicit reason;
- limited duration;
- auditability.

---

## 39. Autonomy State

Debe poder representar reducción o suspensión de autonomía.

---

## 40. Capability Revocation

Debe poder identificar qué capability quedó:

- active;
- restricted;
- approval-required;
- disabled.

---

## 41. Scope Revocation

Debe poder indicar qué objetos/markets/actions quedan fuera.

---

## 42. Revalidation Requirement

Debe poder marcar que un override obliga a volver a comprobar:

- readiness;
- scope;
- plan;
- guardrails;
- permissions.

---

## 43. Plan Version

Debe poder relacionarse una modificación humana con una nueva versión efectiva del plan.

---

## 44. Human Risk Acceptance

Debe poder conservar:

- accepted risk;
- bounded scope;
- duration;
- approving authority.

---

## 45. Decision Application

Debe poder distinguir:

- decision made;
- decision applied;
- desired state verified.

---

## 46. Human Action as External Mutation

Debe poder registrarse qué cambió manualmente durante takeover.

---

## 47. Return of Control

Debe poder representar:

- human control;
- hand back;
- revalidation;
- AI control restored.

---

## 48. Escalation Evidence

Debe poder reconstruirse:

**trigger → escalation → human decision → applied state → verification**

---

## 49. Learning Signal

Debe poder conservarse:

- approved;
- modified;
- rejected;
- overridden;
- unnecessary escalation;
- missing escalation.
