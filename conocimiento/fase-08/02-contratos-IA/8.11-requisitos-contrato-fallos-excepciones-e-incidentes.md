# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.11 — Requisitos contractuales de fallos, excepciones e incidentes

> Documento conceptual. No define schemas, tablas, APIs, enums implementados ni sistemas concretos de incident management.

---

## 1. Problem Record

Debe poder representarse conceptualmente un problema detectado con:

- source;
- time;
- affected action/intervention;
- current state;
- observed symptom.

---

## 2. Problem Class

Debe poder distinguirse entre:

- error;
- exception;
- blocker;
- degradation;
- failure;
- incident;
- critical incident.

---

## 3. Error Type

Debe poder clasificarse conceptualmente como:

- technical;
- business;
- authorization;
- data;
- dependency;
- policy;
- concurrency;
- unknown.

---

## 4. Recoverability

Debe poder representar:

- recoverable;
- non-recoverable;
- partially recoverable;
- unknown.

---

## 5. Retryability

Debe poder indicar:

- retryable;
- non-retryable;
- verify-before-retry;
- retry exhausted.

---

## 6. State Knowledge

Debe poder representar:

- known;
- unknown;
- partially known.

---

## 7. Failure Scope

Debe poder distinguir:

- action-level;
- step-level;
- intervention-level;
- system-level.

---

## 8. Local vs Global

Debe poder expresar si el problema es:

- localized;
- clustered;
- global;
- systemic.

---

## 9. Severity

Debe poder representar una clasificación conceptual equivalente a:

- low;
- medium;
- high;
- critical.

---

## 10. Severity Factors

Debe poder asociar:

- impact;
- scope;
- financial exposure;
- customer exposure;
- reversibility;
- duration;
- propagation;
- business criticality.

---

## 11. Urgency

Debe poder mantenerse separada de severity.

---

## 12. Priority

Debe poder expresar prioridad resultante de:

- severity;
- urgency;
- context.

---

## 13. Propagation Risk

Debe poder representar probabilidad o posibilidad conceptual de extensión a otros recursos/sistemas.

---

## 14. Blast Radius

Debe poder conservarse una estimación del alcance potencial.

---

## 15. Time-to-Damage

Debe poder representarse cuánto margen existe para responder.

---

## 16. Failure Domain

Debe poder asociarse:

- tool;
- data;
- permission;
- system;
- business;
- policy;
- human dependency;
- concurrency;
- external dependency.

---

## 17. Origin

Debe poder distinguirse:

- internal;
- external;
- mixed;
- unknown.

---

## 18. Trigger

Debe poder registrarse qué evento expuso el problema.

---

## 19. Root Cause

Debe poder representarse separadamente cuando sea conocida.

---

## 20. Contributing Factors

Debe poder existir más de un factor contribuyente.

---

## 21. Detection Source

Debe poder registrarse si se detectó por:

- tool;
- guardrail;
- anomaly;
- human;
- verification.

---

## 22. Failure Visibility

Debe poder distinguir:

- explicit;
- silent;
- ambiguous.

---

## 23. Unknown Outcome

Debe poder mantenerse explícitamente UNKNOWN.

---

## 24. Partial Success

Debe poder representar:

- succeeded subset;
- failed subset;
- unknown subset;
- current consistency state.

---

## 25. Retry Evidence

Debe poder conservar:

- attempt;
- reason;
- result;
- state before;
- state after.

---

## 26. Retry Budget

Debe poder representar límite y consumo cuando exista.

---

## 27. Incident Lifecycle

Debe poder representar conceptualmente:

- detected;
- triaged;
- contained;
- recovering;
- resolved;
- closed.

---

## 28. Triage Record

Debe poder contener:

- type;
- severity;
- urgency;
- scope;
- owner;
- immediate response.

---

## 29. Incident Owner

Debe poder identificar:

- AI;
- human;
- specialist;
- shared owner.

---

## 30. Escalation

Debe poder representar:

- trigger;
- target authority;
- reason;
- urgency;
- decision required.

---

## 31. Safe State

Debe poder representar qué estado se considera suficientemente seguro durante el incidente.

---

## 32. Incident Mode

Debe poder indicar que normal execution está restringida.

---

## 33. Containment Action

Debe poder registrar:

- action;
- target;
- scope;
- authority;
- result.

---

## 34. Recovery Action

Debe poder distinguir:

- retry;
- repair;
- rollback;
- compensation;
- failover;
- handoff;
- abort.

---

## 35. Failover

Debe poder representar:

- alternative capability;
- semantic equivalence;
- authority compatibility;
- safety compatibility.

---

## 36. Quarantine

Debe poder representar temporalmente:

- tool quarantine;
- capability quarantine;
- scope quarantine.

---

## 37. Incident Evidence

Debe poder conservar:

- inputs;
- target;
- tool;
- error;
- current state;
- affected objects;
- recent changes.

---

## 38. Incident Timeline

Debe poder reconstruir:

- detection;
- warning;
- containment;
- escalation;
- recovery;
- closure.

---

## 39. Response Latency

Debe poder medir conceptualmente:

- detection-to-action.

---

## 40. Recovery Time

Debe poder registrar tiempo hasta safe operation restored.

---

## 41. Severity Revision

Debe poder cambiarse clasificación con nueva evidencia.

---

## 42. Classification Confidence

Debe poder conservarse incertidumbre sobre:

- severity;
- scope;
- cause;
- recoverability.

---

## 43. Containment Status

Debe poder distinguir:

- active risk;
- contained;
- unresolved;
- reactivated.

---

## 44. Resolution Status

Debe poder representar:

- temporary workaround;
- mitigation;
- permanent fix;
- unresolved root cause.

---

## 45. Resolution Criteria

Debe poder comprobar:

- impact contained;
- system safe;
- recovery verified;
- recurrence risk understood as needed.

---

## 46. Incident Communication Package

Debe poder incluir:

- what happened;
- what is affected;
- what has been done;
- current risk;
- decision needed.

---

## 47. Handoff

Debe poder transferirse ownership sin crear control vacuum.

---

## 48. Risk Acceptance

Debe poder representar aceptación humana explícita de riesgo residual cuando sea necesaria.

---

## 49. Learning Handoff

Al cerrar, debe poder entregarse evidencia de:

- failure type;
- cause;
- response;
- recovery;
- impact.

---

## 50. Audit Trace

Debe poder reconstruirse:

**problem → classification → severity → response → recovery → resolution**
