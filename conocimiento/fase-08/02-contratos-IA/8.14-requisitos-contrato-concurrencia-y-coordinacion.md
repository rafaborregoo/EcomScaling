# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.14 — Requisitos contractuales de concurrencia y coordinación

> Documento conceptual. No define schemas, tablas, locks técnicos ni motores de orquestación concretos.

---

## 1. Active Intervention Awareness

Debe poder representarse qué intervenciones están:

- active;
- scheduled;
- paused;
- recovering;
- queued;
- superseded.

---

## 2. Interaction Type

Debe poder distinguir:

- independent;
- compatible;
- complementary;
- dependent;
- competing;
- conflicting;
- mutually exclusive.

---

## 3. Scope Overlap

Debe poder representar:

- no overlap;
- partial overlap;
- full overlap.

---

## 4. Object / Field Overlap

Debe poder expresar:

- shared object;
- shared field;
- distinct field on same object.

---

## 5. Resource Overlap

Debe poder identificar recursos compartidos como:

- budget;
- inventory;
- API quota;
- traffic;
- customer attention;
- warehouse capacity;
- support capacity.

---

## 6. Goal Compatibility

Debe poder representar:

- compatible goals;
- competing goals;
- contradictory goals;
- unknown compatibility.

---

## 7. Dependency

Debe poder representar:

- upstream intervention;
- downstream intervention;
- dependency condition.

---

## 8. Dependency Graph

Debe poder existir un grafo conceptual entre intervenciones.

---

## 9. Shared Preconditions

Debe poder conocerse si varias intervenciones dependen de la misma condición.

---

## 10. Shared Guardrails

Debe poder asociarse un guardrail global a varias intervenciones.

---

## 11. Global Limit

Debe poder representar:

- allowed total;
- current committed;
- current consumed;
- remaining capacity.

---

## 12. Reservation

Debe poder representar:

- resource;
- amount/capacity;
- owner intervention;
- validity window;
- release condition.

---

## 13. Allocation Conflict

Debe poder detectar double allocation.

---

## 14. Lock

Debe poder representar conceptualmente:

- resource;
- lock scope;
- owner;
- mode;
- acquisition time;
- release condition.

---

## 15. Lock Type

Debe poder distinguir:

- exclusive;
- shared/read;
- ownership-only.

---

## 16. Lock Granularity

Debe poder representar nivel:

- tenant;
- account;
- campaign;
- product;
- field;
- capability.

---

## 17. Waiting for Resource

Debe poder representar:

- resource needed;
- current owner;
- queue position/reason;
- revalidation required after acquisition.

---

## 18. Priority

Debe poder representar priority basis:

- severity;
- business impact;
- risk;
- urgency;
- dependency;
- deadline.

---

## 19. Preemption

Debe poder registrar:

- preempted intervention;
- reason;
- preempting intervention;
- safe pause state.

---

## 20. Ownership

Debe poder representar current owner of shared state/domain.

---

## 21. Ownership Transfer

Debe poder representar explicit handoff.

---

## 22. Single Writer

Debe poder representar que un recurso admite un único writer efectivo.

---

## 23. Admission Decision

Debe poder producir conceptualmente:

- admit;
- queue;
- merge;
- defer;
- reject.

---

## 24. Coordination Outcome

Debe poder distinguir:

- safe parallel;
- serialize;
- queue;
- partition;
- merge;
- preempt;
- cancel;
- escalate.

---

## 25. Conflict Reason

Debe poder explicar:

- shared field;
- resource exhaustion;
- incompatible goal;
- measurement contamination;
- common dependency;
- recovery ongoing.

---

## 26. Partition

Debe poder expresar cómo dividir:

- scope;
- object set;
- market;
- audience;
- time window;
- capability ownership.

---

## 27. Merge

Debe poder representar que dos intervenciones pasan a un único plan coordinado.

---

## 28. Merge Constraints

Debe poder conservar diferencias de:

- approval;
- measurement goal;
- ownership;
- authority.

---

## 29. Duplicate Intervention

Debe poder detectar similitud por:

- intent;
- target;
- timeframe.

---

## 30. Supersession

Debe poder relacionarse:

- old intervention;
- new intervention;
- reason;
- effective plan/version.

---

## 31. Superseded State

Debe poder representar que una intervención ya no debe ejecutar.

---

## 32. Event Ordering

Debe poder conservar:

- event time;
- state time;
- causal sequence.

---

## 33. Late Event

Debe poder marcar eventos stale asociados a una intervención superseded.

---

## 34. Shared State Snapshot

Debe poder representar el global current state relevante antes de una decisión.

---

## 35. Compare-and-Change

Debe poder asociarse expected state antes de commit.

---

## 36. Atomic Reservation Need

Debe poder señalar que check+reserve deben tratarse como una unidad lógica.

---

## 37. Coordination Model

Debe poder representar uso de:

- optimistic;
- pessimistic;
- risk-adaptive coordination.

---

## 38. Concurrency Safety Properties

Debe poder asociar si una acción es:

- independent;
- idempotent;
- commutative;
- reversible.

---

## 39. Critical Section

Debe poder representar:

- resource;
- start;
- end;
- protected steps.

---

## 40. Deadlock

Debe poder representar:

- intervention A waits for B;
- intervention B waits for A;
- no progress.

---

## 41. Livelock

Debe poder representar actividad sin progreso útil.

---

## 42. Starvation

Debe poder indicar una intervención que no obtiene acceso durante tiempo excesivo.

---

## 43. Queue

Debe poder representar:

- waiting reason;
- priority;
- aging;
- exit condition.

---

## 44. Scheduling Window

Debe poder asociarse a:

- business calendar;
- maintenance window;
- promotion window;
- future reservation.

---

## 45. Future Conflict

Debe poder conocer scheduled interventions próximas.

---

## 46. Portfolio State

Debe poder representar:

- active count;
- risk concentration;
- shared-resource pressure;
- portfolio health.

---

## 47. Portfolio Guardrail

Debe poder aplicar restricciones agregadas.

---

## 48. Correlated Risk

Debe poder representar dependencias o assumptions comunes.

---

## 49. Common-Mode Incident

Debe poder identificar afectación simultánea por una misma causa.

---

## 50. Coordination Trace

Debe poder reconstruirse:

- who owned;
- what conflict existed;
- why waiting/preemption happened;
- how conflict was resolved.

---

## 51. Mutation Attribution

Debe poder atribuir una mutación a:

- intervention;
- agent;
- human;
- tool.

---

## 52. Cross-Intervention Journal

Debe poder ordenar cambios compartidos en una timeline común.

---

## 53. Verification Context

Debe poder identificar otras mutaciones concurrentes que afecten interpretación.

---

## 54. Learning Context

Debe poder conservar confounders concurrentes para Fase 9.

---

## 55. Output

La coordinación debe proporcionar suficiente información para:

- execute in parallel;
- serialize;
- queue;
- partition;
- merge;
- preempt;
- cancel;
- escalate;
- revalidate.
