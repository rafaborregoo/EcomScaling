# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.16 — Requisitos contractuales de cierre, handoff y resultado

> Documento conceptual. No define schemas, tablas, APIs, enums implementados ni formatos técnicos definitivos.

---

## 1. Completion State

Debe poder distinguirse si:

- actions remain active;
- required actions are terminal;
- execution is operationally finished.

---

## 2. Closure Readiness

Debe poder representar si la intervención cumple los requisitos para entrar en cierre.

---

## 3. Closure State

Debe poder distinguir conceptualmente:

- completed;
- closure ready;
- closing;
- closed.

---

## 4. Closure Gate

Debe poder evaluar:

- no active critical actions;
- no unknown critical state without explicit handoff;
- verification status known;
- incidents known;
- recovery status known;
- residual risk known;
- handoff status known.

---

## 5. Final Execution State

Debe poder registrar el estado real final de la intervención.

---

## 6. Final State Snapshot

Debe poder conservar una representación suficiente de:

- what state exists now;
- what relevant resources remain;
- what constraints remain active.

---

## 7. Final State vs Expected State

Debe poder distinguir:

- expected state;
- final actual state;
- reason for difference.

---

## 8. Execution Outcome

Debe poder clasificar conceptualmente:

- success;
- partial success;
- failed;
- aborted;
- rolled back;
- inconclusive;
- not executed;
- superseded;
- expired.

---

## 9. Closure Reason

Debe poder representar por qué se cerró.

---

## 10. Operational Outcome

Debe poder mantenerse separado de:

- business outcome;
- strategic outcome.

---

## 11. Outcome Maturity

Debe poder distinguir:

- early;
- mature;
- incomplete.

---

## 12. Outcome Observation Window

Debe poder conservar:

- start;
- duration;
- relevant horizon.

---

## 13. Outcome Confidence

Debe poder representarse por separado de execution confidence.

---

## 14. Execution Confidence

Debe poder conservarse la confianza en lo realmente ejecutado.

---

## 15. Verification Confidence

Debe poder conservarse la confianza en la evidencia de verification.

---

## 16. Planned vs Actual

Debe poder comparar:

- scope;
- parameters;
- timing;
- sequence;
- completion.

---

## 17. Deviations

Debe poder representar:

- scope deviation;
- parameter deviation;
- timing deviation;
- sequence deviation;
- no material deviation.

---

## 18. Execution Fidelity

Debe poder representar cuánto coincidió ejecución real con el plan efectivo.

---

## 19. Residual Risk

Debe poder representar:

- remaining risk;
- severity/materiality;
- accepted/not accepted;
- owner;
- monitoring need.

---

## 20. Residual Issue

Debe poder registrar pendientes no críticos.

---

## 21. Residual Action

Debe poder indicar que una futura acción necesita:

- follow-up intervention;
- external task;
- another owner.

---

## 22. Risk Acceptance

Debe poder conservar:

- what risk was accepted;
- who accepted it;
- scope;
- duration/conditions if relevant.

---

## 23. Handoff Need

Debe poder representar si queda responsabilidad que deba transferirse.

---

## 24. Handoff Package

Debe poder incluir conceptualmente:

- current state;
- evidence;
- scope;
- remaining work;
- risks;
- next action;
- intended owner.

---

## 25. Handoff Status

Debe poder distinguir:

- requested;
- accepted;
- rejected/failed;
- complete.

---

## 26. Handoff Owner

Debe poder registrar quién asume responsabilidad.

---

## 27. Authority Transfer

Debe poder expresar si cambia la autoridad operacional y qué límites mantiene.

---

## 28. Delegation vs Handoff

Debe poder mantenerse explícita la diferencia entre:

- delegated work with original ownership;
- transferred responsibility.

---

## 29. Consumer Readiness

Debe poder representar si el receptor dispone de contexto suficiente para actuar.

---

## 30. Phase 9 Handoff

Debe poder entregar:

- recommendation;
- execution context;
- plan;
- actual execution;
- verification;
- outcome;
- residual uncertainty.

---

## 31. Recommendation Link

Debe poder relacionarse la ejecución con la recomendación que la originó.

---

## 32. Execution Intent Link

Debe poder relacionarse con la intención operacional.

---

## 33. Plan Link

Debe poder conocerse el latest valid plan que gobernó la ejecución.

---

## 34. Intervention Identity

Debe poder distinguir cada ejecución concreta.

---

## 35. Intervention Lineage

Debe poder relacionar:

- pilot;
- rollout;
- retry;
- follow-up;
- supersession;
- rollback;
- recovery.

---

## 36. Learning Unit

Debe poder definir cuál es la unidad correcta de evaluación para Fase 9.

---

## 37. Baseline

Debe poder conservarse el estado relevante antes de la intervención.

---

## 38. Context at Execution Time

Debe poder representar:

- business conditions;
- constraints;
- market;
- channel;
- product;
- segment;
- concurrent interventions.

---

## 39. Expected Benefit / Risk

Debe poder conservar:

- expected benefit;
- expected risk.

---

## 40. Actual Benefit / Risk

Debe poder registrar:

- observed benefit;
- observed risk;
- incident evidence.

---

## 41. Incident Summary

Debe poder conservar:

- incident type;
- severity;
- containment;
- resolution/recovery state.

---

## 42. Guardrail Summary

Debe poder registrar:

- guardrails triggered;
- relevant guardrails not triggered;
- final guardrail state.

---

## 43. Recovery Summary

Debe poder representar:

- strategy;
- result;
- residual impact;
- recovery cost.

---

## 44. Human Interaction Summary

Debe poder conservar:

- approvals;
- modifications;
- rejections;
- takeovers;
- risk acceptance;
- human rationale when available.

---

## 45. Cost

Debe poder representar dimensiones relevantes de ejecución y recovery.

---

## 46. Time Metrics

Debe poder conservar:

- time-to-execution;
- execution duration;
- time-to-value;
- time-in-state;
- closure lag.

---

## 47. Blocker Summary

Debe poder representar:

- what blocked;
- duration;
- resolution.

---

## 48. Resource Cleanup

Debe poder representar:

- locks released;
- reservations released;
- temporary permissions revoked;
- temporary configs cleaned.

---

## 49. Cleanup Verification

Debe poder indicar si cleanup se verificó cuando era material.

---

## 50. Open External Dependency

Debe poder representar un pendiente externo transferido.

---

## 51. Conditional Closure

Debe poder distinguir cierre completo de cierre con seguimiento externo.

---

## 52. Closure Quality

Puede representar conceptualmente:

- clean;
- with residuals;
- degraded;
- inconclusive.

---

## 53. Closure Decision Rights

Debe poder asociar quién está autorizado para cerrar según riesgo.

---

## 54. Closure Approval / Sign-Off

Debe poder registrarse si era necesario.

---

## 55. Closure Timeout

Debe poder representar que una intervención no puede permanecer indefinidamente en verification/closing.

---

## 56. Outcome Evidence Type

Debe poder distinguir conceptualmente:

- experimental;
- quasi-experimental;
- observational;
- anecdotal.

---

## 57. Evidence Quality

Debe poder representar calidad/fuerza de evidencia.

---

## 58. Sample / Exposure Context

Debe poder conservar cuando corresponda:

- sample/volume;
- exposure;
- observation duration.

---

## 59. Known Unknowns

Debe poder representar explícitamente qué sigue sin conocerse.

---

## 60. Post-Closure Evidence

Debe poder añadirse nueva evidencia sin reescribir el registro histórico original.

---

## 61. Outcome Revision

Debe poder actualizarse la interpretación del outcome con el tiempo.

---

## 62. Closed-State Invariant

Debe poder representar:

- no new execution writes within the closed intervention.

---

## 63. New Work Link

Debe poder relacionar una nueva intervención con una cerrada.

---

## 64. Audit Trace

Debe poder reconstruirse:

**recommendation → authorization → plan → execution → monitoring → incidents → verification → closure**

---

## 65. Minimal Closure Statement

Debe poder responder claramente:

> Intervention X is closed because...

con una razón específica y trazable.
