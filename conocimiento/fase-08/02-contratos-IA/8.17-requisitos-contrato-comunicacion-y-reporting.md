# FASE 8 — EJECUCIÓN Y SUPERVISIÓN
## 8.17 — Requisitos contractuales de comunicación y reporting

> Documento conceptual. No define schemas, tablas, canales técnicos, APIs ni enums implementados.

---

## 1. Communication Event

Debe poder representarse conceptualmente un evento candidato a comunicación con:

- source event;
- intervention;
- lifecycle state;
- timestamp;
- actor.

---

## 2. Materiality

Debe poder indicar si un evento es:

- internal only;
- material enough to surface.

---

## 3. Communication Type

Debe poder distinguir conceptualmente:

- information;
- status;
- progress;
- decision request;
- risk warning;
- incident alert;
- escalation;
- recovery update;
- closure report.

---

## 4. Audience

Debe poder representar intended recipient class:

- operator;
- manager;
- executive;
- specialist;
- customer-facing role;
- other authorized actor.

---

## 5. Audience Need

Debe poder expresar:

- awareness required;
- action required;
- no human action required.

---

## 6. Severity

Debe poder distinguir conceptualmente:

- info;
- notice;
- warning;
- high;
- critical.

---

## 7. Priority

Debe poder mantenerse separada de severity.

Puede representar:

- low;
- normal;
- high;
- urgent.

---

## 8. Urgency Reason

Debe poder explicar por qué el tiempo importa.

---

## 9. Message Purpose

Cada comunicación debe tener un primary purpose.

---

## 10. Summary

Debe poder existir una capa breve para comprensión rápida.

---

## 11. Details

Debe poder existir una capa operacional ampliada.

---

## 12. Evidence

Debe poder enlazar evidencia suficiente para audit/debugging.

---

## 13. What

Debe poder representar qué ocurrió.

---

## 14. Why

Debe poder representar causa conocida o razón operacional.

---

## 15. Cause Confidence

Debe poder distinguir:

- confirmed;
- probable/suspected;
- unknown.

---

## 16. Current State

Debe poder incluir lifecycle state actual.

---

## 17. Impact

Debe poder representar business/operational impact.

---

## 18. Confirmed Exposure

Debe poder distinguir alcance confirmado.

---

## 19. Potential Exposure

Debe poder distinguir alcance todavía posible/no confirmado.

---

## 20. Current Safety

Debe poder representar conceptualmente:

- safe;
- at risk;
- unknown;
- safe but degraded.

---

## 21. Action Taken

Debe poder indicar qué containment/response ya ocurrió.

---

## 22. Next Step

Debe poder representar:

- automatic next step;
- human action;
- no action required.

---

## 23. Decision Request

Debe poder incluir:

- decision needed;
- authorized decision owner;
- recommendation;
- options;
- risk;
- scope;
- deadline if real.

---

## 24. Approval Scope

Debe poder representar:

- target;
- action;
- value/range;
- stage;
- duration when applicable.

---

## 25. Current vs Proposed

Debe poder mostrar conceptual diff.

---

## 26. Decision State

Debe poder distinguir:

- delivered;
- seen;
- acknowledged;
- decided;
- applied;
- verified.

---

## 27. Decision Freshness

Debe poder indicar si una solicitud sigue siendo válida.

---

## 28. Approval Expiration

Debe poder representar que el contexto invalidó una approval request.

---

## 29. Communication Status

Debe poder distinguir conceptualmente:

- open;
- updated;
- resolved;
- expired;
- superseded.

---

## 30. Correlation

Debe poder relacionar mensajes pertenecientes al mismo incidente/intervención.

---

## 31. Parent Incident

Debe poder representar un root incident con múltiples síntomas/domains.

---

## 32. Deduplication Identity

Debe poder identificar mensajes/eventos duplicados.

---

## 33. Aggregation

Debe poder representar un resumen de eventos relacionados.

---

## 34. Progress

Debe poder representar:

- completed work;
- remaining work;
- milestone.

---

## 35. Milestone

Debe poder asociarse a:

- pilot complete;
- approval received;
- rollout stage;
- verification complete;
- other material boundary.

---

## 36. Plan Change

Debe poder representar:

- previous plan state;
- new plan state;
- reason.

---

## 37. Plan Supersession

Debe poder indicar qué plan ya no es autoritativo.

---

## 38. Warning Context

Debe poder representar:

- signal;
- current value/state;
- threshold;
- current response.

---

## 39. Incident Package

Debe poder incluir:

- impact;
- containment;
- known;
- unknown;
- current exposure;
- owner;
- next decision.

---

## 40. Incident Timeline

Debe poder reconstruir eventos materiales.

---

## 41. Recovery Update

Debe poder representar:

- restored;
- pending;
- failed;
- residual inconsistency.

---

## 42. Closure Report

Debe poder representar:

- goal;
- actual execution;
- verification;
- operational outcome;
- business outcome maturity;
- residuals;
- owner.

---

## 43. Business Outcome

Debe poder mantenerse separado de execution result.

---

## 44. Outcome Maturity

Debe poder representar:

- early;
- mature;
- incomplete.

---

## 45. Confidence

Debe poder conservar:

- execution confidence;
- verification confidence;
- business attribution confidence.

---

## 46. Missing Data

Debe poder identificar métricas/contexto ausente.

---

## 47. Observability Loss

Debe poder representar que reporting/monitoring confidence ha disminuido.

---

## 48. Confounders

Debe poder asociar:

- concurrent intervention;
- promotion;
- seasonality;
- other material context.

---

## 49. Blocked State

Debe poder incluir:

- blocker;
- owner;
- resolution condition;
- changes applied or not.

---

## 50. Hold / Pause / Abort / Rollback / Superseded / Expired

Debe poder preservar semántica distinta para cada estado.

---

## 51. Unknown Execution State

Debe poder representar explícitamente unknown sin false success/failure.

---

## 52. Actor Attribution

Debe poder distinguir:

- AI;
- Human;
- Policy;
- External System.

---

## 53. Partial Failure

Debe poder representar:

- succeeded subset;
- failed subset;
- materiality.

---

## 54. No-Op

Debe poder indicar:

- no change applied;
- objective already satisfied;
- duplicate request suppressed.

---

## 55. Boundary Explanation

Debe poder explicar si se bloqueó por:

- policy;
- authority;
- scope;
- capability;
- readiness;
- resource conflict.

---

## 56. Operational Meaning

Debe poder traducir technical failure into business consequence.

---

## 57. Channel

Debe poder seleccionar conceptualmente:

- dashboard;
- in-app;
- email;
- chat;
- incident channel;
- other authorized channel.

---

## 58. Channel Priority

Debe poder adaptar delivery a urgency.

---

## 59. Delivery Result

Debe poder representar:

- attempted;
- delivered;
- failed;
- fallback used.

---

## 60. Acknowledgement Requirement

Debe poder indicar qué mensajes requieren confirmación.

---

## 61. Communication Freshness

Debe poder incluir when state/message was last updated.

---

## 62. Correction

Debe poder añadirse una corrección sin borrar el mensaje histórico.

---

## 63. Version / Supersession

Debe poder relacionarse una actualización con información previa.

---

## 64. Privacy Classification

Debe poder aplicar least necessary disclosure.

---

## 65. Tenant Scope

Debe poder garantizar qué tenant/organization puede ver el mensaje.

---

## 66. Security Redaction

Debe poder evitar exposición de:

- tokens;
- secrets;
- credentials;
- sensitive technical detail.

---

## 67. Communication Audit Trace

Debe poder reconstruirse:

**event → classification → audience → message → delivery → acknowledgement/decision → applied state**

---

## 68. Reporting Quality Signal

Debe poder capturarse:

- useful;
- unclear;
- too noisy;
- missing context;
- wrong recipient;
- late.

---

## 69. Learning Handoff

Debe poder transferirse a Fase 9 información sobre:

- alert quality;
- response latency;
- false positives;
- false negatives;
- human action;
- communication effectiveness.
