# 6.07 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para retención, CRM y LTV

## Alcance

La fuente define qué información necesita una evaluación madura de retención y valor de cliente.

No define JSON Schema, tablas, APIs, tipos técnicos ni nombres definitivos de campos.

---

## 1. Identidad y definición de cliente

El contrato debería poder representar:

- customer identifier;
- customer matching quality;
- first-time vs returning;
- active vs inactive;
- subscriber vs non-subscriber;
- VIP;
- lifecycle state.

La calidad de identity matching condiciona retention y LTV.

---

## 2. Natural repurchase cycle

Debería poder expresarse:

- category;
- product;
- expected repurchase window;
- seasonal cycle;
- replenishment timing.

---

## 3. Cohortes

Debería poder representar cohortes por:

- first purchase date;
- acquisition channel;
- first product;
- discount;
- geo;
- customer segment.

---

## 4. Cohort maturity

Debe poder expresarse:

- cohort age;
- maturity;
- right-censoring risk;
- eligible observation window.

---

## 5. Repeat metrics

Cuando existan:

- repeat purchase rate;
- second purchase rate;
- time to second order;
- repeat 30d;
- repeat 90d;
- repeat 180d;
- repeat 365d;
- purchase frequency;
- order frequency distribution.

---

## 6. Customer value

El contrato debería distinguir:

- revenue LTV;
- gross profit LTV;
- contribution LTV;
- observed LTV;
- predicted LTV;
- LTV window;
- cumulative contribution;
- median customer value;
- value distribution.

---

## 7. Acquisition linkage

Puede necesitar:

- CAC;
- acquisition source;
- first product;
- LTV:CAC;
- payback;
- CAC recovery curve.

---

## 8. CRM lifecycle

Debería poder expresar:

- lifecycle stage;
- next best action;
- trigger;
- timing;
- channel;
- flow type;
- campaign type.

---

## 9. CRM channel metrics

Cuando existan:

- delivered;
- clicks;
- click-to-conversion;
- revenue per recipient;
- contribution per recipient;
- unsubscribe;
- spam complaints;
- deliverability indicators;
- send frequency;
- contact pressure.

---

## 10. CRM attribution

Debe poder distinguir:

- platform attributed revenue;
- returning revenue;
- incremental revenue;
- holdout evidence;
- cannibalization risk.

---

## 11. Discount dependency

Puede requerir:

- discount rate;
- full-price repeat;
- promo repeat;
- cohort discount dependency;
- discount holdout outcome.

---

## 12. Reactivation

Debería poder representar:

- inactive eligibility;
- reactivation rate;
- winback trigger;
- organic reactivation;
- incremental reactivation estimate.

---

## 13. Subscription

Cuando aplique:

- active subscribers;
- churn;
- revenue churn;
- voluntary churn;
- involuntary churn;
- failed payments;
- pauses;
- skips;
- tenure.

---

## 14. Customer profitability

Puede incluir:

- revenue;
- COGS;
- fulfillment;
- returns;
- support;
- discounts;
- contribution.

---

## 15. Loyalty y referral

Cuando aplique:

- loyalty participation;
- points/reward cost;
- incremental repeat;
- referral cost;
- referred customer LTV;
- referred customer retention.

---

## 16. Guardrails

El resultado debería poder expresar:

- natural frequency;
- cohort maturity;
- contribution;
- payback;
- cash;
- discount;
- customer trust;
- deliverability;
- attribution;
- customer quality;
- identity quality;
- observed vs predicted;
- category fit;
- ethics.

---

## 17. Estado conceptual

Puede necesitar representar:

- retención saludable;
- mejorable;
- débil;
- subvencionada;
- deteriorándose;
- CRM infrautilizado;
- CRM saturado;
- LTV saludable;
- LTV sobreestimado;
- indeterminado.

No se define un enum técnico.

---

## 18. Evidence gaps

Debe poder expresarse qué impide concluir con confianza, por ejemplo:

- identidad incompleta;
- cohortes inmaduras;
- LTV solo predicted;
- lack of margin;
- attribution ambiguity.

---

## 19. Elementos no definidos

No se definen:

- JSON schemas;
- IDs;
- tablas;
- APIs;
- scoring exacto;
- churn model;
- LTV model técnico;
- next-best-action engine;
- CRM orchestration engine;
- persistence;
- permissions;
- threshold universales.

No deben inventarse.
