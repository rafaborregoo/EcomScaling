# 6.10 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para inventario y salud comercial

## Alcance

Este documento extrae requisitos conceptuales de intercambio.

La fuente no define JSON Schema, APIs, tablas ni tipos técnicos.

---

## 1. Stock state

El contrato debería poder representar:

- stock on hand;
- reserved stock;
- damaged stock;
- inbound stock;
- available to sell;
- sellable stock.

---

## 2. Inventory accuracy

Debería poder expresar:

- system quantity;
- physical quantity;
- accuracy;
- phantom stock;
- hidden stock;
- stock discrepancies.

---

## 3. Demand velocity

Puede necesitar:

- sales velocity;
- demand events;
- traffic;
- PDP views;
- waitlist;
- back-in-stock registrations;
- attempted purchases.

---

## 4. Stockout

Debería poder representar:

- OOS status;
- stockout days;
- weighted stockout importance;
- estimated lost sales;
- substitution;
- hero SKU status.

---

## 5. Coverage

Puede incluir:

- weeks/months of cover;
- expected demand;
- lead time;
- demand variability;
- safety stock;
- reorder point.

---

## 6. Supplier context

Debería poder expresar:

- supplier;
- lead time;
- lead time variability;
- reliability;
- MOQ;
- supplier concentration;
- alternative supplier;
- terms.

---

## 7. Forecast

Puede representar:

- forecast demand;
- actual demand;
- forecast error;
- bias;
- confidence;
- seasonality;
- trend;
- promotion uplift;
- pull-forward.

---

## 8. Aging

Debería poder representar:

- inventory age;
- age buckets;
- slow-moving;
- dead stock;
- aged inventory value;
- markdown risk.

---

## 9. Productivity

Puede incluir:

- sell-through;
- inventory turns;
- DIOH;
- GMROI;
- contribution return on inventory.

---

## 10. Assortment

Puede requerir:

- SKU role;
- ABC class;
- XYZ class;
- lifecycle;
- breadth;
- depth;
- long-tail status;
- strategic role.

---

## 11. Variant availability

Cuando aplique:

- size;
- color;
- variant availability;
- broken size run;
- effective availability.

---

## 12. Concentration

Puede representar:

- SKU concentration;
- category concentration;
- supplier concentration;
- contribution concentration;
- revenue concentration.

---

## 13. Multi-location / Multi-channel

Puede necesitar:

- warehouse/store/country stock;
- channel allocation;
- reservation logic;
- sync status;
- rebalancing need.

---

## 14. Working capital

Debería poder representar:

- inventory value;
- cash tied;
- inventory growth;
- supplier terms;
- prepayment;
- open-to-buy;
- financing need.

---

## 15. Clearance / liquidation

Puede incluir:

- markdown;
- expected hold value;
- liquidation value;
- cash released;
- contribution impact.

---

## 16. Product portfolio

Puede necesitar:

- product role;
- basket role;
- retention role;
- traffic role;
- strategic importance;
- incremental vs cannibalized demand.

---

## 17. State

Puede representar conceptualmente:

- healthy;
- tight;
- oversized;
- unproductive;
- imbalanced;
- supply risk;
- concentration risk;
- forecast problem;
- assortment inefficient;
- indeterminate.

No se define un enum técnico.

---

## 18. Evidence gaps

Debería poder expresar:

- unreliable stock;
- missing demand history;
- unknown lead time;
- missing supplier data;
- weak forecast;
- missing variant data;
- incomplete landed cost.

---

## 19. Elementos no definidos

No se definen:

- JSON Schema;
- stock reservation implementation;
- WMS tables;
- supplier APIs;
- reorder engine;
- forecasting model;
- automatic safety-stock algorithm;
- sync architecture;
- persistence;
- permissions.

No deben inventarse.
