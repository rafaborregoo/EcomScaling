# 6.11 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para escalabilidad y límites

## Alcance

Este documento describe información que un sistema de IA necesita intercambiar para evaluar scalability.

La fuente no define JSON Schema, APIs, tablas ni tipos técnicos.

---

## 1. Growth Objective

El contrato debería poder representar:

- qué se quiere escalar;
- target growth;
- time horizon;
- scale vector;
- strategic objective.

---

## 2. Market Capacity

Puede necesitar:

- TAM;
- SAM;
- SOM;
- market penetration;
- demand growth;
- saturation signals;
- category/geo opportunity.

---

## 3. Acquisition Capacity

Debería poder expresar:

- spend;
- average CAC;
- marginal CAC;
- maximum sustainable CAC;
- channel capacity;
- channel saturation;
- creative capacity;
- new-customer volume.

---

## 4. Conversion Capacity

Puede incluir:

- CVR;
- segment CVR;
- marginal traffic quality;
- cold/broad/international traffic mix;
- conversion deterioration;
- site capacity.

---

## 5. Customer Quality

Puede necesitar:

- retention;
- LTV;
- contribution/customer;
- returns;
- quality by marginal cohort.

---

## 6. Inventory Capacity

Debería poder representar:

- coverage;
- replenishment;
- lead time;
- supplier capacity;
- supplier concentration;
- inventory financing requirement.

---

## 7. Operational Capacity

Puede incluir:

- throughput;
- utilization;
- peak capacity;
- backlog;
- headroom;
- support load;
- carrier capacity;
- 3PL capacity.

---

## 8. Financial Capacity

Debería poder representar:

- cash;
- cash runway;
- liquidity buffer;
- payback;
- debt;
- supplier credit;
- working capital;
- cash requirement.

---

## 9. Technology Capacity

Puede necesitar:

- concurrent-user capacity;
- checkout capacity;
- API/integration capacity;
- tested peak;
- failure modes.

---

## 10. Organizational Capacity

Puede incluir:

- founder dependency;
- key-person dependency;
- management bandwidth;
- manual processes;
- approvals;
- staffing;
- process standardization.

---

## 11. Complexity

Debería poder expresar:

- number of channels;
- markets;
- SKUs;
- teams;
- integrations;
- coordination burden;
- reporting fragmentation.

---

## 12. Marginal Economics

Puede necesitar:

- marginal revenue;
- marginal cost;
- marginal contribution;
- marginal profit;
- incremental risk.

---

## 13. Headroom

Debería poder representar headroom por subsistema:

- acquisition;
- inventory;
- operations;
- cash;
- support;
- technology;
- organization.

---

## 14. Bottleneck

La salida debería poder representar:

- current dominant constraint;
- next likely constraint;
- evidence;
- severity;
- dependency.

---

## 15. Margin of Safety

Puede necesitar expresar conceptualmente:

- current state;
- critical threshold;
- remaining buffer;
- uncertainty.

La fuente no define fórmula universal.

---

## 16. Stress Tests

Puede representar escenarios:

- base;
- upside;
- downside;
- stress.

Con variables como:

- CAC;
- CVR;
- supplier delay;
- demand;
- returns;
- cash need.

---

## 17. Reversibility

Debe poder distinguir:

- reversible scale decisions;
- partially reversible;
- irreversible/fixed commitments.

Ejemplos:

- paid spend;
- hires;
- warehouse;
- inventory PO;
- market expansion.

---

## 18. Scale State

Puede expresar:

- highly scalable;
- scalable;
- scalable with constraints;
- near limit;
- saturated;
- financially limited;
- operationally limited;
- supply limited;
- acquisition limited;
- fragile;
- not scalable currently.

No se define enum técnico.

---

## 19. Evidence / Confidence

La salida debería separar:

- observed;
- forecast;
- assumptions;
- stress scenarios;
- evidence gaps;
- confidence.

---

## 20. Elementos no definidos

No se definen:

- JSON Schema;
- scoring formula;
- scale-capacity engine;
- simulation engine;
- auto-budgeting;
- organization model;
- alert thresholds;
- persistence;
- permissions.

No deben inventarse.
