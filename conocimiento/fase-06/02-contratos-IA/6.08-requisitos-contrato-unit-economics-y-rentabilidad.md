# 6.08 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para Unit Economics y rentabilidad

## Alcance

Este bloque define qué información necesita una evaluación económica madura.

No define JSON Schema, tablas, APIs, tipos técnicos ni nombres definitivos de campos.

---

## 1. Revenue

El contrato debería poder distinguir:

- gross sales;
- net revenue;
- discounts;
- returns;
- refunds;
- taxes;
- shipping revenue;
- currency;
- period.

---

## 2. COGS

Debería poder representar:

- product cost;
- manufacturing/purchase cost;
- inbound freight cuando aplique;
- policy/definition used.

---

## 3. Variable costs

Puede incluir:

- fulfillment;
- shipping subsidy;
- payment processing;
- packaging;
- returns;
- marketplace fees;
- affiliate commissions;
- variable software fees;
- acquisition.

---

## 4. Contribution layers

La evaluación debería poder distinguir cuando estén disponibles:

- gross profit;
- gross margin;
- contribution before acquisition;
- contribution after acquisition;
- contribution/order;
- contribution/customer;
- contribution/session;
- contribution/visitor.

La nomenclatura concreta puede variar, pero debe ser consistente.

---

## 5. Fixed y Semi-variable costs

Debería poder representar:

- fixed costs;
- overhead;
- salaries;
- rent;
- software;
- stepped/semi-variable capacity costs.

---

## 6. CAC

Puede necesitar:

- blended CAC;
- channel CAC;
- historical CAC;
- marginal CAC;
- target CAC;
- break-even CAC.

---

## 7. First-order economics

Debería poder expresar:

- first-order contribution before CAC;
- CAC;
- first-order contribution after CAC;
- first-order status.

---

## 8. Lifetime economics

Cuando existan:

- revenue LTV;
- gross profit LTV;
- contribution LTV;
- observed/predicted;
- cohort window;
- cumulative contribution.

---

## 9. Payback

Debe poder representar:

- CAC recovery curve;
- payback point;
- payback period;
- cash implication.

---

## 10. Segmentation

La evaluación puede requerir economics por:

- product;
- channel;
- customer;
- cohort;
- geo;
- order.

---

## 11. Product economics

Puede incluir:

- SKU revenue;
- SKU margin;
- SKU contribution;
- return rate;
- fulfillment cost;
- first-product future value.

---

## 12. Customer profitability

Puede incluir:

- revenue;
- contribution;
- returns;
- support;
- discounts;
- cost to serve;
- net customer value.

---

## 13. Order profitability

Debería poder representar:

- order revenue;
- variable costs;
- order contribution;
- negative contribution order flag/state conceptually;
- order size.

---

## 14. Working capital

Cuando esté disponible:

- inventory days;
- receivable days;
- payable days;
- inventory investment;
- supplier terms;
- cash requirement.

---

## 15. Business profitability

Puede requerir:

- total contribution;
- fixed costs;
- operating profit;
- profit target;
- break-even status.

---

## 16. Marginal economics

Debería poder distinguir:

- average vs marginal CAC;
- average vs marginal contribution;
- incremental costs;
- incremental contribution.

---

## 17. Forecast and scenarios

Puede ser necesario representar:

- base case;
- upside;
- downside;
- sensitivity to CAC;
- COGS;
- returns;
- shipping;
- repeat.

---

## 18. Profitability leaks

La salida debería poder señalar conceptualmente si el leak principal está en:

- acquisition;
- product cost;
- discounts;
- returns;
- fulfillment;
- overhead;
- cash/working capital.

---

## 19. States

Puede necesitar representar:

- healthy unit economics;
- narrow economics;
- sustainable first-order loss;
- dangerous first-order loss;
- profitable growth;
- low-quality growth;
- destructive growth;
- cash constrained;
- structurally unprofitable;
- indeterminate.

No se define un enum técnico.

---

## 20. Evidence and confidence

La salida debería poder separar:

- observed data;
- modeled data;
- forecast;
- assumptions;
- confidence;
- missing costs;
- missing definitions.

---

## 21. Elementos no definidos

La fuente no define:

- JSON Schema;
- APIs;
- tables;
- calculation engine;
- accounting policy engine;
- cash forecast engine;
- unit-cost allocation engine;
- thresholds universales;
- alerting;
- persistence;
- permissions.

No deben inventarse.
