# 6.13 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para confianza, incertidumbre y comunicación

## Alcance

La fuente define qué información debería poder representar un sistema para comunicar juicios calibrados.

No define JSON Schema, APIs, tablas, enums obligatorios ni scoring técnico.

---

## 1. Claim

Debería poder representarse:

- exact claim;
- scope;
- metric/area;
- period;
- segment.

---

## 2. Claim Type

Puede necesitar distinguir:

- fact;
- interpretation;
- hypothesis;
- recommendation.

---

## 3. Evidence

Debería poder representar:

- source;
- type;
- observation;
- comparability;
- maturity;
- recency;
- reliability;
- independence.

---

## 4. Confidence Dimensions

Puede necesitar separar:

- data confidence;
- comparison confidence;
- pattern confidence;
- cause confidence;
- intervention/recommendation confidence.

---

## 5. Confidence State

Conceptualmente:

- high;
- medium;
- low;
- insufficient.

La fuente no define production enum.

---

## 6. Status

Puede representar:

- healthy;
- improvable;
- attention;
- risk;
- critical;
- indeterminate.

Status y confidence deben permanecer separados.

---

## 7. Severity / Impact

Puede incluir:

- severity;
- economic impact;
- customer impact;
- operational impact;
- systemic impact.

---

## 8. Uncertainty

Debería poder expresar:

- what remains uncertain;
- alternative explanations;
- unknown inputs;
- forecast uncertainty;
- comparator uncertainty.

---

## 9. Assumptions

Puede necesitar:

- assumption;
- importance;
- decision sensitivity;
- whether observed or modeled.

---

## 10. Evidence Gaps

Debería poder representar:

- missing evidence;
- criticality;
- value of information;
- whether missing evidence can change decision.

---

## 11. Comparator

Puede necesitar:

- internal history;
- cohort;
- peer;
- external benchmark;
- comparator quality;
- methodology;
- date;
- geography;
- sample;
- metric definition.

---

## 12. Source Conflict

Debe poder expresar:

- conflicting sources;
- source agreement;
- unresolved reconciliation.

---

## 13. Hypotheses

Puede necesitar:

- ranked hypotheses;
- supporting evidence;
- disconfirming evidence;
- prediction;
- falsifiability.

---

## 14. Recommendation

Debería poder representar:

- recommended action;
- recommendation confidence;
- reversibility;
- expected outcome;
- validation criteria.

---

## 15. Decision Context

Puede necesitar:

- cost of error;
- cost of waiting;
- urgency;
- reversibility;
- decision stakes.

---

## 16. Communication

Puede representar:

- audience;
- conclusion;
- impact;
- evidence;
- confidence;
- limitation;
- next step.

---

## 17. Forecast

Cuando aplique:

- base;
- upside;
- downside;
- range;
- horizon;
- observed vs predicted.

---

## 18. Traceability

El juicio debería poder reconstruirse conceptualmente:

`Data → Comparison → Pattern → Interpretation → Confidence → Conclusion`

---

## 19. Post-Intervention Validation

Puede necesitar:

- expected outcome;
- observed outcome;
- hypothesis update;
- recommendation update.

---

## 20. Elementos no definidos

No se definen:

- fixed confidence formula;
- weights;
- runtime enums;
- alert thresholds;
- confidence scoring engine;
- Bayesian implementation;
- database schema;
- persistence;
- permissions.

No deben inventarse.
