# 6.12 — Requisitos conceptuales de contrato para anomalías, red flags y patterns

## Alcance

La fuente define qué información debería poder intercambiar un sistema de IA para detectar y comunicar patrones de riesgo.

No define JSON Schema, tablas, APIs ni tipos técnicos.

---

## 1. Observation

Debería poder representarse:

- metric;
- value;
- period;
- baseline;
- segment;
- source;
- direction;
- deviation.

---

## 2. Expected Relationship

Puede necesitar:

- related metrics;
- expected direction;
- expected ratio;
- mathematical consistency;
- business consistency.

---

## 3. Anomaly

Debería poder expresar:

- deviation;
- baseline type;
- historical context;
- peer context;
- expected range;
- anomaly status.

---

## 4. Red Flag

Puede necesitar:

- signal;
- affected area;
- severity;
- scope;
- persistence;
- velocity;
- reversibility;
- confidence;
- urgency.

---

## 5. Pattern

Debería poder representar:

- participating metrics;
- directions;
- temporal relation;
- repeated occurrence;
- pattern/archetype;
- supporting evidence.

---

## 6. Evidence Diversity

Puede necesitar distinguir evidence sources:

- transactional;
- behavioral;
- operational;
- financial;
- attributed;
- modeled.

---

## 7. Data Validity

Debe poder expresar posibles explanations por:

- tracking change;
- timezone;
- backfill;
- methodology change;
- missing data;
- duplication;
- pipeline failure.

---

## 8. Temporal Context

Puede incluir:

- one-off;
- persistent;
- trend;
- acceleration;
- regime change;
- seasonality;
- event;
- expected variance.

---

## 9. Segment Scope

Debería poder representar si la señal afecta:

- SKU;
- channel;
- device;
- browser;
- geo;
- cohort;
- whole business.

---

## 10. Hypotheses

La salida debería poder expresar:

- plausible explanations;
- evidence for;
- evidence against;
- missing evidence.

No presentar hipótesis como hechos.

---

## 11. Impact

Puede necesitar:

- economic impact;
- customer impact;
- operational impact;
- downstream risk;
- systemic propagation.

---

## 12. Causal Chain

Puede representar conceptualmente:

- upstream signal;
- intermediate effect;
- downstream outcome.

Ejemplos:

`Stockout → CVR ↓ → CAC ↑`

o:

`Late Delivery → Support ↑ → Reviews ↓ → CVR ↓`

---

## 13. Pattern Library Reference

Puede identificar conceptualmente archetypes como:

- growth at any cost;
- harvesting;
- promo addiction;
- low-quality growth;
- operational overload;
- inventory trap;
- attribution illusion;
- LTV fantasy;
- false CRO win;
- profitable but insolvent;
- overcomplexity;
- data blindness.

---

## 14. Response Class

Puede expresar:

- monitor;
- investigate;
- act;
- escalate.

La fuente no define thresholds exactos.

---

## 15. Confidence

Debe representar:

- low;
- medium;
- high;

o equivalente conceptual.

No se define enum técnico.

---

## 16. Evidence Gaps

Debería poder expresar:

- insufficient sample;
- missing baseline;
- missing segment;
- conflicting sources;
- unknown event context;
- missing lag-adjusted data.

---

## 17. Elementos no definidos

No se definen:

- anomaly detection algorithm;
- z-score thresholds;
- alert rules;
- JSON Schema;
- database schema;
- pattern-matching engine;
- causal inference engine;
- notification engine;
- persistence;
- permissions.

No deben inventarse.
