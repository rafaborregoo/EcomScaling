# 6.10 — Aprendizaje de inventario, forecast y replenishment

## Alcance

El bloque aporta conocimiento útil para aprendizaje longitudinal sobre demand, supply y capital.

No define una memoria técnica.

---

## 1. Forecast learning

Cada ciclo puede comparar:

- forecast;
- actual;
- error;
- bias;
- seasonality;
- promotion impact.

---

## 2. Replenishment learning

Puede aprenderse qué decisiones de reorder producen:

- stockouts;
- healthy coverage;
- overstock;
- aging;
- capital drag.

---

## 3. Lead-time learning

Debe observarse:

- actual lead time;
- variability;
- supplier reliability.

Esto permite ajustar future safety stock.

---

## 4. Supplier learning

Comparar proveedores por:

- on-time;
- quantity accuracy;
- defects;
- flexibility;
- terms.

---

## 5. Stockout learning

Cada stockout puede generar evidencia sobre:

- lost sales;
- substitution;
- waitlist;
- back-in-stock conversion;
- customer impact.

---

## 6. Variant learning

La demanda por size/color/variant permite mejorar future allocation.

---

## 7. Launch learning

Initial buys pueden compararse con:

- sell-through;
- stockout;
- markdown;
- reorder speed.

Esto ayuda a calibrar launch inventory.

---

## 8. Promotion learning

Promotions deberían registrar:

- uplift;
- pull-forward;
- stock depletion;
- margin;
- post-promo demand.

---

## 9. Clearance learning

Clearance puede evaluarse por:

- aged stock reduction;
- cash released;
- contribution;
- markdown depth.

---

## 10. Assortment learning

SKU con low direct sales pueden demostrar valor por:

- basket role;
- traffic;
- retention;
- cross-sell.

No debe aprenderse únicamente desde revenue.

---

## 11. Portfolio learning

Comparar concentración y resilience a lo largo del tiempo ayuda a identificar dependence.

---

## 12. Allocation learning

Multi-location/multi-channel data puede revelar cuándo rebalancing evita new purchases.

---

## 13. Cash learning

Inventory growth vs sales growth permite detectar capital drag.

---

## 14. Demand-signal learning

Puede compararse poder predictivo de:

- sales;
- waitlist;
- search;
- PDP views;
- OOS attempts;
- back-in-stock signups.

---

## 15. ABC/XYZ learning

Clasificaciones pueden actualizarse cuando cambian:

- economic value;
- predictability;
- lifecycle.

---

## 16. Supplier contingency learning

Incidentes de supply pueden revelar el valor real de alternative supplier o substitute SKU.

---

## 17. What is not defined

La fuente no define:

- learning schema;
- forecasting engine;
- reinforcement logic;
- memory store;
- promotion/decay;
- auto-reorder model;
- cross-tenant generalization.

No deben inventarse.
