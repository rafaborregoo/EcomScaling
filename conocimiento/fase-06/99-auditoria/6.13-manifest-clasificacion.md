## Conceptos presentes en múltiples capas

### Confidence

- RAG: conceptual model.
- Contracts: dimensions/states.
- Runtime: local assignment.
- Policies: calibration and limits.
- Experience: post-hoc calibration.
- Tenant: source/maturity context.

### Status

- RAG: healthy/improvable/attention/risk/critical/indeterminate.
- Contracts: judgment state.
- Runtime: determine separately from confidence.
- Policies: never conflate status and confidence.

### Evidence Gap

- RAG: unknown propagation and VOI.
- Contracts: missing evidence.
- Runtime: data collection decision.
- Policies: do not impute unknown.
- Tenant: actual missing data.

### Recommendation Confidence

- RAG: distinct from diagnosis confidence.
- Contracts: field requirement conceptually.
- Runtime: action/experiment/wait.
- Policies: reversible vs irreversible.
- Experience: compare recommendation outcome.

### Traceability

- RAG: Data → Comparison → Pattern → Interpretation → Confidence → Conclusion.
- Contracts: provenance requirements.
- Runtime: communication output.
- Policies: material judgment must be explainable.
