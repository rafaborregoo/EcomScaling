# 6.05 — Contexto tenant para CRO

## Propósito

La evaluación de conversión depende de contexto específico de cada ecommerce.

No se incluyen valores concretos de ningún tenant.

---

## 1. Definiciones de conversión

- conversion event;
- session-based vs user-based;
- eligible traffic;
- funnel definitions;
- tracking source.

---

## 2. Mix del negocio

- device mix;
- browser mix;
- channel mix;
- source mix;
- new/returning;
- geo;
- landing mix;
- product/category mix;
- promotion mix.

---

## 3. Funnel

Disponibilidad de:

- product views;
- add to carts;
- checkout starts;
- payment attempts;
- purchases;
- successful orders;
- cancellations;
- refunds;
- returns.

---

## 4. Oferta

- pricing;
- promotions;
- discounting;
- bundles;
- free shipping;
- shipping thresholds;
- return policy;
- account requirements;
- payment options.

---

## 5. Producto e inventario

- product mix;
- stock availability;
- size availability;
- hero products;
- out-of-stock periods.

---

## 6. Operación

- shipping costs;
- delivery promise;
- fulfillment constraints;
- support capacity;
- payment gateway;
- payment methods;
- fraud controls.

---

## 7. Brand y trust

- reviews;
- guarantees;
- brand maturity;
- shipping transparency;
- payment trust;
- customer familiarity.

---

## 8. Economics

- AOV;
- margin;
- contribution;
- revenue/session;
- contribution/session;
- returns cost;
- support cost.

---

## 9. Histórico

- historical CVR;
- historical funnel;
- best sustainable performance;
- device benchmarks;
- browser benchmarks;
- channel benchmarks;
- product benchmarks.

---

## 10. Experimentation maturity

- traffic volume;
- order volume;
- testing capability;
- experimentation tooling;
- ability to randomize;
- sample size constraints.

---

## 11. Known technical context

- deploys;
- checkout migrations;
- payment incidents;
- browser issues;
- performance issues;
- error logs.

---

## 12. Business maturity

Negocios tempranos pueden requerir priorizar:

- bugs;
- offer;
- trust;
- basics.

Negocios maduros pueden soportar:

- robust experimentation;
- personalization;
- advanced segmentation.

---

## 13. Restricciones

- stock;
- cash;
- operations;
- support;
- legal/ethical limitations.

---

## 14. Ausencia de contexto

Si faltan definiciones, economics o funnel fiable, la conclusión debe limitarse y reducir confidence.
