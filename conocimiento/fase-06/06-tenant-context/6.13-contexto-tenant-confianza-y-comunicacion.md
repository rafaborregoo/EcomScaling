# 6.13 — Contexto tenant para confianza y comunicación

## Propósito

Confidence depende de la calidad de data, comparabilidad, maturity, business context y decision stakes del ecommerce concreto.

No se incluyen valores concretos de ningún tenant.

---

## 1. Data Sources

- ecommerce platform;
- analytics;
- ad platforms;
- payment processor;
- CRM;
- finance;
- warehouse;
- support.

---

## 2. Source Reliability

- known tracking quality;
- reconciliation status;
- data ownership;
- methodology changes;
- historical issues.

---

## 3. Metric Definitions

- revenue;
- CAC;
- CVR;
- AOV;
- LTV;
- contribution;
- margin;
- returns;
- inventory.

---

## 4. Comparable Cohorts

- historical;
- channel;
- product;
- customer type;
- geo;
- season;
- campaign.

---

## 5. Benchmark Sources

- internal;
- peers;
- category;
- external industry;
- vendor benchmark.

---

## 6. Business Regime

- pricing changes;
- market expansion;
- new products;
- channel shifts;
- seasonality;
- structural changes.

---

## 7. Maturity

- cohort age;
- return lag;
- LTV maturity;
- campaign maturity;
- experiment duration.

---

## 8. Decision Stakes

- reversible test;
- large inventory PO;
- price change;
- market entry;
- hiring;
- major spend increase.

---

## 9. Risk Tolerance

- cash buffer;
- downside tolerance;
- acceptable uncertainty;
- required evidence for strategic decisions.

---

## 10. Audience

- executive;
- analyst;
- channel specialist;
- operator.

Audience changes depth, not truth.

---

## 11. Communication Preference

- concise;
- analytical;
- confidence labels;
- range-based forecasts;
- scenario detail.

---

## 12. Known Assumptions

- margin stability;
- retention stability;
- demand persistence;
- supply continuity.

---

## 13. Evidence Gaps

- missing margin;
- unknown customer identity;
- incomplete return data;
- weak benchmark comparability;
- missing cohort maturity.

---

## 14. Critical Unknowns

No todas las missing data tienen igual importancia.

Debe identificarse qué missing evidence puede cambiar decision.

---

## 15. Human Escalation Context

- legal/ethical boundaries;
- financial authority;
- irreversible commitments;
- governance expectations.

---

## 16. Missing Context

Si falta reliable source context, comparator quality, maturity o decision stakes, confidence communication debe ser más cauta.
