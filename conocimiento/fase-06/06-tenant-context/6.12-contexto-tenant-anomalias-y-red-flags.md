# 6.12 — Contexto tenant para anomalías y red flags

## Propósito

La detección de anomaly depende de baselines, seasonality, definitions, risk tolerance y operating model propios de cada ecommerce.

No se incluyen valores concretos de ningún tenant.

---

## 1. Baselines

- historical periods;
- comparable cohorts;
- seasonality;
- promotion periods;
- launches;
- events.

---

## 2. Metric Definitions

- revenue;
- orders;
- CAC;
- ROAS;
- MER;
- CVR;
- AOV;
- LTV;
- contribution;
- inventory;
- returns.

---

## 3. Data Sources

- ecommerce platform;
- ad platforms;
- analytics;
- payment processor;
- CRM;
- warehouse;
- finance.

---

## 4. Tracking Context

- migrations;
- methodology changes;
- backfills;
- timezone;
- attribution changes;
- identity changes.

---

## 5. Business Segments

- channel;
- device;
- browser;
- geo;
- SKU;
- category;
- cohort;
- customer type.

---

## 6. Risk Tolerance

- acceptable CAC deterioration;
- cash buffer;
- inventory risk;
- support tolerance;
- margin compression;
- critical CX limits.

---

## 7. Operating Events

- promotions;
- product launches;
- site releases;
- carrier incidents;
- stockouts;
- supplier delays;
- pricing changes.

---

## 8. Customer Lifecycle

- new vs returning;
- cohort age;
- retention windows;
- LTV windows.

---

## 9. Paid Mix

- prospecting;
- retargeting;
- branded;
- channel mix.

---

## 10. Promotion Context

- discount depth;
- promo frequency;
- full-price demand;
- campaign timing.

---

## 11. Inventory Context

- weeks cover;
- hero SKUs;
- supplier concentration;
- aging;
- replenishment.

---

## 12. Operational Context

- capacity;
- backlog;
- SLA;
- returns;
- support;
- delivery.

---

## 13. Financial Context

- contribution;
- cash;
- payback;
- inventory financing;
- working capital.

---

## 14. Expected Relationships

Cada tenant puede tener relaciones operativas propias que ayuden a detectar inconsistencies.

---

## 15. Known Exceptions

Eventos conocidos deberían registrarse para evitar false positives.

---

## 16. Missing Context

Si faltan baseline, event context, segment context o metric definitions, la detección debe reducir confidence.
