# 6.09 — Contexto tenant para operaciones y experiencia

## Propósito

La evaluación operacional depende de la promesa, modelo logístico, geografía, producto y capacidad propios de cada ecommerce.

No se incluyen valores concretos de ningún tenant.

---

## 1. Customer promise

- standard delivery;
- express delivery;
- premium shipping;
- backorder rules;
- promised dates;
- SLA.

---

## 2. Fulfillment model

- in-house;
- 3PL;
- hybrid;
- warehouse locations;
- automation level.

---

## 3. Processing

- processing time;
- picking;
- packing;
- handoff;
- throughput;
- labor model.

---

## 4. Capacity

- current capacity;
- peak capacity;
- utilization;
- headroom;
- known bottlenecks;
- seasonal peaks.

---

## 5. Carriers

- carrier mix;
- regions;
- service levels;
- cost;
- dependency;
- redundancy.

---

## 6. International operations

- customs;
- duties;
- transit expectations;
- returns complexity;
- geo-specific SLAs.

---

## 7. Support

- channels;
- staffing;
- hours;
- FRT;
- resolution time;
- FCR;
- ticket taxonomy;
- self-service;
- automation.

---

## 8. Returns

- policy;
- free/paid returns;
- return windows;
- exchange rules;
- refund timing;
- restock process;
- recovery value.

---

## 9. Cancellation

- buyer cancellation rules;
- merchant cancellation rules;
- stock cancellation;
- backorder behavior.

---

## 10. Product characteristics

- fragility;
- size complexity;
- sizing;
- high-return categories;
- packaging needs.

---

## 11. Economics

- fulfillment cost;
- shipping cost;
- shipping revenue;
- shipping subsidy;
- support cost;
- return cost;
- contribution constraints.

---

## 12. Customer experience

- CSAT;
- NPS;
- reviews;
- complaints;
- critical complaint categories;
- customer effort.

---

## 13. Operational data

- timestamps;
- carrier scans;
- return reasons;
- cancellation reasons;
- warehouse events.

---

## 14. Incident history

- recurring incidents;
- severity;
- MTTR;
- root causes;
- provider failures.

---

## 15. Growth plans

- expected order growth;
- campaign peaks;
- launches;
- geographic expansion.

---

## 16. Constraints

- staffing;
- warehouse space;
- carrier limits;
- support limits;
- working capital;
- inventory availability.

---

## 17. Missing context

Si faltan promise, timestamps, capacity, carrier or return reason data, la evaluación debe reducir confidence y limitar conclusiones.
