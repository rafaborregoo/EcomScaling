# 6.03 — Contexto tenant para adquisición y demanda

## Propósito

La evaluación de adquisición depende fuertemente de economics, estrategia, mix y restricciones propias de cada ecommerce.

No se incluyen datos concretos de ningún tenant.

---

## 1. Objetivo estratégico

- crecimiento;
- rentabilidad;
- expansión;
- market entry;
- otras prioridades.

---

## 2. Definición de adquisición

- definición de new customer;
- definición de CAC;
- costes incluidos;
- paid vs blended;
- attribution model;
- attribution window.

---

## 3. Economics

- sustainable CAC;
- target CAC;
- contribution;
- first-order contribution;
- lifetime contribution;
- LTV;
- payback;
- AOV;
- margin;
- cash constraints.

---

## 4. Customer quality

- repeat;
- refunds;
- returns;
- churn;
- support cost;
- discount dependency;
- cohort quality.

---

## 5. Channel mix

- paid social;
- paid search;
- organic;
- direct;
- email;
- referral;
- affiliates;
- creators;
- marketplaces.

---

## 6. Roles de canal

Cada canal puede configurarse o interpretarse como:

- demand generation;
- demand capture;
- retargeting;
- retention;
- brand.

---

## 7. Dependencias

- paid dependency;
- platform concentration;
- creator concentration;
- campaign concentration;
- creative concentration;
- product concentration;
- market concentration.

---

## 8. Demanda propia

- branded search;
- direct;
- organic;
- referrals;
- social organic;
- repeat visitors;
- creator mentions;
- category demand.

---

## 9. Escala y capacidad

- current spend;
- spend history;
- new customer volume;
- marginal CAC;
- reach;
- frequency;
- market size;
- penetration;
- acquisition capacity.

---

## 10. Restricciones

- working capital;
- cash reserves;
- supplier/payment terms;
- inventory coverage;
- warehouse capacity;
- fulfillment;
- support;
- logistics;
- return capacity.

---

## 11. Promociones y oferta

- discount intensity;
- promotion dependency;
- offer subsidy;
- full-price acquisition performance.

---

## 12. Maduración y cohortes

- cohort age;
- repeat windows;
- LTV maturity;
- refund/return maturity;
- historical cohorts.

---

## 13. Incrementalidad

Cuando el tenant disponga de evidencia:

- holdouts;
- geo tests;
- lift tests;
- controlled experiments.

Si no existe, no debe inventarse.

---

## 14. Ausencia de contexto

Cuando falten economics, definición de cliente nuevo, atribución o madurez de cohortes, la confianza debe reducirse y la conclusión limitarse.
