# 6.10 — Flujo operacional de evaluación de inventario y salud comercial

## Propósito

Define la secuencia para determinar si el ecommerce mantiene suficiente availability sin comprometer capital y resiliencia.

---

## 1. Paso 1 — Validar stock

Comprobar si physical y system stock coinciden.

Revisar:

- phantom stock;
- hidden stock;
- damaged stock;
- reserved stock;
- inbound stock.

Si accuracy es baja, detener automatizaciones fuertes.

---

## 2. Paso 2 — Identificar demand velocity

Medir por SKU/variant:

- sales;
- traffic;
- waitlist;
- search;
- OOS interest.

---

## 3. Paso 3 — Calcular coverage

Conceptualmente:

`Coverage = Current Sellable Stock / Expected Demand`

Siempre contextualizar con:

- lead time;
- variability;
- seasonality.

---

## 4. Paso 4 — Incorporar lead time

Revisar:

- average lead time;
- variability;
- supplier reliability;
- MOQ;
- supplier terms.

---

## 5. Paso 5 — Evaluar stockout risk

Priorizar:

- hero SKU;
- high-contribution SKU;
- strategic SKU;
- long lead-time items.

---

## 6. Paso 6 — Evaluar aging

Analizar:

- age buckets;
- slow-moving;
- dead stock;
- aged inventory value;
- markdown risk.

---

## 7. Paso 7 — Analizar sell-through

Revisar por:

- SKU;
- category;
- lifecycle;
- season.

---

## 8. Paso 8 — Analizar inventory turns

Comparar:

- historical;
- product lifecycle;
- category.

Evitar interpretar high turnover sin revisar stockouts.

---

## 9. Paso 9 — Analizar GMROI / Contribution Return

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué valor produce el capital invertido en este SKU/categoría?

---

## 10. Paso 10 — Analizar forecast

Comprobar:

- error;
- bias;
- volatility;
- seasonality;
- promotion effects;
- new-product uncertainty.

---

## 11. Paso 11 — Analizar concentration

Revisar:

- SKU;
- category;
- supplier;
- contribution;
- revenue.

---

## 12. Paso 12 — Analizar working capital

Evaluar:

- inventory value;
- cash tied;
- supplier terms;
- purchase timing;
- cash available;
- financing.

---

## 13. Paso 13 — Analizar assortment role

Para cada SKU relevante:

- traffic;
- profit;
- cross-sell;
- premium;
- retention;
- strategic role.

No eliminar únicamente por low sales.

---

## 14. Paso 14 — Analizar variants

Cuando aplique:

- size curve;
- color;
- effective availability;
- broken runs.

---

## 15. Paso 15 — Analizar multi-location

Comprobar si stock total está mal distribuido.

Evaluar rebalancing antes de nuevas compras.

---

## 16. Paso 16 — Analizar channel allocation

Revisar:

- web;
- marketplace;
- stores;
- margin;
- service promise;
- reserved stock.

---

## 17. Paso 17 — Analizar replenishment

Antes de reorder:

- demand;
- contribution;
- age;
- lead time;
- safety stock;
- cash.

---

## 18. Paso 18 — Analizar liquidation

Cuando existe slow/dead stock:

- hold expected value;
- liquidation now value;
- markdown;
- cash released;
- opportunity cost.

---

## 19. Paso 19 — Evaluar growth impact

Preguntar si:

- paid puede escalar;
- CRM puede promocionar;
- inventory puede soportar demand growth;
- cash puede financiar reorder.

---

## 20. Paso 20 — Aplicar guardrails

Revisar:

- accuracy;
- stockout;
- working capital;
- dead stock;
- lead time;
- supplier;
- margin;
- concentration;
- assortment complexity;
- forecast confidence;
- promotion alignment;
- multi-channel sync;
- variants;
- growth.

---

## 21. Paso 21 — Emitir estado

Clasificar conceptualmente:

- healthy;
- tight;
- oversized;
- unproductive;
- imbalanced;
- supply risk;
- concentration risk;
- forecast problem;
- assortment inefficient;
- indeterminate.

---

## 22. Árbol rápido de stockout risk

Si weeks of cover ↓:

1. demand velocity ↑?
2. lead time largo?
3. supplier unstable?
4. hero SKU?
5. replenishment ordered?
6. substitute available?
7. paid still driving demand?

---

## 23. Árbol rápido de overstock

Si inventory ↑:

1. sales ↑ proporcionalmente?
2. aging ↑?
3. forecast bias?
4. MOQ?
5. supplier bulk buy?
6. lifecycle decline?
7. assortment proliferation?

---

## 24. Árbol rápido de cash trap

Si inventory value ↑ y cash ↓:

1. sales growth?
2. turnover?
3. aged inventory?
4. supplier terms?
5. prepayment?
6. dead stock?
7. markdown/liquidation option?

---

## 25. Árbol rápido de assortment

Si muchos SKU tienen low sales:

1. traffic role?
2. basket role?
3. retention role?
4. margin?
5. inventory burden?
6. cannibalization?
7. strategic credibility?

---

## 26. Ciclo continuo

`Validate → Measure → Forecast → Allocate → Replenish → Monitor → Learn`

La política debe actualizarse cuando cambian demand, lead time o cash.
