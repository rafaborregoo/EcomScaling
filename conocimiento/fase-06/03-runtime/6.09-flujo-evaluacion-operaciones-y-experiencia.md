# 6.09 — Flujo operacional de evaluación de operaciones y experiencia

## Propósito

Define el orden lógico para evaluar si el ecommerce puede cumplir la promesa realizada al cliente con calidad y coste sostenibles.

---

## 1. Paso 1 — Definir promise

Identificar:

- delivery promise;
- service level;
- shipping method;
- backorder expectation;
- customer expectation.

Sin promise no puede juzgarse reliability correctamente.

---

## 2. Paso 2 — Validar operational data

Comprobar:

- order timestamps;
- shipped timestamp;
- delivered timestamp;
- cancellation reason;
- return reason;
- carrier assignment.

Si tracking es insuficiente, reducir confidence.

---

## 3. Paso 3 — Medir order processing

Analizar:

- processing time;
- backlog;
- backlog growth;
- orders aging;
- P90/P95.

---

## 4. Paso 4 — Medir fulfillment

Evaluar:

- fulfillment time;
- order accuracy;
- picking/packing errors;
- throughput;
- cost/order.

---

## 5. Paso 5 — Medir delivery

Revisar:

- OTD;
- late delivery;
- failed delivery;
- lost parcels;
- damage;
- first-attempt delivery;
- carrier/geo segmentation.

---

## 6. Paso 6 — Medir support

Revisar:

- tickets/100 orders;
- issue taxonomy;
- FRT;
- resolution time;
- FCR;
- reopen rate;
- WISMO;
- support cost.

---

## 7. Paso 7 — Medir returns

Analizar:

- return rate;
- reason mix;
- SKU;
- size;
- channel;
- geo;
- cost;
- refund time;
- recovery value.

---

## 8. Paso 8 — Medir cancellations

Determinar:

- pre-fulfillment;
- merchant;
- stock-related;
- buyer remorse;
- processing delays.

---

## 9. Paso 9 — Segmentar

Analizar por:

- carrier;
- warehouse;
- SKU;
- geo;
- service level;
- promotion;
- customer cohort.

---

## 10. Paso 10 — Evaluar customer impact

Observar:

- reviews;
- complaints;
- repeat;
- WISMO;
- support sentiment.

No asumir causalidad automática.

---

## 11. Paso 11 — Evaluar economics

Incorporar:

- fulfillment cost;
- shipping subsidy;
- support cost;
- return cost;
- failed delivery cost;
- replacement/refund cost.

---

## 12. Paso 12 — Evaluar capacity

Comprobar:

- current utilization;
- peak capacity;
- headroom;
- throughput;
- bottleneck.

---

## 13. Paso 13 — Growth stress-test

Preguntar qué ocurre con:

- +20 % orders;
- +50 %;
- +100 %.

Identificar el primer sistema que falla.

---

## 14. Paso 14 — Identificar bottleneck

Posibles restricciones:

- warehouse;
- packing;
- support;
- inventory;
- carrier;
- reverse logistics.

Priorizar la restricción dominante.

---

## 15. Paso 15 — Analizar resilience

Revisar:

- single provider dependency;
- incident history;
- recovery time;
- redundancy;
- peak preparedness.

---

## 16. Paso 16 — Aplicar guardrails

Revisar:

- promise;
- OTD;
- order accuracy;
- backlog;
- capacity;
- cost/order;
- returns;
- support;
- automation;
- carrier dependency;
- communication;
- trust;
- scale.

---

## 17. Paso 17 — Emitir estado

Clasificar conceptualmente como:

- healthy;
- efficient;
- under pressure;
- saturated;
- logistics issue;
- fulfillment issue;
- product issue;
- support issue;
- experience deterioration;
- economically inefficient;
- indeterminate.

---

## 18. Árbol rápido de delays

Si delivery empeora:

1. ¿processing time subió?
2. ¿backlog subió?
3. ¿fulfillment estable?
4. ¿carrier transit empeoró?
5. ¿se concentra por geo/carrier?
6. ¿cambió promise?

---

## 19. Árbol rápido de tickets

Si tickets/order ↑:

1. ¿WISMO?
2. ¿returns?
3. ¿sizing?
4. ¿product defects?
5. ¿payment?
6. ¿tracking communication?
7. ¿support capacity?

---

## 20. Árbol rápido de returns

Si return rate ↑:

1. reason mix;
2. SKU;
3. size;
4. channel;
5. geo;
6. promotion;
7. content mismatch;
8. damage;
9. late delivery.

---

## 21. Árbol rápido de scale

Si orders ↑:

1. OTD stable?
2. backlog stable?
3. errors stable?
4. tickets/order stable?
5. returns stable?
6. cost/order stable?
7. headroom suficiente?

---

## 22. Incident loop

`Incident → Root Cause → Corrective Action → Preventive Action → Monitor`

Recurring incidents deben tratarse como evidencia estructural.

---

## 23. Ciclo continuo

`Observe → Segment → Diagnose → Prioritize Bottleneck → Fix → Re-measure → Learn`

La operación debe evaluarse conjuntamente con economics y customer impact.
