# 6.13 — Flujo operacional de calibración de confianza y comunicación

## Propósito

Define cómo pasar de una conclusión analítica a un juicio comunicable, calibrado y útil.

---

## 1. Paso 1 — Define the Claim

Preguntar:

> ¿Qué exactamente estoy afirmando?

Evitar statements vagos.

---

## 2. Paso 2 — Classify Claim Type

Separar:

- fact;
- interpretation;
- hypothesis;
- recommendation.

---

## 3. Paso 3 — Identify Evidence

Registrar:

- source;
- period;
- segment;
- methodology;
- sample;
- maturity.

---

## 4. Paso 4 — Validate Data Confidence

Revisar:

- source reliability;
- definition;
- completeness;
- consistency;
- tracking changes.

---

## 5. Paso 5 — Validate Comparator

Comprobar:

- comparable cohort;
- historical relevance;
- external benchmark quality;
- regime changes.

---

## 6. Paso 6 — Evaluate Materiality

Preguntar:

> ¿La deviation importa económicamente o operacionalmente?

---

## 7. Paso 7 — Check Supporting Metrics

Revisar si:

- corroborate;
- contradict;
- are independent;
- are derived from same source.

---

## 8. Paso 8 — Evaluate Alternative Explanations

Listar solo hypotheses relevantes.

Priorizar:

1. most likely;
2. plausible;
3. less likely.

---

## 9. Paso 9 — Evaluate Causal Strength

Distinguir:

- observation;
- correlation;
- experiment;
- direct failure evidence.

---

## 10. Paso 10 — Identify Unknowns

Separar:

- unknown;
- uncertain.

No imputar unknown como neutral/zero.

---

## 11. Paso 11 — Propagate Uncertainty

Si input crítico tiene low confidence, reducir confidence de derived conclusions.

---

## 12. Paso 12 — Evaluate Decision Stakes

Considerar:

- severity;
- economic impact;
- reversibility;
- cost of error;
- cost of waiting;
- urgency.

---

## 13. Paso 13 — Determine Evidence Threshold

Mayor stakes e irreversibility → mayor evidence requirement.

Pero high cost of waiting puede justificar actuar antes.

---

## 14. Paso 14 — Assign Local Confidence

No una única confidence global.

Evaluar por claim:

- metric;
- interpretation;
- cause;
- financial impact;
- recommendation.

---

## 15. Paso 15 — Determine Status

Separadamente de confidence:

- healthy;
- improvable;
- attention;
- risk;
- critical;
- indeterminate.

---

## 16. Paso 16 — Select Language

### High

“Los datos muestran…”

### Medium

“La evidencia apunta a…”

### Low

“Una hipótesis plausible es…”

### Insufficient

“No existe evidencia suficiente para…”

---

## 17. Paso 17 — Select Response Mode

### Act

Cuando high impact y evidence sufficient.

### Investigate

Cuando severity alta y confidence baja/media.

### Experiment

Cuando uncertainty meaningful + reversible + enough volume.

### Wait

Cuando low severity + natural maturation required.

### Collect Data

Cuando missing evidence tiene high VOI.

### Monitor

Cuando signal está dentro de normal variance.

### Escalate to Human

Cuando strategic/legal/ethical/financial risk lo requiera.

---

## 18. Paso 18 — Communicate Conclusion

Orden recomendado:

1. conclusion;
2. impact;
3. evidence;
4. confidence;
5. limitation;
6. next step.

---

## 19. Paso 19 — Make Assumptions Explicit

Solo assumptions capaces de cambiar la decisión.

---

## 20. Paso 20 — Add Range / Scenario If Needed

No usar exact point estimate cuando uncertainty es material.

Preferir:

- range;
- base;
- upside;
- downside.

---

## 21. Paso 21 — Make Recommendation Testable

Expresar:

> If hypothesis is correct, expected outcome should be X.

---

## 22. Paso 22 — Post-Intervention Validation

Después:

- compare expected vs observed;
- update confidence;
- revise hypothesis if needed.

---

## 23. High Severity + Low Confidence

No ignorar.

Secuencia:

`Validate Urgently → Protect Downside → Refine Diagnosis`

---

## 24. Indeterminate

Si evidence crítica falta:

- limitar claim;
- indicar qué sí puede afirmarse;
- indicar qué no.

---

## 25. No-Action Flow

Si movement está dentro de expected variability:

- do not invent issue;
- monitor;
- state why no intervention is justified.

---

## 26. Full Phase 6 Flow

`Raw Data`
→ `Validate Data`
→ `Understand Context`
→ `Select Comparable Cohort`
→ `Apply Benchmark`
→ `Evaluate Guardrails`
→ `Analyze Trend`
→ `Detect Patterns`
→ `Assess Materiality`
→ `Assess Confidence`
→ `Communicate Judgment`

---

## 27. Continuous Updating

Nueva evidence debe actualizar:

- hypothesis ranking;
- cause confidence;
- recommendation confidence.

Nunca defender la primera hypothesis por inercia.
