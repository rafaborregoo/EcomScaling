# FASE 5.08 — Flujo runtime de diagnóstico, capacidad, incidentes y Service Recovery

## Alcance

Este documento traduce a secuencia operacional el razonamiento del corpus 5.08.

No define implementación técnica.

## 1. Entrada

Puede activarse ante:

- delayed deliveries;
- unpredictable delivery;
- backlog;
- high order error rate;
- damage rate;
- WISMO;
- support saturation;
- return cost;
- declining CX;
- capacity saturation;
- carrier/system incident;
- new market with poor CX.

## 2. Paso 1 — Validar señal

Comprobar:

- order volume;
- timestamps;
- SLA definitions;
- contact reasons;
- carrier data;
- return reasons;
- affected geography;
- affected SKU.

No intervenir sobre datos ambiguos como si fueran causa confirmada.

## 3. Paso 2 — Descomponer el proceso

Ante delivery issue separar:

- processing;
- picking;
- packing;
- carrier pickup;
- transit;
- last-mile.

No culpar al carrier por defecto.

## 4. Paso 3 — Localizar bottleneck

Analizar:

- capacity;
- utilization;
- backlog;
- process stage;
- error concentration;
- staffing;
- systems;
- carrier pickups.

Aplicar lógica de constraint.

## 5. Paso 4 — Clasificar problema

Determinar si es:

- temporary peak;
- structural capacity issue;
- process failure;
- quality failure;
- carrier issue;
- information problem;
- product problem;
- support demand issue.

## 6. Paso 5 — Contener deterioro

Cuando sea necesario:

- reduce demand;
- adjust promise;
- prioritize orders;
- activate alternative;
- communicate impact;
- stop affected process/product.

## 7. Paso 6 — Atacar root cause

Ejemplos:

### WISMO
Tracking, delays, promise accuracy, updates.

### Order errors
Process stage, SKU/operator/location pattern.

### Damages
Packaging, carrier, route, handling.

### Support overload
Contact reason, recontact, self-service quality.

### Returns
Product, process, reverse logistics, restocking.

## 8. Paso 7 — Diseñar intervención

Posibles familias:

- process redesign;
- capacity increase;
- flexible staffing;
- carrier mix;
- promise adjustment;
- packaging change;
- SOP;
- automation;
- self-service;
- service recovery;
- 3PL/hybrid;
- demand throttling.

## 9. Paso 8 — Definir validación

Según intervención:

- processing time;
- order cycle time;
- on-time delivery;
- perfect order;
- order accuracy;
- backlog;
- damage rate;
- tickets/order;
- WISMO;
- resolution time;
- returns;
- cost/order;
- repeat.

## 10. Paso 9 — Aplicar guardrails

No mejorar coste destruyendo:

- SLA;
- quality;
- satisfaction;
- retention.

No mejorar speed destruyendo:

- margin;
- accuracy.

## 11. Paso 10 — Aprender

Documentar:

- incident;
- root cause;
- intervention;
- outcome;
- recurrence;
- customer impact;
- economic impact.

## 12. Incident Management

Secuencia conceptual:

**detect  
→ contain  
→ communicate  
→ resolve  
→ root-cause review  
→ prevent recurrence**

## 13. Service Recovery

Cuando el fallo afecta al cliente:

**recognize  
→ explain when appropriate  
→ resolve  
→ compensate proportionally  
→ learn**

## 14. Growth Breaks Operations

Señales:

- backlog ↑;
- SLA ↓;
- errors ↑;
- WISMO ↑;
- refunds ↑.

Secuencia:

1. stop deterioration;
2. limit additional demand if necessary;
3. locate constraint;
4. recover SLA;
5. raise capacity;
6. re-scale progressively.

## 15. Capacity Decision

Ante saturation:

1. ¿temporary or structural?;
2. temporary → flexible capacity;
3. structural → permanent capacity;
4. después localizar bottleneck.

No invertir indiscriminadamente.

## 16. Carrier/System Failure

Prioridades:

1. protect orders;
2. identify scope;
3. activate alternative;
4. communicate real impact;
5. resolve backlog;
6. post-mortem.

## 17. New Market with Poor CX

Investigar:

- carrier;
- customs;
- promise;
- support;
- returns.

No aumentar marketing hasta estabilizar experiencia.

## 18. Salidas conceptuales

El runtime puede concluir:

- internal processing bottleneck;
- carrier issue;
- last-mile issue;
- capacity constraint;
- quality issue;
- packaging issue;
- support demand issue;
- return process issue;
- data/observability issue;
- temporary peak;
- structural capacity need;
- 3PL evaluation needed;
- demand throttling required;
- customer recovery required;
- operations stable enough to re-scale.

No se definen códigos técnicos de salida.
