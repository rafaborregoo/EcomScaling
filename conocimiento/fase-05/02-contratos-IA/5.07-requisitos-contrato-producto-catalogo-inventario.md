# FASE 5.07 — Requisitos conceptuales del contrato de producto, catálogo e inventario

## Estado

Este artefacto define requisitos semánticos derivados del corpus 5.07.

No define schemas técnicos, APIs, tablas ni modelos de datos obligatorios.

## 1. Identidad de producto y SKU

Debe poder distinguir:

- producto;
- SKU;
- variante;
- componente de bundle;
- sustituto;
- producto padre cuando proceda.

## 2. Rol estratégico

Debe poder representar, cuando exista evidencia:

- acquisition product;
- hero product;
- margin product;
- retention product;
- cross-sell product;
- premium product;
- traffic product;
- experimental product.

## 3. Product Scorecard

Puede ser necesario comunicar:

- Revenue;
- Contribution;
- growth;
- stock;
- turnover;
- returns;
- repeat;
- strategic role.

## 4. Inventario

Debe poder representar:

- on-hand stock;
- available-to-promise;
- reserved;
- damaged;
- incoming;
- stockout;
- overstock;
- dead stock;
- inventory age.

## 5. Demand and Forecast

Puede ser necesario intercambiar:

- baseline demand;
- observed demand;
- stockout-censored demand;
- forecast;
- forecast error;
- forecast bias;
- seasonality;
- launch spike;
- steady-state demand.

## 6. Replenishment

Debe poder representar:

- sales velocity;
- lead time;
- lead-time variability;
- safety stock;
- reorder point;
- replenishment priority;
- supplier reliability.

## 7. Sell-Through e Inventory Efficiency

Puede ser necesario comunicar:

- sell-through;
- inventory turnover;
- days of inventory;
- full-price sell-through;
- GMROI;
- inventory return on capital.

## 8. Purchasing and Supplier

Puede incluir:

- supplier;
- MOQ;
- payment terms;
- unit cost;
- supplier concentration;
- sourcing model;
- supply risk.

## 9. Variants

Debe poder representar:

- size/color/configuration;
- demand curve;
- stock imbalance;
- variant-level stockout;
- variant-level overstock.

## 10. Quality

Puede ser necesario comunicar:

- defect rate;
- returns by cause;
- supplier;
- batch;
- SKU;
- period;
- expectation gap;
- recall/compliance status when relevant.

## 11. Catalog and Assortment

Debe poder representar:

- assortment;
- long tail;
- product line;
- category;
- adjacency;
- assortment expansion;
- rationalization;
- sunset;
- migration.

## 12. Cross-Functional Context

Puede ser necesario intercambiar información con:

- marketing;
- CRM;
- finance;
- operations;
- merchandising.

## 13. Data Quality

Debe poder advertir sobre:

- inventory accuracy;
- SKU mapping;
- location mismatch;
- incoming uncertainty;
- missing reservations;
- inconsistent identifiers.

## 14. No definido por la fuente

No se definen:

- JSON schemas;
- APIs;
- table names;
- ERP model;
- warehouse model;
- exact reorder formulas;
- exact safety-stock formulas;
- forecasting algorithm;
- allocation engine;
- supplier scoring formula;
- RBAC;
- approval workflows;
- automated purchasing rules.

No deben inventarse.
