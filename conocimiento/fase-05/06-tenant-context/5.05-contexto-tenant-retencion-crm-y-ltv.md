# FASE 5.05 — Contexto de tenant para retención, CRM y LTV

## Propósito

Los playbooks de retención dependen fuertemente del contexto real del ecommerce.

No se inventan datos concretos.

## 1. Categoría y recurrencia

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- natural replenishment cycle;
- purchase frequency;
- recurring vs non-recurring product;
- expected repeat window.

## 2. Base de clientes

Puede incluir:

- new customers;
- repeat customers;
- loyal;
- at-risk;
- lapsed;
- reactivated.

## 3. Cohortes

Puede ser relevante disponer de cohortes por:

- first purchase month;
- channel;
- campaign;
- promotion;
- first product;
- geography.

## 4. Economics

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- Revenue/customer;
- contribution/customer;
- Revenue LTV;
- Contribution LTV;
- CAC;
- payback;
- refunds;
- returns;
- support cost.

## 5. Second Purchase

Puede incluir:

- time to second purchase;
- next logical product;
- product affinity;
- replenishment timing.

## 6. Customer Experience

Puede ser relevante conocer:

- delivery quality;
- support;
- post-purchase communication;
- onboarding;
- returns experience;
- time to value.

## 7. CRM capabilities

Puede incluir:

- email;
- SMS;
- push;
- flows;
- campaigns;
- segmentation;
- personalization;
- suppression;
- contact policy.

## 8. Lifecycle state

Puede ser necesario representar:

- prospect;
- first-time buyer;
- repeat;
- loyal;
- at-risk;
- lapsed;
- reactivated.

## 9. Segmentation inputs

Pueden incluir:

- recency;
- frequency;
- monetary value;
- product history;
- category;
- engagement;
- risk;
- preferences.

## 10. Promotions and loyalty

Puede ser relevante conocer:

- loyalty program;
- points;
- VIP tiers;
- memberships;
- subscription;
- discount behavior;
- full-price behavior.

## 11. Subscription context

Puede incluir:

- active churn;
- passive churn;
- skips;
- pauses;
- cancellation reasons;
- payment failures;
- dunning.

## 12. Contact pressure

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- current frequency;
- overlapping flows;
- unsubscribe;
- spam complaints;
- channel usage.

## 13. Data quality

Puede ser relevante conocer:

- duplicate customers;
- identity confidence;
- guest checkout;
- multiple emails;
- missing events;
- segmentation quality.

## 14. Consent and privacy

El contexto puede incluir:

- channel consent;
- personalization permissions;
- available first-party data.

No deben inventarse permisos.

## 15. Madurez

El ecommerce puede encontrarse en:

- inicial;
- tracción;
- crecimiento;
- escala.

La madurez condiciona complejidad de CRM y lifecycle.

## 16. Restricciones que no deben asumirse

No inventar:

- repeat target;
- churn threshold;
- LTV target;
- payback limit;
- lifecycle windows;
- frequency caps;
- customer segments;
- subscription cadence;
- consent;
- contact permissions;
- profitability by customer.
