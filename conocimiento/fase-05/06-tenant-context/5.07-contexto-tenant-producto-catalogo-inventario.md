# FASE 5.07 — Contexto de tenant para producto, catálogo e inventario

## Propósito

Las decisiones de producto e inventario dependen del contexto real del ecommerce.

No se inventan valores concretos.

## 1. Catálogo

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- products;
- SKUs;
- categories;
- variants;
- bundles;
- strategic roles;
- assortment size.

## 2. Product Economics

Puede incluir:

- Revenue;
- Contribution;
- margin;
- returns;
- CAC;
- repeat;
- attach rate.

## 3. Stock Position

Puede ser relevante:

- on-hand;
- available-to-promise;
- reserved;
- damaged;
- incoming;
- stockouts;
- overstock;
- dead stock.

## 4. Inventory Efficiency

Puede incluir:

- inventory age;
- turnover;
- days of inventory;
- sell-through;
- full-price sell-through;
- GMROI;
- inventory return on capital.

## 5. Demand

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- sales velocity;
- baseline demand;
- promotion effects;
- launch spikes;
- seasonality;
- lost-sales periods;
- waitlist signals.

## 6. Forecasting

Puede incluir:

- forecast;
- forecast horizon;
- forecast error;
- bias;
- scenario range;
- confidence limitations.

## 7. Replenishment

Puede ser relevante:

- reorder point;
- safety stock;
- lead time;
- lead-time variability;
- purchase cadence;
- replenishment priority.

## 8. Suppliers

Puede incluir:

- supplier;
- concentration;
- reliability;
- defect history;
- MOQ;
- payment terms;
- capacity;
- sourcing model.

## 9. Variants

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- size/color/configuration curves;
- variant-level availability;
- variant-level turnover;
- imbalance.

## 10. Quality

Puede incluir:

- defect rate;
- returns by cause;
- batch;
- supplier;
- product reviews;
- support complaints;
- recall/compliance issues.

## 11. Cash and Capital

Puede ser relevante:

- inventory investment;
- working capital;
- purchasing budget;
- open-to-buy;
- cash constraints.

## 12. Channels and Locations

Puede incluir:

- ecommerce;
- marketplaces;
- retail;
- warehouses;
- geographic stock allocation.

## 13. Data Quality

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- SKU mapping;
- inventory accuracy;
- reservations;
- incoming dates;
- warehouse discrepancies;
- source systems.

## 14. Product Lifecycle

Puede representar:

- launch;
- growth;
- maturity;
- decline.

## 15. Portfolio Strategy

Puede incluir:

- hero dependency;
- portfolio gaps;
- category adjacency;
- long tail;
- rationalization candidates;
- sunset plans.

## 16. Maturity

El ecommerce puede estar en:

- inicial;
- tracción;
- crecimiento;
- escala.

La madurez condiciona profundidad del sistema de inventory management.

## 17. Restricciones que no deben asumirse

No inventar:

- safety stock;
- reorder thresholds;
- forecast accuracy;
- stockout tolerance;
- MOQ;
- cash availability;
- supplier capacity;
- target inventory turns;
- markdown limits;
- assortment targets;
- supplier risk score.
