# FASE 5.11 — Contexto de tenant para crisis, recovery y continuidad

## Propósito

Las decisiones de crisis dependen del estado real del ecommerce, sus dependencias, recursos y tolerancia al riesgo.

No deben inventarse valores.

## 1. Critical Systems

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- storefront;
- checkout;
- payments;
- inventory;
- fulfillment;
- CRM;
- analytics;
- support;
- ad platforms.

## 2. Sources of Truth

Puede incluir:

- ecommerce orders;
- payment records;
- finance;
- analytics;
- CRM;
- WMS/OMS;
- support systems.

## 3. Current Operating State

Puede ser relevante:

- normal;
- degraded;
- crisis;
- recovery;
- normalization.

## 4. Incident Context

Puede incluir:

- incident type;
- start;
- current status;
- affected systems;
- affected customers;
- blast radius;
- severity;
- urgency.

## 5. Data Integrity

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- broken tracking;
- reliable fallbacks;
- attribution status;
- pipeline status;
- affected period;
- backfill capability.

## 6. Revenue and Funnel

Puede incluir:

- traffic;
- CVR;
- AOV;
- orders;
- payment success;
- checkout status.

## 7. Acquisition

Puede ser relevante:

- spend;
- channel dependence;
- marginal CAC;
- contribution;
- campaign controls;
- ability to pause/reduce/restore.

## 8. Cash and Finance

Puede incluir:

- cash available;
- runway;
- obligations;
- refunds;
- supplier payments;
- inventory commitments;
- financing options.

## 9. Inventory and Supply

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- stock coverage;
- critical SKUs;
- supplier dependency;
- incoming stock;
- alternative suppliers;
- preorders/waitlists.

## 10. Fulfillment

Puede incluir:

- backlog;
- SLA;
- current capacity;
- temporary capacity;
- carrier dependence;
- alternate carrier.

## 11. Support

Puede ser relevante:

- contact rate;
- dominant contact reasons;
- capacity;
- escalation capability;
- proactive communication capability.

## 12. Customer and Reputation

Puede incluir:

- affected customers;
- reviews;
- complaints;
- refunds;
- chargebacks;
- trust impact.

## 13. Product Risk

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- defect issue;
- affected SKU/batch;
- customer safety;
- regulatory/compliance concern;
- distribution status.

## 14. Resilience

Puede incluir:

- fallback payment;
- backup carrier;
- supplier alternatives;
- manual procedures;
- graceful degradation;
- redundancy.

## 15. Ownership

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- incident owner;
- system owners;
- decision rights;
- escalation path;
- communication owner.

## 16. Communication

Puede incluir:

- internal channels;
- external channels;
- affected audience;
- communication requirements;
- legal constraints when known.

## 17. Recovery Baseline

Puede ser necesario definir:

- technical recovery;
- backlog target;
- SLA state;
- contribution state;
- data confidence;
- customer state.

No se inventan thresholds.

## 18. Validation and Rescale

Puede incluir:

- validation period;
- available headroom;
- rescale capability;
- rollback capability;
- monitored guardrails.

## 19. Crisis Debt

Puede ser relevante conocer:

- temporary workarounds;
- technical debt;
- process debt;
- promises made;
- normalization plan.

## 20. Historical Incidents

Puede incluir:

- incident frequency;
- repeated causes;
- previous mitigations;
- prior failures of playbooks.

## 21. Maturity

Un ecommerce más maduro puede disponer de:

- runbooks;
- command center;
- incident owner;
- monitoring;
- fallbacks;
- tabletop exercises;
- post-mortems.

No debe asumirse que existen.

## 22. Restricciones que no deben asumirse

No inventar:

- severity threshold;
- crisis mode threshold;
- cash runway;
- rollback permission;
- legal communication duties;
- incident owner;
- backup systems;
- acceptable residual risk;
- recovery baseline;
- validation window.
