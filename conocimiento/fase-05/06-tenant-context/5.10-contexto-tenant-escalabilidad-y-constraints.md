# FASE 5.10 — Contexto de tenant para escalabilidad y constraints

## Propósito

Las recomendaciones de escalado dependen completamente del estado real del ecommerce.

No se inventan valores.

## 1. Demand

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- current demand;
- TAM/market constraints;
- channel saturation;
- demand headroom;
- growth engine mix.

## 2. Acquisition

Puede incluir:

- current spend;
- average CAC;
- marginal CAC;
- channel headroom;
- creative throughput;
- saturation signals.

## 3. Conversion

Puede ser relevante:

- funnel performance;
- current bottleneck;
- capacity unlocked by CRO;
- post-CRO next constraint.

## 4. Economics

Puede incluir:

- Contribution;
- margin;
- LTV;
- payback;
- marginal economics;
- break-even constraints.

## 5. Inventory

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- stock coverage;
- replenishment;
- inventory headroom;
- stockout risk;
- working capital impact.

## 6. Suppliers

Puede incluir:

- capacity;
- lead time;
- concentration;
- second-source options;
- flexibility;
- contractual constraints.

## 7. Fulfillment

Puede ser relevante:

- throughput;
- backlog;
- SLA;
- capacity;
- utilization;
- capacity cliffs;
- 3PL/internal model.

## 8. Customer Support

Puede incluir:

- contact rate;
- capacity;
- avoidable support demand;
- response/resolution performance.

## 9. Cash and Finance

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- available cash;
- financial headroom;
- financing;
- supplier terms;
- burn;
- payback;
- inventory financing needs.

## 10. Organization

Puede incluir:

- founder dependency;
- ownership;
- decision rights;
- approval layers;
- handoffs;
- process latency;
- management capacity.

## 11. Talent

Puede ser relevante:

- missing capabilities;
- current team load;
- utilization;
- specialization/generalist mix.

## 12. Technology

Puede incluir:

- performance limits;
- manual work;
- automation;
- system errors;
- technical debt;
- integration complexity.

## 13. Complexity

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- geographies;
- channels;
- SKUs;
- systems;
- exceptions;
- coordination overhead.

## 14. Resilience

Puede incluir:

- single points of failure;
- supplier concentration;
- platform dependence;
- carrier dependence;
- redundancy;
- fallback options.

## 15. Data and Observability

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- dashboards;
- alerts;
- metric definitions;
- audit trail;
- detection latency;
- action latency.

## 16. Growth Engines

Puede incluir:

- current primary engine;
- saturation state;
- adjacent engine hypotheses;
- exploration capacity;
- validated alternatives.

## 17. Scale Readiness

Puede requerir contexto real sobre:

- demand;
- economics;
- inventory;
- operations;
- cash;
- organization;
- data.

## 18. Maturity

El ecommerce puede encontrarse en:

- initial;
- traction;
- growth;
- scale.

La madurez condiciona el tipo de constraint y la profundidad de governance necesaria.

## 19. Restrictions that must not be assumed

No inventar:

- headroom;
- marginal CAC;
- capacity;
- buffer;
- cash availability;
- supplier capacity;
- readiness;
- risk tolerance;
- decision thresholds;
- founder dependency;
- technology limits;
- demand ceiling.
