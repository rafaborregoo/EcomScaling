# FASE 5.09 — Contexto de tenant para datos, tracking y experimentación

## Propósito

Las recomendaciones sobre datos y experimentación dependen de la instrumentación, fuentes y madurez reales del ecommerce.

No deben inventarse valores.

## 1. Sources

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- ecommerce platform;
- analytics;
- advertising platforms;
- CRM;
- finance;
- payment systems;
- data warehouse;
- support/product sources.

## 2. Tracking Architecture

Puede incluir:

- client-side;
- server-side;
- hybrid;
- event_id;
- deduplication;
- idempotency;
- sessionization.

## 3. Event Taxonomy

Puede ser relevante conocer:

- event names;
- meanings;
- triggers;
- properties;
- sources;
- identifiers.

## 4. Metric Dictionary

Puede incluir definiciones de:

- Revenue;
- Net Revenue;
- New Customer;
- CAC;
- CVR;
- AOV;
- LTV;
- repeat;
- Contribution.

## 5. Time Semantics

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- timezone;
- created/paid/fulfilled dates;
- reporting period;
- refund timing;
- late-arriving data.

## 6. Currency and Tax

Puede incluir:

- transaction currency;
- reporting currency;
- FX treatment;
- VAT/tax inclusion.

## 7. Data Quality

Puede ser relevante:

- completeness;
- accuracy;
- consistency;
- timeliness;
- uniqueness;
- validity;
- reconciliation gaps;
- missingness.

## 8. Identity

Puede incluir:

- customer_id;
- guest checkout;
- normalized email;
- duplicate customers;
- anonymous-to-known linking;
- identity confidence.

## 9. Consent and Privacy

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- consent availability;
- channel tracking restrictions;
- PII usage;
- personalization permissions;
- data access constraints.

No se inventan permisos.

## 10. Attribution

Puede incluir:

- platform attribution;
- last click;
- first click;
- multi-touch;
- data-driven model;
- blended metrics.

## 11. Experimentation Capability

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- traffic volume;
- experiment tooling;
- randomization capability;
- geo structure;
- holdout capability;
- experiment registry;
- rollout/rollback capability.

## 12. Existing Experiments

Puede incluir:

- hypothesis;
- status;
- metrics;
- result;
- learning;
- experiment ID.

## 13. Dashboard Context

Puede ser relevante:

- dashboards;
- refresh cadence;
- metric hierarchy;
- alerts;
- source lineage.

## 14. Pipeline Context

Puede incluir:

- ingestion;
- transformation;
- storage;
- model;
- dashboard;
- observability;
- schema changes;
- backfill capability.

## 15. Data Maturity

Puede encontrarse en:

### Inicial
Basic reliable tracking y transactional source.

### Tracción
Cohorts, customer identity, product economics, experiment log.

### Crecimiento
Server-side, reconciliation, triangulation, automated quality checks.

### Escala
Incrementality, advanced experimentation, lineage, observability, causal measurement.

## 16. Decision Context

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- business question;
- decision size;
- reversibility;
- expected impact;
- cost of waiting;
- value of information.

## 17. Restrictions that must not be assumed

No inventar:

- source completeness;
- consent;
- event coverage;
- identity accuracy;
- experiment power;
- MDE;
- confidence;
- statistical significance;
- attribution model quality;
- data freshness;
- lineage completeness;
- RBAC.
