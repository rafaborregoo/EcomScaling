# FASE 5.06 — Contexto de tenant para Unit Economics y rentabilidad

## Propósito

Las conclusiones económicas dependen de datos, definiciones y restricciones reales del ecommerce.

No deben inventarse valores.

## 1. Revenue

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- Gross Sales;
- Net Revenue;
- discounts;
- refunds;
- returns;
- cancellations.

## 2. Product Costs

Puede incluir:

- COGS;
- factory cost;
- landed cost;
- packaging;
- freight;
- duties;
- insurance;
- import costs.

## 3. Variable Costs

Puede ser relevante:

- payment fees;
- fulfillment;
- shipping;
- shipping subsidy;
- returns cost;
- variable support;
- fraud/chargebacks.

## 4. Acquisition

Puede incluir:

- total acquisition spend;
- paid spend;
- new customers;
- blended CAC;
- paid CAC;
- fully loaded CAC;
- average CAC;
- marginal CAC.

## 5. Margin and Contribution

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- Gross Profit;
- Gross Margin;
- Contribution Pre-Marketing;
- Contribution Post-Marketing;
- Contribution/Order;
- Contribution/Customer;
- Contribution/Session.

## 6. LTV and Payback

Puede incluir:

- Revenue LTV;
- Contribution LTV;
- cumulative contribution;
- payback;
- cohort maturity;
- realized vs projected value.

## 7. Product Economics

Puede ser relevante:

- SKU revenue;
- SKU margin;
- returns;
- shipping;
- attach rate;
- repeat;
- strategic role.

## 8. Channel Economics

Puede incluir:

- CAC;

## 9. Geography Economics

Puede incluir:

## 12. Fixed and Semi-Variable Costs

- Contribution/Order;

## 15. Scenarios and Assumptions

Puede incluir:

- base;
- downside;
- upside;
- demand assumptions;
- CAC assumptions;
- return assumptions;
- margin assumptions.

## 16. Data Quality

Puede ser necesario conocer:

- source of data;
- accounting definitions;
- gross/net treatment;
- time windows;
- taxes;
- attribution;
- missing data.

## 17. Maturity

El ecommerce puede encontrarse en:

- inicial;
- tracción;
- crecimiento;
- escala.

La madurez condiciona profundidad necesaria del modelo económico.

## 18. Restricciones que no deben asumirse

No inventar:

- margin targets;
- break-even CAC;
- target CAC;
- payback limit;
- LTV;
- working capital capacity;
- fixed costs;
- stock position;
- financing availability;
- downside tolerance;
- margin of safety.
