# Fase 03 · D11 — Tenant context para confianza e incertidumbre

## Propósito

Contexto específico del ecommerce necesario para evaluar coverage, reliability, applicability, freshness y evidence burden.

No contiene valores concretos de ningún tenant.

---

## 1. Source Inventory

Puede incluir fuentes disponibles:
- Shopify;
- Meta;
- Google Ads;
- GA;
- payment provider;
- ERP;
- warehouse;
- CRM;
- support;
- reviews;
- finance systems.

---

## 2. Source Reliability

D1 puede aportar, por tenant:
- canonical source;
- known conflicts;
- staleness;
- reconciliation status;
- lineage.

---

## 3. Coverage Map

Debe conocerse qué parte del negocio está cubierta:
- channels;
- markets;
- devices;
- products;
- customer types;
- operational units.

---

## 4. Context Completeness

D2 puede aportar:
- promotions;
- price changes;
- launches;
- seasonality;
- market shifts;
- strategy changes;
- measurement changes.

---

## 5. Sample / Volume

El sample suficiente depende de:
- orders;
- sessions;
- transactions;
- cohorts;
- market size;
- segment size.

---

## 6. Domain Horizons

Puede existir configuración por fenómeno:
- payment outage: minutes/hours;
- ads: hours/days;
- finance: days/weeks;
- retention: weeks/months.

---

## 7. Business Complexity

Coverage requirement puede aumentar con:
- markets;
- channels;
- catalogs;
- warehouses;
- payment methods;
- business models.

---

## 8. Claim Scope Hierarchy

Puede incluir:
- global;
- market;
- channel;
- campaign;
- creative;
- device;
- browser;
- product;
- SKU;
- cohort;
- payment method;
- warehouse.

---

## 9. Historical Patterns

Repeated tenant patterns pueden aportar prior support.

El historial completo pertenece a experience-learning.

---

## 10. Source Authority / Validity

Para documents/knowledge:
- authority;
- validity;
- version;
- last update;
- tenant applicability.

---

## 11. Measurement Architecture

Necesaria para distinguir:
- real-world variable;
- measured variable;
- observed data;
- tracking artifacts.

---

## 12. Known Data Gaps

Puede incluir:
- missing COGS;
- missing frequency;
- unavailable logs;
- incomplete attribution;
- partial market coverage.

---

## 13. Critical Evidence Availability

Debe conocerse qué evidence puede obtenerse:
- automatically;
- via connector;
- by user question;
- only over time;
- not at all.

---

## 14. Cost-of-error Context

Puede variar según:
- cash survival;
- inventory commitments;
- outages;
- strategic decisions;
- low-risk analytical questions.

---

## 15. Business Stage / Maturity

Si stage/maturity itself is uncertain, stage-relative claims deben degradarse.

---

## 16. Current Context Regime

Historical conclusions pueden perder applicability tras:
- pricing changes;
- market expansion;
- strategy changes;
- channel shifts;
- measurement changes.

---

## 17. Exclusiones

No pertenecen a tenant-context:
- universal confidence principles;
- current confidence matrix;
- current unknown list;
- full confidence history;
- global evidence hierarchy.
